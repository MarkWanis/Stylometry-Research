Dauid wæs haten diormod hæleð, Israela brëga, æðelæ and rice, cyninga cynost, Criste liofost.
Wæs he under hiofenum hearpera mærost ðara we an folcum gefrigen hæbben.
Sangere he wæs soðfæstest, swiðe geðancol to ðingienne þiodum sinum wið þane mildostan manna
Wæs se dryhtnes ðiowa Dauid æt wige soð sigecempa, searocyne man, casere creaftig, þonne
Hwëðere him geiode, swa ful oft gedeð þætte godferhte gylt gefræmmað þurh lichaman lene geðohtas.
Gelamp þæt him mon ansende saula neriend, witgan mid wordum, weorada dominus, and secgan het,
Him ða ðingode þioda aldor, Dauid georne, and to dryhtne gebæd, and his synna hord selfa ontende,
Ond eac efter menio miltsa ðinra, dryhten weoruda, adilga min unriht to forgefenesse gaste minum.
Aðweah me of sennum, saule fram wammum, gasta sceppend, geltas geclansa, þa ðe ic on aldre æfre
Forðan ic unriht min eal oncnawe, and eac synna gehwær selfum æt eagan, firendeda geðrec beforan
Nu ic anum ðe oft syngode, and yfela feola eac gefræmede, gelta gramhegdig, ic ðe, gasta breogo,
Ic on unrihtum eac ðan in synnum geeacnod wæs.
Ðu ðæt ana wast, mæhtig dryhten, hu me modor gebær in scame and in sceldum; forgef me, sceppend
Ac ðu, selua god, soð an lufast; þy ic ðe mid benum biddan wille lifes and lisse, liohtes aldor, forðan ðu
Ðu me, meahtig god, milde and bliðe þurh ysopon ealne ahluttra, þonne ic geclænsod Criste hero, and
Ontyn nu, elmehtig, earna hleoðor, þæt min gehernes hehtful weorðe on gefean bliðse forðweard to ðe;
Ahwerf nu fram synnum, saula neriend, and fram misdedum minra gylta þine ansione, ælmeahtig god,
Æc ðu, dryhten Crist, clëne hiortan in me, mehtig god, modswiðne geðanc to ðolienne ðinne willan and
Ne aweorp ðu me, weoruda dryhten, fram ansione ealra þinra miltsa, ne ðane godan fram me gast
Sæle nu bliðse me, bilewit dominus, þinre hælo heht, helm alwihta, and me, lifgende liohtes hiorde,
Simle ic ðine weogas wanhogan lærde, ðæt hio arlease eft gecerdan to hiora selfra saula hiorde, god
Befreo me an ferðe, fæder mancynnes, fram blodgete and bealaniðum, god lifigende, gylta geclansa,
Ontyn nu, waldend god, weoloras mine; swa min muð sioððan mæhte ðine and lof georne liodum to
Ic ðe onsegednesse sona brohte, weoruda dryhtne, ðer ðu wolde swa, ða ðu þæt ne lufedest, lifes
Ac ðe micle ma, mehtig dryhten, lifiende Crist, liicwerðe bið se gehnysta gast, hiorte geclansod and
Gedoo nu fræmsume frofre ðine to ðinum godan gastes willan, ðætte Sione dun sigefest weorðe, and
Swa þu, frea meahtig, anfehst siþðan liofwende lac lioda þinra, hælend manna; hio ðæt halige cealf on
Forgef me, lifigende meotod mancynnes, mæhtig dominus, ðæt ða sorhfullan saule wunde, þa ðe ic on
Swæ þingode þiode aldor, Dauid to dryhtne, deda gemyndig, þæt hine mëhtig god mannum to frofre
