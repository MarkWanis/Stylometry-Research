Ic on wonge aweox, wunode þær mec feddon hruse ond heofonwolcn, oþþæt me onhwyrfdon gearum
Nu eom mines frean folme bysigo dlan dæl, gif his ellen deag, oþþe æfter dome ri an mæ þa fremman,
Cuð is wide þæt ic þristra sum þeofes cræfte under hrægnlocan hwilum eawunga eþelfæsten forðweard
Feringe from, he fus þonan wendeð of þam wicum.
Wiga se þe mine wisan cunne, saga hwæt ic hatte.
