Ne forstolen ne forholen nanuht, þæs ðe ic age, þe ma ðe mihte Herod urne drihten.
Ic geþohte sancte Eadelenan and ic geþohte Crist on rode ahangen; swa ic þence þis feoh to findanne,
Garmund, godes ðegen, find þæt feoh and fere þæt feoh and hafa þæt feoh and heald þæt feoh and fere
Þæt he næfre næbbe landes, þæt he hit oðlæde, ne foldan, þæt hit oðferie, ne husa, þæt he hit oðhealde.
Gif hyt hwa gedo, ne gedige hit him næfre.
Binnan þrym nihtum cunne ic his mihta, his mægen and his mihta and his mundcræftas.
Eall he weornige, swa syre wudu weornie, swa breðel seo swa þystel, se ðe ðis feoh oðfergean þence
