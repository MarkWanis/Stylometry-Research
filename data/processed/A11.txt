Is ðeos burch breome geond Breotenrice, steppa gestaðolad, stanas ymbutan wundrum gewæxen.
Weor ymbeornad, ea yðum stronge, and ðer inne wunað feola fisca kyn on floda gemonge.
And ðær gewexen is wudafæstern micel: wuniad in ðem wycum wilda deor monige, in deope dalum
Is in ðere byri eac bearnum gecyðed ðe arfesta eadig Cudberch and ðes clene cyninges heafud,
Is ðer inne midd heom Æðelwold biscop and breoma bocera Beda, and Boisil abbot, ðe clene Cudberte
Eardiæð æt ðem eadige in in ðem minstre unarimeda reliquia, ðær monia wundrum gewurðað, ðes ðe
