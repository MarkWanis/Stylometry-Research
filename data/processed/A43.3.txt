Wið dweorh man sceal niman VII lytle oflætan, swylce man mid ofrað, and writan þas naman on ælcre
Þænne eft þæt galdor, þæt her æfter cweð, man sceal singan, ærest on þæt wynstre eare, þænne on þæt
And ga þænne an mædenman to and ho hit on his sweoran, and do man swa þry dagas; him bið sona sel.
Her com in gangan, in spiderwiht, hæfde him his haman on handa, cwæð þæt þu his hæncgest wære,
Ongunnan him of þæm lande liþan; sona swa hy of þæm lande coman, þa ongunnan him ða liþu colian.
Þa com in gangan dweores sweostar; þa geændade heo and aðas swor ðæt næfre þis ðæm adlegan
