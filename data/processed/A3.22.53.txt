Ic seah on bearwe beam hlifian, tanum torhtne.
Þæt treow wæs on wynne, wudu weaxende.
Wæter hine ond eorþe feddan fægre, oþþæt he frod dagum on oþrum wearð aglachade deope gedolgod,
Nu he fæcnum weg þurh his heafdes mægen hildegieste oþrum rymeð.
