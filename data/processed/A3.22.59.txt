Ic seah in healle hring gyldenne men sceawian, modum gleawe, ferþþum frode.
Friþospede bæd god nergende gæste sinum se þe wende wriþan; word æfter cwæð hring on hyrede,
Him torhte in gemynd his dryhtnes naman dumba brohte ond in eagna gesihð, gif þæs æþelan goldes
Ne mæg þære bene æniges monnes ungefullodre godes ealdorburg gæst gesecan, rodera ceastre.
Ræde, se þe wille, hu ðæs wrætlican wunda cwæden hringes to hæleþum, þa he in healle wæs wylted
