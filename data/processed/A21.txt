Sy þe wuldor and lof wide geopenod geond ealle þeoda, þanc and wylla, mægen and mildse and ealles
Wealdest eall on riht.
Þu eart frofra fæder and feorhhyrde, lifes latteow, leohtes wealdend, asyndrod fram synnum, swa ðin
Swa wæs on fruman frea mancynnes ealre worulde wlite and frofer, clæne and cræftig.
Þu gecyddest þæt þa ðu, ece god, ana gewrohtest þurh halige miht heofonas and eorðan, eardas and
Þu settest on foldan swyðe feala cynna and tosyndrodost hig syððon on mænego; þu gewrohtest, ece
Þa wæs geforðad þin fægere weorc, and ðu sunnandæg sylf halgodest and gemærsodest hine manegum
Þone heahan dæg healdað and freoðiaþ ealle þa ðe cunnon cristene þeawas, halige heortlufan and ðæs
And nu and symble þine soðan weorc and ðin mycele miht manegum swytelað, swa þine cræftas heo
Ealle þe heriað halige dreamas clænre stefne and cristene bec, eall middaneard, and we men cweþað on
And on worulda woruld wunað and rixað cyning innan wuldre and his þa gecorenan, heahþrymnesse
Þær is seo mæste lufu; haligdomes heofonas syndon þurh þine ecan word æghwær fulle, swa syndon
We þæt soðlice secgað ealle: þurh clæne gecynd þu eart cyning on riht, clæne and cræftig.
Þu gecyddest þæt þa ðu, mihtig god, man geworhtest and him on dydest oruð and sawul, sealdest word
Swylc is Cristes miht.
