Ic seah turf tredan, X wæron ealra, VI gebroþor ond hyra sweostor mid; hæfdon feorg cwico .
Fell hongedon sweotol ond gesyne on seles wæge anra gehwylces.
Ne wæs hyra ængum þy wyrs , ne siðe þy sarre, þeah hy swa sceoldon reafe birofene, rodra weardes
Hrægl bið geniwad þam þe ær forðcymene frætwe leton licgan on laste, gewitan lond tredan.
