Eadward kyng gret Aylmer biscop & Girth earl & Toly and alle mine þeynes on Estangle frendlike.
& ic kithe ihu þat ic habbe unnen Baldewine abbot þe abbotriche into Seint Eadmundes biri & alkere
Nu bidde ic alle þe manne þe ben Godes frend and min þat him filste to lage & to rithte loc wer him ned
And ic nelle nane men þafien þat him fro hande teo ani þing þat ic him gifen habbe oþer get þinke to
