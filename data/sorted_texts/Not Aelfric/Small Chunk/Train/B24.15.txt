Hu micel goldes wæs Salomone broht æghwylce dæge.
Þæt wæs feower ðusenda & syx & syxtig talentana.
On ælcre talentan wæs LXXX punda.
Swa micel wæs gebroht on ðam temple, butan þam þe him fram cypemannum com, & fram cyningum, &
On salomones tide wæs gold swa genihtsum, & seolfor on hierusalem swa stanas ofer eorðan.
Hu nyt bið þæt ðeah þu ðe ealne middaneard & ealle eorðan wille gestrynan, gif þu þinre sawle unfreme
