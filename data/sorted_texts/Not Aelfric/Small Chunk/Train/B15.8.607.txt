Ærest on wylfing ford on Temese.
Þ of wylfinga ford andlang anre smale dic to niwan hæminga londgemære on ða heafdo.
Þ forð be ðon heafodon on fippel beorgas.
Of fippel beorgon on Culanhema dic, andlang dic on æppelford.
Of æppelforda andlang þære richt temese onbutan utan ege þæt eft on wylfing ford.
