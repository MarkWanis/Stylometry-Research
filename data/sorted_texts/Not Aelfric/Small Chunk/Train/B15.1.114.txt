Edward king gret Harold erl & Aylnod abbot & Godwine & ealle mine þeynes on Sumerseten frendliche.
Ich queþe eou þæt ich wille þæt Gyse bissop beo þisses bissopriches wrthe heer inne mid eou and alch
And ich bidde eou alle þat ge him beon on fultome Cristendom to spekene loc whar hit þarf sy & eower
And gif what sy mid unlage out of þan bissopriche geydon sy hit on londe oþer an oððer þinge þat
God eu ealle gehealde.
