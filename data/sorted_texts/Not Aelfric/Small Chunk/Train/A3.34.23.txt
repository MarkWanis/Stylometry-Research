Frod wæs min fromcynn biden in burgum, siþþan bæles weard wera life bewunden, fyre gefælsad.
Nu me fah warað eorþan broþor, se me ærest wearð gumena to gyrne.
Ic ful gearwe gemon hwa min fromcynn fruman agette eall of earde; ic him yfle ne mot, ac ic hæftnyd
Hæbbe ic wundra fela, middangeardes mægen unlytel, ac ic miþan sceal monna gehwylcum degolfulne
Saga hwæt ic hatte.
