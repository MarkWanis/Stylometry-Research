To mæsseprostum.
Sacerd scel on his scrifscire wislice and wærlice lædan and læran þa godcunde heorde, þe he heald scel.
Æiþer he sceal ge wel bodian ge wel bysnian oþrum mannum, and æiþer he scel æt godes dome gescead
Þy he na mæig na wandian, gif he riht deþ, naþor ne for heanum ne for ricum, þæt he mannum þæt
Þeh ura hyrda hwylc an scep foriyme, we wyllaþ, þæt he hit forgilde; hwæt gefarað þonne æt godes
Mid hwam forgildaþ hi hig þonne, læt we nu?
Wa heom þonne, þæt hi æfre underfengon þa godcunde heorde, þe hi gehealdan sceoldan.
Hu mæig blind man oþerne lædan, oððe hu mæig ungelæred mann oþerne læran?
