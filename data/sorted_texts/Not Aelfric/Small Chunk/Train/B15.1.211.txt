H þurh godes geuæ Ængelandes king grete ealle mine bissopes & ealle mine eorles & ealle mine
And ic keðe eow þæt icc hæbbe geunne ðan ercebiscope & ta muneken æt Christescircean on Cantorebiri
And sake & socne on strande & on streame on wude & on felde tolnes & teames grihbreces & hamsocne
And ic nelle þæt ani man æni þing þar on tyo buten hi hære wicnres þa hi hit bætecen willeð ne
God eow gæhealde.
