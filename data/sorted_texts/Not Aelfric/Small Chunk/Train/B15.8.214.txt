Þis synt þa land gemæro to wicham.
Ærest se burna on easthealfe on suþhealfe sanctæ Agustines gemæro & eft sanctæ Agustines land on
Swa forþ andlang broces oþ hose graf & þær west be mearc fleote to sture & swa east be sture oþ burnan
& þæt den on blean earnes hyrst on easthealfe þening den & seo burh stræt on suþhealfe Ælfrices mearc
Cluse & haming ford on norðhealfe þæs hiredes mearc to Sancte Mildryþe swa forð oþ langan leagas
