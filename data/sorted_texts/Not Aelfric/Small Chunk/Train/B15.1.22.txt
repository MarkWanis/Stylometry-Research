In nomine domini. Eadward cyning & þa hiwan in Wintanceastre lætað to Ðænewulfe bisceope twentig
On þa gerad þe man gemynd æfter his dege to þære halgan stowe do in Wintanceastre swa swa he þænne
Ælce geare to þære edmeltide þæt mon geselle twelf seoxtres beoras & twelf geswettes wilisc ealoð &
Gyf hit on lencten gebyrige þæt þæ þonne þære flæscun geweorð on fisce gestriene buton þæt þis
Gif hit þænne sie se ðe þæt land hæbbe þæt he þis his geagenes þances tuwa forgymeleasie buton hit
& min gemund þær on þam gerihtum þæ þarto bælimpað arerad be þam ilcan þe hit herbufan gecweden
Þonne bebeodad hie on Godes ælmihtiges naman & on sanctes Petres & on sanctes Paules þæ hit hiera
& his gewrit was awriten on Wintanceastre on þara witena gewitnesse þe hiera naman hær bæniþan
