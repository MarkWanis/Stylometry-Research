Eadward kingc gret Ealdred ercebiscop & Wulstan biscop & Wulfwi biscop & ealle mine heafedmen &
Icc kiþe eow þat icc wille & icc ann þæt Sancte Peter & þa gebroðera on Westminstre habben to heora
And ic anne hem eft alswa þæt hy habben þar to saka & soknæ, toll & team, infangenne þeof &
For þæn icc nelle nateshwan geþafian þæt anig man ætbrede oððe geutige mine gife & mine ælmesse
Ne eft þæt þær ænig man aenigne onstingc habbe on ænigum þingan oððe on ænige timan be strande ne
& ic wille & fæstlice bebeode þæt þæs freodom & þeos mundbirdnesse beo strang & staþelfast into
God eow ealle gehealde.
