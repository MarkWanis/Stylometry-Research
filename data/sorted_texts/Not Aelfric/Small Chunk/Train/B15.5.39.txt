Þis syndan þa gerichte þe liccaþ into þam minstre on Lambourne.
Þæt is an hyde landes sker & sacleas sake & soken tol & team & þanne teoþeu æker on þæs kynges lande
& of ælcere hide geneatlandes on vp hæme toune and on byrihæme tune oenne æker to teoþunge odðe
Se þe þis benime þan minstre æt Lambourne & þan preoste Cristes cours habbe he & Sancta Maria &
Þis is bitealð be þæs kinges hase innan þare scire & innan þan hundred on Lambourne.
Þars to is gewitnesse.
