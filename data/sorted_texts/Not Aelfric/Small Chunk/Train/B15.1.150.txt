Eadward king gret Wulsi biscop & Guirð eorll & alle mine þeingnes on Oxenfordescire freondlice.
& ic kiþe eow þat ic habbe gegefen Criste & Sancte Petre into Westmunstre ðet cotlif ic wæs geboran
& ic an heom þer ofer saca & socna, toll & team & infangeneþeof & blodwite & weardwite & hamsocne
Nu grete ic wel mine leofne mæi Wigod on Wallingeforde.
& ic beode ðe þat ðu on minre stede beride þas land þam hælge to hande for ic nelle nateshwon
& hwa se ðas ælmesse holdlice healde healde hine God & Godes moder on ece blisse.
& hwa swa hio awende sieo he awænded fram Gode to þare hellware stiþe pinnesse buton he hit on
God eow ealle gehealde & Sancte Petres holde.
