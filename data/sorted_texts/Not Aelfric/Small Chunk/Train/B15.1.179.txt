Willelm kyng gret mine eorlas and ealle mine þegnas on þan sciran þær Ealdred arcebiscop hæfþ land
And ic kyþe eow þæt ic wille he beo his biscoprices weorþe and his socne tolles and teames binnan burh
And ic nelle nanan men geþolian þat ynn fram hande drafe ænig þæra þinga þæs gehennd rihte habbene
And gif hit ænig man deð frencisc oððe flemisc oððe englisc gekyþe me fore and ic him cæde sone
