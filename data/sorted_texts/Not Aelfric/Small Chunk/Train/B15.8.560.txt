Ðis synd þa lond gemæro þe gebyriað into codestune.
Ærest on gytincges æwelm.
Of gytincges æwylm on noð dene on þone grenan weg þ on þone haran stan.
Of þam haran stane andlang grenan weges on scepe clif.
Andlang scepe clifes inon meos mære, of meos mære andlang dene on geolowon ford.
Of geolowon forda on þrist longan dene, ondlong þristlongan dene to brocces slæde.
Of brocces slæde ondlong ecce þ on þa twegen þornas.
Of þam twam þornan on wad beorh, of wad beorhce on lafercan beorh.
Of lafercan beorhge a bi þam æcran heafdan þ on scyttan fæn.
Of scyttan fæn þ on gytinc ondlong þ eft on gytincges æwylm.
