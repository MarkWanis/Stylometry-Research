Ðis syndon þa gemæro to ðeouecan hylle.
Ærest owandes forda eastweard olluncges stete.
Þ hit cymeð to þære hegce olluncges ðære hegge þ in lawern.
Up be lawern þ swa suðen to ðæm forda.
Ofer ðæm forda ollunc stræte þæt on butan þa cotu þ swa on ælles burnan.
Olluncges burnan þ swa on ðæt sic, olluncges sices þ swa wið.
Suþan ðæm more up bi ðæm æcerhege to ðære stræt, olluncges stræt þ swa on þa hegce.
Olluncges ðære hegce norð weard þæt on ða stræt.
Olluncges stræte eastweard, þ swa on ðone hyl.
Olluncges hegce þ swa on lawern, olluncges streames þ swa norðen in ðone ford.
And ða þreo æcras on þæm hylle foran on easthalfe lawern & on norðhealfe stræte.
Ælfweard wæs se forma man & nu hit stant his dohtor on handa & heo is se oðer man.
