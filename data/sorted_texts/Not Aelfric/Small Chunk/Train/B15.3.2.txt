Qua propter ego Swiðuulf episcopus & ða higan æt Hrofes cestre dabunt Biorhtuulfo presbytero
Hæc sunt ætenim termini histius predicti agelli circum acentia bisceopes mearc an norðan east be
Hæc sunt prata que ad illam terram pertinent I et bioccan lea, & an suð healfe strodes an cyninges
Eadgar cynincg of his agenre handa sealde þas boc Leofrice on þara gewitnesse þe her beniþan
Haddune booc.
