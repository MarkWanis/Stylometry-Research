Ðis syn þa gerihto and þa laga, thet archebiscop ah ofer eal Euerwic bynan burh an butan.
Þæt is, aerest Legeraþorp and on nordhealf Munecagate & fra Þurbrandes hus eal up on Walbegate and
Mid sace & mid socne, mid tolle & mid teme, and alcne þridde penig, þe cumde up on Walbegate and
And þridde penig of þone fiscoup scrasudwrasas ford yb dic and þonne þridde, þe up cumð of les
And alc man, þe mid coupfare cume to Euerwyc, eastan odde nordan, sudan odde westan, mid horsum
And fare alc man þe come wille he to kynges scire, wille he to biscopes, be Godes leoue and þes kynges.
And swa hwilc coupman so cumth into biscopes scire and coupad in kinges scire, gyfe þan kinge þæt
And gif ani man formaele hine sylfne of biscopes scire innan kynges scire, habbe se king.
And ealle þa unriht ðe man wirced, gehaded man odde lawede, hoc est se læreda misbeode freolses daes
Þe man wirces binnan burh & butan, ne ðeo kinges greua ne eorles to þissum giltum, buton biscop and
And twa minetras hafy se biscop in Euerwic.
Ðis is to gewitnesse Arngrim Monachus & Oudergrim & Clibern & Wlfstan & Ouldalf & Ulfkil &
And se ercebiscopes hirde & Huga sciregreuan and Willem of Snotingham and Beringar þaes kynges
And habbe he Godes grame þe þis undo; Amen.
