Ðys synd þa lond ge mære into wylleres ege.
Ærest of þere ege on sondbroc.
Of sondbroce on heig weig.
Onlong weiges on norð bommes broc.
Up onlong broces on þa ealdan dic.
Andlong þere dic þ on badsetena gemære.
Of badsetena gemære on tunwealdes stan.
Of tunewealdes stane on wudanhammes broc.
Up onlong broces þ on burhwellan.
Of burhwellan on ða burhwalles of þam burhwallan onbutan eadulfing garan.
And þer onlong stræt þ on suðwardne þone burhwal, And westwardne.
Swa nord oð þa egge.
Þ on bleccan mære.
Onlong riðies.
Þ on ge rihte a be ðan heafdan þ æft on wylleres eige.
