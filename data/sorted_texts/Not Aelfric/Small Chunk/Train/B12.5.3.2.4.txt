Ðonne andswarige se seoca and þus cweðe, To þam þæt ge me smyrian sceoldon.
Ðonne mid ealle liðnysse hine mid feawum wordum godcundre lare getrymme and gestrangie and þus
Aerest þinga ðu most þa digelnysse ðines modes sefan ðurh soþe andetnisse geyppan and unðeowa
Gif he þonne hwæt andettan wille ðæs þe he ær geandet næfde, ðonne tæce se preost, hwæt him to bote
Gif he þonne worldcund man sy, þonne secge him se preost, þæt he mot ærest his hiwhrædene dihtan,
Gif ðonne se seoca cwæþ, þæt he naðer ne mæge ne his synna andette, ne oþrum gemiltsian, ne sylle
