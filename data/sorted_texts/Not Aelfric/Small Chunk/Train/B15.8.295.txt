Ðis is þara XX hidæ boc æt mordune þæ edwig cing gæbocode wynsige hys þægne on ece yrfæ.
Ðis synt þa land ge mero to Mordune ærest of headdam dune slade nyðer on hred burnan andlang burnan
Andlang streames on foslace.
Þonne andlang foslace on þara ecera & heafda an lang þære heafda on ettan pæn on þonne ellem stub
Þonon on hnottan forð of ðam forda on þone ealdan wylle þonon eft on headdan dune sled.
