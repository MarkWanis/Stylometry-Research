Her onginneð Eadmundes gerædnes.
Eadmund cyngc gesamnode micelne sinoð to Lundenbirig on ða halgan easterlican tid ægðer ge
Ðar wæs Oda arcebiscop & Wulfstan arcebiscop & manega oðre biscopas smeagende ymbon heora
Ðæt is ærest þæt hi budon, þæt þa halgan hadas, þe Godes folc læron sculon lifes bisne, ðæt hi heora
& gif hi swa ne don, þonne syn hi þæs wyrðe þe on ðam canone cweð, and þæt hi þolian worldæhta &
Teoðunge we bebeodað ælcum Cristene men be his Cristendome and ciricsceat & Romfeoh &
And gif hit hwa don nelle, si he amansumod.
Gif hwa Cristenes mannes blod ageote, ne cume he na on ðæs cyninges neawiste, ær he on dædbote ga,
Se þe wið nunnan hæme, gehalgodre legerstowe ne sy he wyrðe buton he gebete þe ma þe manslaga;
Eac we gecwædon, þæt ælc biscop bete Godes hus on his agenum & eac þone cyningc minegige, þæt
Ða ðe mansweriað & liblac wyrcað, beon hi a fram ælcum Godes dæle aworpene, buton hi to rihtre
