Her swytelað on þisum gewritæ þæt Ælfgifu seo hlefdige bæcweð V hida æt Heilincigæ into ealdan
And heo bæcwæð Wulfweardæ hwitan V hida of þam ilcan landæ his dæg.
And æftær his dæge ga þa V hida into ealdan mynstræ, þus seo hlæfdige hit bæcwed Wulfweardæ.
Þa wes Wulfweard gyrnenðæ þæt hæ mostæ ofgan his deg þa V hida þæ wæron into mynstre
And sæ bisceop Stigandæ and sæ hiræd on ealdan mynstræ him þæs gætiþodon wið scylcon gersumen
Nu wæron þus heora forewæarð, þæt Wulfweard hebbæ þa V hida his dæg.
And æftær his dæge ga þa V hida into ealdan mynstræ and þa oþræ V hida forð mið þæ seo hlæfdige
Ðises is to witnæssæ, Stigandæ bisceop and Harold eorl and sæ hired on ealdan mynstræ and Ælfwine
Þissæ gewrita syndon III, an is on ealdan mynstræ and þæt oþær is on niwan mynstre and þæt þridde
