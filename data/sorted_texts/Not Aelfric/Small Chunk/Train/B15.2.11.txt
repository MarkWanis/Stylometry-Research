Ælfheah ealdorman becwæð Ælfhere ealdormenn XX hida æt Kingestune.
Ða abæd Osgar abbud æt ælfhere ealdormenn þæt he moste ofgan þæt land æt him mid sceatte ða tiþode
And se abbod sealde him ða an hund mancosa goldes.
Ða wes ofer Eastron micel gemot æt Aþelwarabirig.
& hit wes gesitolad ðar þam hlafardingan þa ðæron weron, þæt wes Adelwold bisceof & Ælfstan bisceop
And þis wes gedon on micelre gewitnesse & þysses gewrites geclofan nam se ealdorman ælfhere to
