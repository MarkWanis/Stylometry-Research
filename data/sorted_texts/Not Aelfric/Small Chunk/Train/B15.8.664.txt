Erest æt wiðig mæde on þone weg oð hit sticað on ðære ealdan stanbricge.
Syððan be onheafdan od winces burch on þa ealdan dic.
Of ðære dic on edbyrhting leage, of ðære leage be wyrt walan oð wodnes die.
Of ðere dic þes upp on þone wudu oððone ealdan wæg, andlang wæges, on ealdan lege, of þære leage on
Of þæm stane &lang hricges on þone ealdan weall, of þæm weall to steorte þanon on corsan, be corsan
