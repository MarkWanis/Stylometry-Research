Edward kincg gret wel mine biscopes & mine eorles & ealle mine þeignes on þan sciren þar Seinte Petre
& ic kiþe eow þat ic habbe gegifen him sace & socne, toll & theam, infangeneþeof & flemenesfirmth,
& ic nelle geþafian þat anig man anig onsting habbe ofer his land ne ofer his men be strande ne be lande
God eow ealle gehealde, Amen.
