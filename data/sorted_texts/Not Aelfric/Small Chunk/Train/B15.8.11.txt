Ðys synd þa XII acera londe gemæra æt ulenbeorge þe Berotulf cyng gefreoda & gebocoda into
Þ is ærest of ulan wyllan on osland mære.
Of osland mære onlong mores on ða brodan stret.
Þanon on ðone rescihtan garan.
Of þam garan on ða mær aac on stanbeorge.
Þanne on þa lytle dic.
On stanbeorge.
Of þam beorge on ðone ealde weg.
Of þam wege in þ riði.
Þanne on ederes broc.
Swa uppon heortpord.
Þanon fif gemære.
Swa on fearnlege.
Þanne on feala mæres broc.
Þ swa on ðone ealdan broc.
Of þam broce on buntinge dic.
Þanne on wynes wyrðe.
Swa on þonne grenan garan.
Þonon on þ riði.
Swa æft on feala mæres broc.
Onlong streames on sceannforde.
Þonan to þam ryðie.
Ðwyres ofer ðene ridig on fif acan.
Þonon on ceoldryðe bece ufeweardne.
Þanon in ðene broc andlang streames.
Of þam streame on ryschealas middewearde on þene blacan broc.
Þonon on ðene ealdan broc.
Onlong broces midstreame on þa lytle riðig.
Of þam riðige æft on ulan wylle.
