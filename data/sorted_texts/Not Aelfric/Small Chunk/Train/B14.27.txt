Ðis is sio gerædnes, þe æþelred cyning and ealle his witan æt Wudustoce geræddan.
An ærest þæt þe ealle to Gode ælmihtigan georne gebugan & his bebodu healdan and unrihtes ealle
An uton ænne God lufian and weorðian and ænne Cristendom ealle healde & ælcne hæþendom mid ealle
Uton ænne cynehlaford holdliche.
