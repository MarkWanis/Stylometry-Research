Willelm kyng gret Hereman biscop & Brihtwi & Scewine & ealle mine ðegenas on Dorsæton freondlice.
& ic cyþe eow ðæt se abbod æt Abbodesbyrig & ða gebroþra & ða land & ða men.
& ælc ðara ðinga ðæt ðe hyrþ in to sancte Petres mynstre to Abbodesbirig ðæt hit ys eall on minre munde
And ic geann him ðæt he beo wirþe hys sace & his socne binnan porte & butan & tolles & teames &
And ic nelle nanum men geþafian ðæt him æt ænigon ðingon misbeode.
God eow gehealde.
