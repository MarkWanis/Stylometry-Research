Þis aren þe landmæres into Haddedune.
Erest of erningestrete into Sumerlededic, and of þare dic into billingbroc.
Andlang broces, and of þan broce into þe mære.
Norð of þan mære andlong Weyes into aldandic.
East andlang dices into ellen styb and on þa stræte.
And suð andlang stræte þat it comeð foren on gean sumerlededic þer it ær bigan þat fiue hiða land mære.
