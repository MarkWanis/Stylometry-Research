Ðis sinte ða gewitnisse of þam lande ðe Alfric paz bohte at frewines laue at hire on paules stret,
Ðis sinte ða gewitnisse of ðam wiman leduue ialdit and of Ieleire, reinfrei þæt is Gedmer, Acuic, and
Ða sint þage gewitnisse of þam lande þe Alfric bohte at Ailworde þat is walword at iacobes circa:
And Særle and Pain namen þæt toll.
Of þam lande bute gestete þe Alfric bohte at Ricerde.
Ðis sint þage gewitnesse Osbern, Alger filius and ware scolfues sunu Rob se ce, and Alger lifflæd, Ralf
