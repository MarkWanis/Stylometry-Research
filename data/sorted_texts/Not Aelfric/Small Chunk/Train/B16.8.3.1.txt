Geatfleda geaf freols for Godes lufa & for heora sawla þearfe þ is Ecceard smið & Ælstan & his wif &
And Arcil & Cole & Ecferð Aldhunes dohter & ealle þa men þe heo nam heora heafod for hyra mete on
Swa hwa swa þis awende & hyre sawla þises bereafie bereafige hine God ælmihtig þises lifes, & heafona
And eac heo hafað gefreod þa men þe heo þigede æt Cwæs patrike.
