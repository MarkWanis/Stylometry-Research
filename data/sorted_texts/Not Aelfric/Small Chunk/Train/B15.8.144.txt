Ærest on ucingc ford of ðæs cumbes heafde on þone smalan weg þurh ðone mor to wiðigslæde þæt to
Swa to wudu forda.
Þ to lulles beorge.
Swa forð andlang harpaðes to mægenstanes dene swa to wiðleagate þ to nædran beorge.
Þ ðurh þone mor beriht ge mære on wiðig mor ðæt eft on ucingc ford.
Ærest of sæ up on hengestes rigc.
Ðonan norð on gyrdweg.
Swa norð andlang hricges on middeweardna sealtera cumb.
Þ be cumbe ing on holan broc þ andlang streames ing on hlos moc.
Adune on stream on seofan æceras.
Þær of streame eft onbutan seofan æceras.
Eft suð on hlos moc.
Ðæt adune on stream oð ða suran apeldran.
Þ suð on ða norð ræwe.
Ðanan eft ofer cern on gata ford.
Ðæt suð bufan litlan graf.
Wwa suð on earnes hlingc.
Of earnes hlince eft ut on sæ.
Þis is seo boc þe æþelstan cing gebocode Frithestane bisceope to Wiþiglea & to Cearn þis syndon
