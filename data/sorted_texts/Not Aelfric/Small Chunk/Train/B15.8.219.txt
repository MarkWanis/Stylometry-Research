Þis his þas anes hiwisces boc at Winterburnan, & ðas oþres hiwisces on whit þe þær to hyrþ, þe Eadred
Þis synt þa land gemæro þæs hiwisces æt winterburnan: ærest of byrhtferþes hlæwe andlang
Þonne niþer andlang burnan swa seo læfer scæt to healdan hlince, þonen swa seo læfer scæt on þæs
Of þam forda swa seo læfre scæt on chypmanna ford, of þam forda swa seo læfer scæt on hors wylle,
Þis his þæs hiwisces land gemæro þe þær to hyrþ on whit: fram hyrste andlang slades to wullafes
Of þam stane andlang lanan to þæs mores heafde, ðonon andlang slades ut on scos fleot, þonne
