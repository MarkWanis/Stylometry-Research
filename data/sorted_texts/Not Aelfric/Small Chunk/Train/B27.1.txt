Ac þis is ne þynceð no us well gesæd.
Forðy on ælcne timan mon ah to læranne & to tihtanne folc to bereowsunge, & to wirceanne ures
Eac biscepas æt heora bisceopstole sæcgað larspel þonne hi lædað in penitentes, & hi doð
And wite ge to gewissan butan ge andettan eowre synnan mid nane þinge ne mid gebedum ne mid
Sumes goodes mannes gesihðe be heofene rice & be helle wite ræd hit & well understond & þu bist þe
Bysne be drymannum & be anum godan men macharius gehatæn.
Gesihðe sumes haliges mannes.
Be andetnyssa.
Be domes dæige.
Be ðam ðe men sceolan forgiuan swa swa hi wyllað þæt godd forgiue heom.
