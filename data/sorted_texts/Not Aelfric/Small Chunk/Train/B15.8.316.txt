Þis is þara tyn hida land boc at Winter burnan þe eadgar cing ge bocode eadrice his þegene on ece yrfe
Þis syndon þara fif hida land gemæra into Winterburnan be westan tune syndries landes.
Ærest æt þam haran stane innan winterburnan þon andlang burnan on þa ellen stybbas þonne of þam
Þonne andlang dic on þone haran stan on hig dune þonne andlang fyrh up to þam heafdon þonne forþ
