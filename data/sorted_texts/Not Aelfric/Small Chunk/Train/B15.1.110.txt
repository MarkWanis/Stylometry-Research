Eadward cingc grett Wulfwig biscop & Raulf eorl & ealle mine þegenas on Oxnafordescire freondlice.
& ic cyðe eow þæt ic hæbbe geunnan Criste & Sancte Dionisie into his halgan mynstre begeondan sæ
& wite he wið God se þe hitt of þære haligan stowe geutige.
& ic wille þæt se biscop dihte boc þær to be minan fullan geleafan.
