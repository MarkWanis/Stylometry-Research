Ðis synd ða land gemæran to oddungga lea.
Ærest of stigelec on þ riþig ondlong riðies þ on þane grenan weg ondlong weges on þa mearc þ swa to
Ondlong weges on þa dic of þam dice on deor leage þæt swa on longan dic ondlong dices on eardlæge
Þonne suð rihte on croh hæma gemæra ondlong þæs gemæres eft on stigelac.
Ða land gemæru æt þære healfre hide æt lawern ærest of lawern on þ riþig ondlond riþiges on þ graf swa
Ondlong þæs weges on haran læh swa eastrihte be þære gebura londe þ on fearn leage of þære leage on
Ðonne is ealles þæs landes þe Osuuold bisceop bocað Cyneþegne þrinde healf hid & VI æceras æt
And se haga be suðan byrig se is XII gerda lang and VIIII gerda brand.
On þa gerad wyrce þ he wyrce þ þ land seo unfor worht into þære halgan stowe.
