Uulfwold abbod gret Gisa bisceop & Ægelnoð abbot & Tofig sciregerefan & ealle þa þegenas on
And ic cyðe eow þæt Eadweard cingc min hlaford geaf me þæt land æt Eofestige þæt min fæder ahte and
Nu kyðe ic eow eallan þæt ic habbe gifen þæt land in to Sanctus Petres mynstre in to Baðan þam
And se ðe mine gyfe geece þe ic habbe gyfen in to Sanctus Petres mynstre geece God ælmihtig his lif
And se þe hit þence to litlianne gelitlige hine God elmihtig her on worulde & þonne he heonan faran
