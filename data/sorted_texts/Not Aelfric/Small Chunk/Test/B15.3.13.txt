Mid tyþe & mid geþafunge Eadgares cynenges & Ælfheres Merna ealdermannes & þæs heorodes æt
& æfter his dæge twam mannum swylce him gecwemest sy & æfter heora forðsiþe þam biscope to bryce
Ðis synd þa landgemæra to þordune.
Ondlong amman broces þæt hit sticað in pidelan.
Ondlong pidelan þæt hit cymð to oredeshamme.
Of oredeshamme on deorelmes dic.
Of deorelmes dic on incsetena gemære.
Of incsetena gemære in þa stræt.
Of þære stræte in þornhæma dic.
Of þære dic in þæt sic.
Of þam sice þæt hit sticað in ammam broc.
& XII æceras on incsetena lande & seo wudung on gemænan grafe to þordune.
Ðonne is ealles þæs landes III hida þe Oswald bisceop bocað æþelstane his ðegne on þa gerad wyrce
