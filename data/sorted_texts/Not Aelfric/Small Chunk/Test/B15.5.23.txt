Her swuteliað on ðisse Cristes bec Æþelnoðes arcebiscop forword & Tokiges embe þæt land æt
Þæt wæs þæt Tokig com to Hrisbeorgan to ðam arcebiscop syþþan Æðelflæd his wif forðfaren wæs &
& se arcebiscop him þæs tiðude & sæde þæt he riht wið hine gedon hæfde þæt he sylf him for ðam
& ðises wæs to gewitnysse Æþelstan æt Bleddehlæwe & Leofwine his sunu & Leofric æt Eaningadene
