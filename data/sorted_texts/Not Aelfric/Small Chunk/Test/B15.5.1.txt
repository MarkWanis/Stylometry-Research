Ceolulf rex wilnade þæs londes æt Bremesgrefan to Heaberht biscop & to his higun.
& þa sende he his erendwreocan to Wulfheard to Intanbeorgum & heht þæt he cuome to him & to þæm
Þa dede he swæ þa hio him to spræcon se biscop & his weotan ymb þæt lond, þæt he his him geuþe þæt
& þa wæs he eaðmodlice ondeta þæt he swæ wulde & to him wilnende wæs þætte hio him funden suelce
Þa sende he monn to þæm ærcebiscope & to Eadberhte & to Dynne & him heht segcgan þæt he wilnade
Þa se ærcebiscop & Eadberht hit wæron erndiende to cyninge þa cuom Dynne to gelærde þone cyninge
