Ærest of strætforda up andlang þæs grenan weges to smiceran æc; andlang wegges to Ketanlege
Of þam herpaðe benorþan labirihtes gete on ðære dice hyrnan adune on þære land sceare to þam twam
Fram ðære lace to þam ligcgendum æc; fram ðam licgendum æc innan sticelan ford; of ðam forda
Fram þære dice hyrnan andlang hæccean to bridianbeara lace on þone hnottan stybb; of þam hnottan
Andlang hæccean to þære ræw apoldran; andlang hæccean to cern hlinces herpaðe.
Andlang herpaðes to þan holan wege; andlang dic suð to þære holan lace; andlang ðære lace up innan
