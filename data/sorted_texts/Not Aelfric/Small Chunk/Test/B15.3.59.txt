Odo biscop of Baius gret Landfranc arcebiscop & Hæimonem scir gerefan, & ealle þæs kinges þegenas
Si eow eallum cuð, þæt ic Odo biscop of Baius, & eorl on Cænt geann ure moder þæt is Christes circean
Þis ic do for mines lafordes alysednesse Willelmes kinges, & for minre, & for þæra manna alysednesse,
