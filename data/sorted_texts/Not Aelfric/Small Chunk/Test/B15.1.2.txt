In seo nama of ure heland cryst wurthmynt of god and for hale of myne sawle ye ædelred myd fultune of
Yc wil and yc getithige thæt seo sylfa haly stowe swa fre ge beo swa swa thæt was tha tha myne lyfod
And thæt seo theowan of god thær weald and myta of eall thyng habben swa swa osryc hæfad and swa
And yc wyll thæt nea mann of mynn ne othth cynnesmann bycon seo habberon othth men of seo sylfan
Thys was gewreten and gegyfe wyd seo wyll of swydewale dryht in ceo mycel gadryenge and
And swa hwa swa seo freonesse gyf and statheled to thæt haly cyryc gebreacad ic bydde god thæt thron
And forthan ye wyll thæt thylc stathelyng of gedan and freonesse gyfe yn eall tydene obyden ye adelred
