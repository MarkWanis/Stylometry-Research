Uesicula i croph.
Mergulus saxonice dohfugal uel alacra uel saxonice tuheri.
Cignus saxonice suuan.
Onocrotalus saxonice quë sonitum facit in aqua roredumble.
Herodio falco saxonice.
Caradrion, quidam dicunt saxonice lericha.
Upupa, siue opopa, id est saxonice uuito hoffa.
Uespertilio saxonice fledare mustra.
Migale, quidam dicunt haramo.
