Ðis synd þa land gemæru æt mearnan clyfe X hida.
Þ is þonne ærest of turf hlawan in þone holen weg.
Of þam holan wege innon afene þ hit cymeþ wiþ ufan wearroces yge.
And se mæd hom þær to.
Þ swa up on þone lytlan hlauw.
Of þam hlawe on þa mær dic.
Of þære dic on bradan wege.
Ondlang weges innan þ sic.
Ondlang þæs sices innon þone þyrs pyt.
Of þæm pytte innan pidewællan.
Of pidwællan æfter þam wege to þam uferan heafdan.
Þæt æfter heafdan æft in turf hlawen.
