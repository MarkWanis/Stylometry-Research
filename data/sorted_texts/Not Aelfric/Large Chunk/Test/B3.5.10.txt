Leofa man, ðe is mycel þearf þæt ðu þas drihtenlican tide georne geþence.
Þonne þu arisan wylle, bide þinne drihten eadmodlice forgifennesse ealra þara synna þe þu wið Godes
And lufa þinne drihten mid inneweardum mode, and mid inneweardum mægene.
And bide hine georne þæt ðu mote on þisse halgan tide hwæt hwyge gewyrcan þæs þe his milda willa sy,
And ðenc þæt God ælmihtig on þisse halgan tide swyðe mycel adreah and aræfnode and geðrowode for
And he sylf hi gesette butan æghwylcan eorðlican bigleofan, and he wæs geset ofer þæs temples
And he þær ðone deofol oferswiðde and geðreatode þæt ungetreowe folc and ða unlædan Iudeas hine
And him heora þegna þreat to gelæddon and hine gebundon and hine to heora demum and to heora
And hi hine syððan to deaðe forræddon and hine on rode ahengon and gefæstnodon and hi þær his
And heo hine mid bradre handa under þæt wange slogan and heo him fullice on þæt neb ræhton and
Soð hi þæs sædon þeah hi his na wendon.
He is and a wæs and a bið ealra cyninga cyning, and he mihte eaðe him gebeorgan æt ðam deaþe, gyf
Ac he wolde þæt we clæne þurh hine wurdon, and he wolde þa.
And gyt wile þæt we onfon rihtne geleafan for ðon þe he us of deofles hæftnydnysse alysde.
And þonne is ðe, leofa man, mycel nydþearf þæt ðu on ælcne timan wið deofles lare wær sy.
And þæt þu þas drihtenlican tide georne geþence mid þinre cyricsocne and mid þinum gebedum, and
And beorhðe georne wið ealle unnytlice þing and wið ealle idelnessa, and geearnaðe to Gode
