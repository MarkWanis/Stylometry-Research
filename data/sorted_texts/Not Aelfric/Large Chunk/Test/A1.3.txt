Ða wearð folctoga forht on mode, acul for þam egesan.
Geseah he engles hand in sele writan Sennera wite.
Þæt gyddedon gumena mænigeo, hæleð in healle, hwæt seo hand write to þam beacne burhsittendum.
Werede comon on þæt wundor seon.
Sohton þa swiðe in sefan gehydum, hwæt seo hand write haliges gastes.
