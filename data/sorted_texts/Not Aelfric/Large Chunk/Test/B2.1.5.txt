Interrogatus Iesus a discipulis de consummatione seculi, dixit eis: Cum uideritis abhominationem
Væ pregnantibus et nutrientibus in illis diebus.
erunt enim tribulationes tales quales non fuerunt ab initio creature quam condidit Deus usque nunc neque
Leofan men, ures Drihtnes apostolas ahsadan hwilum hine sylfne ymbe þisre worulde geendunge.
Ða sæde he heom þæt swilce earfoðnessa & swylce gedrecednessa sculan on worulde ær þam ende
And þæt godspel cwæð: Wa ðam wifum þe þonne tymað & on þam earmlican timan heora cild fedað.
La, nyde hit sceal eac on worulde for folces synnan yfelian swyðe, forðam nu is se tima þe Paulus se
He sæde hwilum þam biscope Tymothee þæt on ðam endenyhstan dagum þissere worulde beoð
And gecnawe se ðe cunne, nu is se tima þæt ðeos woruld is gemæncged mid mænigfealdan mane &
Ðæt is on Englisc, forðam þe unriht weaxeð ealles to wide, soð lufu colað.
Ne man God ne lufað swa swa man scolde, ne manna getrywða to ahte ne standað, ac unriht ricsað wide
Eala, eala, ac þa wæs mycel blis & bot seo betste mannum towerd þa Crist com on ðas woruld þurh
And mycel is seo þwyrnes þe nu is towerd, gebide ðære yrmðe se þe hit gebide, þæt Antecrist geboren
Crist wæs ealra bearna betst geboren þe æfre geboren wurde, & Antecrist bið ealra þæra bearna wyrst
Nu sceal hit nyde yfelian swyðe, forðam þe hit nealæcð georne his timan, ealswa hit awriten is &
Þæt is on Englisc, æfter þusend gearum bið Satanas unbunden.
Þusend geara & eac ma is nu agan syððan Crist wæs mid mannum on menniscan hiwe, & nu syndon
Men syndon swicole, & woruld is þe wyrse, & þæt us dereð eallum; & huru hit sceal hefegian
Nu ða yfelan & ða swicelan swa oferlice swyðe brædað on worulde ongean þæt mæste yfel þe mannum
Eala, mycel wæs seo ehtnes þe cristene þoledon iu ær on worlde oft & gelome þurh wælhreowe
And oft ær wæs mænigfeald ehtnes, næfre þeah þam gelic þe æfter ðysan gyt bið.
Forðam hit wæs oft ær þæt Godes halgan fela wundra þurh Godes mihta openlice worhtan on gemang
Ac hit ne bið na swa on Antecristes timan.
Ne magan þonne halige men on þam timan ænige tacna openlice wyrcan, ac sculan þolian eal þæt heom
Ne God þonne ane hwile his mihta ne his wundra sylf nele cyðan, swa he oft ær dyde, ac læt þone
He bið mennisc man geboren, ac he bið þeah mid deofles gaste eal afylled; & se gesewenlica feond
And feorðehealf gear he ricsað ofer mancynn & mid his scincræftum mæst manna beswicð þe æfre ær
And þa ðe he elles mid his lotwrecnum bepæcan ne mæg, þa he wile þreatian & ægeslice wyldan &
He aginð leogan deoflice swyðe & ætsæcð Cristes & cweð þæt he sylf sy Godes agen bearn, &
And god him geðafað þæt for manna gewyrhtum þæt he sume hwile mot swa wodlice derian, forðam þe
And þa ðe swa gesælige þonne weorþað þæt hi Godes lage healdað on an swyðe georne & on rihtan
Ac se bið gesælig þe þonne ne awacað, forðam raðe æfter þam witod him bið towerd þurh Godes mihte
La, hwylc wundor bið þeah se mennisca deofol synfullum mote heardlice derian, þonne God geþafað
Nis se man on life þe mæge oððe cunne swa yfel hit asecgan swa hit sceal geweorðan on þam deoflican
Ne byrhð þonne broðor oðrum hwilan ne fæder his bearne ne bearn his agenum fæder ne gesibb
And þeodscypas winnað & sacað heom betweonan foran to þam timan þe þis sceal geweorþan.
Eac sceal aspringan wide & side sacu & clacu, hol & hete & rypera reaflac, here & hunger, bryne &
And mænigfealde tacna beoð wide gesawene on sunnan & on monan & on mistlican tunglan, & fela
And eal hit forwurde gyf God ne gescyrte þæs þeodscaðan lifdagas þe raþor ðurh his mihta.
Ac for þæra gebeorge þe him syn gecorene & ðe he habban wyle gehealden & geholpen, he fordeð
Ðonne wurð Godes dom rihtlice toscaden; & ða þonne witodlice þe nu God lufiað & Godes lagum
Ðær is ece blis & æfre bið in earla worulda woruld a butan ende, amen.
