Æt Hymeltune, Wulfgete & Wulfmære.
Ic Osuuold þurh Godes giefe arcebisceop mid geþafunge & leafe Eadwardes Angulcyninges & Ælfheres
Þæt is an hid æt Hymeltune sumum cnihte þam is Wulfgeat noma mid eallum þingum þe þærto belimpað
Sit autem terra ista libera omni regi nisi ecclesiastici censi.
Sancta (10) Maria & sanctus Michahel cum sancto Petro & eallum Godes halgum gemiltsien þis
Gief hwa buton gewrihtum hit abrecan wille hæbbe him wið God gemæne on þam ytemestan dæge
Þis is þære are hide landgemæru æt Hymeltune.
Þæt is ærest æt egcbrihtingcþirne ufeweardre.
Of þære þyrne ondlond þære hægce þ on scipene lea.
Of scipene lea þæt on efene lea þæt to mægdenne brigce.
Of þære brigce ondlong hegce þæt to hryancrofte.
Ondlong þæs croftes heafodlondes þæt to þæm oþran heafodlonde.
Of þæm heafodlonde þæt to bercrofte.
Of þæm crofte þæt eft to hegcbrihtigncþyrne ufeweardre.
Þonne gebirað se fifta æcer þære dalmædue to þære hide.
Þis is þæs wuda gemære þe to þære hide gebyreð.
Þæt is ærest æt ceastergeate to ceasterwege.
Ondlong ceasterweges to middelwege.
Of middelwege þæt eft to ceastergeate.
Of þæm geate to longan leage.
Of longan leage ondlong þære dic to deorleage, to þæm hriþige, ondlong hriþies to dunhæmstedes
Ondlong þæs æcergeardes to longan æcre.
Ondlong þæs æceres to þæm midlestan wicwege.
Ondlong þæs weges þæt eft to ceastergeate.
Wulfgeat wæs se forma man & Wulfmær is þe oðer þe hit nu on honda stant.
Her is seo hondseten.
