Willelm Engla Kyncg & eorl ofer Normandie & ofer þa Mans.
Ic sende gretincge arcebiscopan & leodbiscopan abbodan & eorlan & eallum geleaffullan mannum.
We gelyfað & geara witan þat we þurh Godes mildheortnysse synd uponhafan to þisses Kynerices
For þi gedauenað us þat we geornlice sinægen ymb þa gesundfullan staþelunge ealles uran underðeoddan
We willað eac þat hit beo cuð eallan geleaffullan mannum æigþer þam þe nu andwyrde syndan on life &
And anbidað þara eadigan ærista dæg mid unformolsnodan lichamon.
Þa for þissere spræce wæs lange hwile micel geflit betwux Arfast biscope & Baldwine þara forseadan
Us þuhte þa riht þat hi begen comon to urum hyrede & þat heora ægþer tala come beforan us & beforan
Hi comon þa begen beforan us to gesetton dæge & se biscop tealde ful gerædelice his tale gif hit soð
Se abbod Baldwine tealde þa his tala þær to geanes ærest hu Cnut Kincg draf ut þa preostas of þara
And sette munecas þær mid gemænan ræde his arcebiscopa & leodbiscopa & abboda & eorla &
And þone forman abbod þæs mynstres hadode se biscop of Lundene & þone oþerne se biscop of
And hisne abbod Baldwine se nuis se ðridda abbod hine halgode se ercebiscop of Cantwarabyrig & he
And on eallan þam fyrste þat mynster & þæs mynstres ealdras wæron æfre uncwid & uncrafod fram
And þa munecas underfengon had of swa hwilcon biscope swa him betst gelicode be heora abbodes
Siððan he þis eall geteald hæfde.
Þa brohte he forð Cnutes Kynges gewrit & Eadwardes þæs wulderfullan Kynges.
On þam wæs geswutelad þat ða Kyngas gefreodon þa halgan stowe & æcne freodom sealdon fram ealra
Þawa heora beigra tala & þæs abbodes gewitnesse gehyrd hæfdon þa bebead ic him eallum þe ðær æt
Ða eodon þa witan togædere to þam dome se ercebiscop Lanfram of Cantwarabyrig & Thomas
Ic geþafode þa swa hit riht wes heora ealra unwiðsecgendlican dome & geþafunge sylle.
Siððan þær æfter us gelicode þat we mid ræde arcebiscopa & biscopa & abboda & eorla & oðra
And we gefæstnodan eac þas kartan mid uran agenum hand gewrite.
And mid ures insigeles onðryste to þam þat þara foresprecenan stowe freodom on ecnysse þuyrhwunie.
