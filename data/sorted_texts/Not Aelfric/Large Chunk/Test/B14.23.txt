In nomine Domini, anno dominciae incarnationis MVIII.
Ðis is seo gerædnes, þe Engla cyng & ægðer ge gehadode ge læwede witan gecuran and geræddan.
Ðæt is þonne ærest, þæt we ealle ænne God lufian & weorðian & ænne Cristendom georne healdan &
& ures hlafordes gerædnes & his witena is, þæt man rihte laga up arære & ælce unlaga georne afylle, &
& ures hlafordes gerædnes & his witena is, þæt man Cristene menn & unforworhte of earde ne sylle, ne
& ures hlafordes gerædnes & his witena is, þæt man Cristene men for ealles to lytlum to deaðe ne
Ac elles geræde man friðlice steora folce to þearfe & ne forspille for lytlum Godes handgeweorc & his
& ures hlafordes gerædnes & his witena is, þæt ælces hades menn georne gebugan for Gode & for
& huruþinga Godes þeowas biscopas & abbudas, munecas & mynecena, preostas & nunnan to rihte
& ures hlafordes gerædnes & his witena is, þæt muneca gehwylc, þe ute sy of mynstre & regoles ne
& se munuc, þe mynster næbbe, cume scirebiscope & trywsie hine sylfne wið God & wið men, & he
& gyf he þæt gelæste, þonne bið he wyrðe, þæt hine man þe bet healde, wunige þær he wunige.
& canonicas, þær seo ar sy, þæt hy beoddern & slæpern habban magan, healdan heora mynster mid rihte
& ealle mæssepreostas we biddað & lærað, þæt hy beorgan heom sylfum wið Godes yrre.
Fulgeorne hy witan, þæt hy nagon mid rihte þurh hæmedþing wifes gemanan.
& se ðe þæs geswican wille & clænnesse healdan, hæbbe he Godes miltse, & þær to eacan to
& se þe þæt nelle þæt his hade gebyrige: wanige his weorðscype ge for Gode ge for worolde.
& æghwylc Cristen man eac unriht hæmed georne forbuge & godcunde laga rihtlice healde.
& sy ælc cyrice on Godes griðe & on ðæs cynges & on ealles Cristenes folces.
& ænig man heonan forð cyrican ne ðeowige, ne cyricmagunge mid unriht ne macie, ne cyricþen ne
& gelæste man Godes gerihta georne æghwylce geare.
Ðæt is sulhælmessan XV niht onufan eastran & geogoðe teoðunge be pentecosten & eorðwæstma be
& saulsceat is rihtast, þæt man symle gelæste æt openum græfe.
& gyf man ænig lic of rihtscriftscire elles hwar lecge, gelæste man saulsceat swa þeh into þam
& ealle Godes gerihta fyrðrige man georne, ealswa hit þearf is.
& freolsa & fæstena healde man rihtlice.
Sunnandæges freols healde man georne, swa þærto gebyrige.
& cypinga & folcgemota on ðam halgan dæge geswice man georne.
& sancte Marian freolstida ealle weorðie man georne ærest mid fæstene & syððan mid freolse.
& to æghwylces apostoles heahtide fæste man & freolsige; buton to Philippus & Iacobus freolse ne
Elles oðre freolsa & fæstena healde man georne, swa swa þa heoldan þa ðe betst heoldan.
& sancte Eadwerdes mæssedæg witan habbað gecoren, þæt man freolsian sceal ofer eal Englaland on
& fæstan ælce Frigedæg, butan hit freols sy.
& ordal & aðas syndan tocweðen freolsdagum & rihtymbrendagum & fram Adventum Domini oð
& beo ðam halgum tidan, ealswa hit riht is, eallum Cristenum mannum sib & som gemæne, & ælc sacu
& gyf hwa oðrum scyle borh oððon bote æt woroldlican þingan, gelæste hit georne ær oððon æfter.
& sy ælc wydewe, þe hy sylfe mid rihte gehealde, on Godes griðe & on þæs cynges.
& sitte ælc XII monað werleas; ceose syððan þæt heo sylf wille.
& æghwylc Cristen man do, swa him þearf is: gyme his Cristendomes georne & gewunige gelomlice to
& gearwige eac to huslgange oft & gelome gehwa hine sylfne, & word & weorc fadige mid rihte & að
& æghwylc unriht aweorpe man georne of þysan earde, þæs þe man gedon mæge.
& swicollice dæda & laðlice unlaga ascunige man swyðe, þæt is: false gewihta & woge gemeta & lease
Ac lufige man Godes riht heonan forð georne wordes & dæde; þonne wyrð þysse þeode sona God
& beo man georne ymbe friðes bote & ymbe feos bote æghwar on earde, & ymbe burhbota on
& ymbe scipfyrðunga, swa man geornost mæge, þæt æghwylc geset sy sona ofer eastran æghwylce
& gyf hwa butan leafe of fyrde gewende, þe se cyning sylf on sy, plihte him sylfum & ealre his are.
& se þe elles of fyrde gewende, beo se CXX scillinga scyldig.
& gyf ænig amansumad man, butan hit friðbena sy, on þæs cynges neaweste ahwar gewunige, ær ðam
& gyf hwa ymb cyninges feorh syrwe, sy he his feores scyldig; & gif he ladian wille, do þæt be ðæs
& gyf hwa forsteal oððon openne wiþercwyde ongean lahriht Christes oððe cyninges ahwar gewyrce,
& gyf he ongean riht þurh æhlyp geonbyrde & swa gewyrce, þæt hine man afylle, licge ægylde eallan
& æfre alicgan heonan forð þa unlaga, þe ær þysan wæran to gewunelice wide.
& æghwylce unlaga alecge man georne.
