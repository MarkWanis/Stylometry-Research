Ad sacerdotes.
Tæcað cristenum mannum georne and gelome rihtne geleafan and þæt hi cunnon heora cristendomes and
An geleafa is on ænne soðne God, and an fulluht we habbað, þæt we sculon rihtlice healdon.
Se gehealt his fulluht rihtlice, se ðe gehealt Godes beboda and forbuhð deofles unlarum; and se abrecð
God ælmihtig is þeah swa mildheort, þæt he wile gemiltsian þam, þe fram synnum gecyrð, gyf he mid
Synfulles mannes læcedom is, þæt he andette and bete georne and æfre geswice ælces unrihtes.
Utan we don sylfe, swa us mycel þearf is, healdan Godes bebodu and bysnian and bodian georne þære
Hu mæg ænig man oðrum fremian oððon to his hlaforde oðrum þingian, gif he his hlaforde sylf hæfð
Ac utan gecweman Gode ælmihtigan mid clænum geþance and ægðer ge on wordum ge on weorcum ge
Leofan men, doð, swa ic eow lære for Godes lufan and for sanctam Marian and eac ic bidde eow,
Hit gebyreð eow, þæt ge me gearwe beon swa fyr swa nyr swa hwæþer, swa ic wille, and þæt ic huru
Doð sylfe wel and læraþ wel þam, þe ge læran sculon, þæt hi georne to gode don, swa mycel, swa hi
