Ða þa he on neorxnawonge wæs, he wæs mid heortan clænnesse and mid heofenlicre gesyhðe eadigra
We þa syðþan of ðæs ylcan mannes flæsce on þas wræclican woruld acende wurdon, and we ða
Ac flæsclice men, forðon þe hy þa ungesynelican ne magon angytan, heo þonne tweogað, hwæþer hit
Ac Adam se æresta man ne þorfte he na tweogan, þeah ðe he ut adrifen wære of neorxnawonges gefean,
