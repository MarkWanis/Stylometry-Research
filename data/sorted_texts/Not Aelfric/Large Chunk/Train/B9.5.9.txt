Her onginneð seo æftre boc dialogorum be life & wundrum sanctus Benedictes abbodes.
Her uppyrneð se æftera stream ðære godcundan spræce, se cymð of þam burnon þæs gastlican
Þysum wordum se ylca gyldenmuða Gregorius wæs sprekende & þus cwæð, sum wæs arwurðes lifes
Se of þære tide his cnihthades wæs berende ealdlice heortan & oferstah his ylde mid godum þeawum
Ac þa þa he wæs þa gita on þissere eorðan, þæt he mihte freolice brucan hwilwendlicra þinga, þa
He wæs geboren of freogum & of æðelum kynrene of Nursie þære mægðe & wæs befæsted to
Ac þa þa he on þam geseah fela manna faran þurh þa stæðhlepan hyra leahtra, þa wiðteah he his
Þam he wiðbræd, þe læs hwæt unrihtes hine on his geþance gehrine, & he sylf syððon ferde eall on
Witodlice forsewenum bigengum þara boccræfta he forlet his fæder hus & æhta & gewilnode, þæt
He gewat þanone swiðe gewissfullice, swilce he ungewis wære, & ferde swyðe wislice swa
Ne leornode ic na ealle þyses halgan weres dæda, ac þa feawa, þe ic cyðe, ic oncneow æt his feower
Witodlice þa þa se halga wer Benedictus eallunga forlet to leornienne þa boccræftas, þa geteohhode
Þa þa hi becomon to þære stowe, þe is genemned Effide, þa gewunedon hi þær mid manegum
Þa weras hi þær mid soðre lufe gehæfdon on þæs eadigan Petres cyricean.
