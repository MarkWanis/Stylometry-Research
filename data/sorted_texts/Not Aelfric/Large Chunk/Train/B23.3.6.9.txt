On VI nihtne monan do þonne hig on þin beð, ðonne hafast þu þæron nenige wunelic sar, ac þu þer byst

On IX nihtte monan, fer to cyniges bene, gang in to him æt fulre seo, þonne byst þu gesundful wyð hine.

On XII nihte monan byð god to feranne ofer sæ & on hird to ferenne & æac to gewyfianne.
