Ðis man gerædde, ða se micele here com to lande.
Ealle we beþurfan, þæt we geornlice earnian, þæt we Godes miltse & his mildheortnesse habban moton
Nu wille we, þæt eal folc to gemænelicre dædbote þrig dagas be hlafe & wirtum & wætere, þæt is on
& cume manna gehwilc bærefot to circan buton golde & glæncgum, & ga man to scrifte.
& gan ealle ut mid halidome & clipian inweardre heortan georne to Criste.
& sceote man æghwilce hide pænig oððe pæniges weorð.
& bringe man þæt to cirican & siððan on þreo dæle be scriftes & be tunesgerefan gewitnesse.
& gif hwa þis ne gelæste, ðonne gebete he þæt, swa swa hit gelagod is: bunda mid XXX pænigum þræl
& swa hwar swa þæt feoh up arise, dæle man on Godes est æghwilcne pænig.
& ealswa þone mete, þe gehwa brucan wolde, gif him þæt fæsten swa geboden nære, dæle man on
& hiredmanna gehwilc sille pænig to ælmessan, oððe his hlaford sille for hine, buton he silf hæbbe; &
& þeowemen þa ðrig dagas beon weorces gefreode wið ciricsocne & wið ðam, þe hi þæt fæsten þe
& on æghwilcan mynstre singe eal geferræden ætgædere heora saltere þa ðry dagas.
& ælc mæssepreost mæssige for urne hlaford & for ealle his þeode.
& þar to eacan mæssige man æghwilce dæge on ælcan minstre ane mæssan sinderlice for ðare neode,
& æt ælcan tidsange eal hired aþenedum limum ætforan Godes weofode singe þone sealm: Domine,
& ealle gemænelice, gehadode & læwede, bugan to Gode georne & geearnian his mildse.
& æghwilce geare heonon forð gelæste man Godes gerihta huru rihtlice, wið ðam þe us God ælmihtig
