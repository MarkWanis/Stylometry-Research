Seðe on anre nihte monan weorðeð untrum, se bið on ðære adle swiðe geswenced.
On II nihta monan, hraðe æfter sare he ariseð.
Gif on III he winneð, & eft in þære untrumnesse se mon swelteð.
Gif on IIII, he winneð & eft ariseð.
Gif on V, ne gedegeð þe þa adle.
Gif on VI, he winneð & ariseð.
On VII nihte aldne monnan, he sceal feala findan, butan him mihtig God milde wurþe, færlice hine deað
Gif he bið an VIII, ne leofeð he na lange.
Gif on IX, he bið lange seoc.
Gif on X, on his heortan unhælo cymð, & he bið fræcnoð.
Gif on XI, on langum sare he sargað, & he gelomlice his hælo hafoð eft.
Gif on XII, he winneð & eft ariseð.
On XIII, lytel sticce he ligeð seoc.
On XIIII, he winneð & ariseð.
Gif on XV, fræclice bið his þing.
Gif on XVI, on þære stowe he ariseð.
Gif on XVII, he swinceð & eft ariseð.
On XVIII, he winneð & eft ariseð.
On XIX, he winneð & ariseð.
On XX, he ariseð.
On XXI, he bið lange seoc.
On XXII, rædlice he hal ariseð.
On XXIII, he ariseð.
On XXIIII, he bið langa lama & swelteð.
On XXV, he hraþe ariseð.
Gif on XXVI & on XXVII, frecne bið þæs mannes þing, þæt ifel uneaðe beflið.
On XXVIII & on XXIX, he of þam sare sone ariseð.
Gif XXX, he bið lange seoc, þæhweðere ariseð.
Ðis is eallum gemæne iungum and ealdum.
