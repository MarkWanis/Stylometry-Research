Halwun hoce on excestre freode hægelflæde hire wimman þi hy bocte & tilde for hire sawuale crist &
Amen.
Her kyð on þisse bec þæt æilgyuu gode alysde hig & dunna & heora ofspring, æt mangode to XIII
& æignulf portgerefa & Godric gupa namon þæt toll on manlefes gewittnisse, & on leowerdes healta,
And hæbbe he godes curs, þe þis æfre undo a on ecnysse, Amen.
Her kyð on þyssere bec þæt godwine blaca bohte hine sylfne & his wyf & his ofspring æt willelme
And hæbbe he godes curs þe hit æfre undo amen.
Her kyð on ðysse bec þæt edwy beorneges sunu lysde hyne and his wif and his cyld on edwerdes
A kynstanes gewittnesse preost, and a leofsuna gewittnisse a wunforda & an ælfrices hwita & on
Her kyð on þissere bec þæt ediuuu sæuugeles laf bohte gladu æt colewine wyð healfe punde to cepe
And ælword portgerefa nam þæt toll.
And þær to was gewitniss leowine leowordes broðor & ælwi blaca & ælwine se cyng & land byriht &
Amen.
Her cyð on þisse bec þæt brihtmær æt holacumbe hæfð geboht hine & ælfgifu his wif & hira cild, &
On dudemannes gewitnisse preostes on exancestre & on leofwines preostes on hwita stane, & on
And hæbbe he cristes curs & sancta marian & sanctus petre þe þis æfre undo.
And on ealles þæs hundrides gewitnisse on exancestre.
