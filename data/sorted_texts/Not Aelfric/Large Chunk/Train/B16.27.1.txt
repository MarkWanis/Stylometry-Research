Her cyð on þisum gewrite þæt Orcy hæfð gegyfen þæ gegyldhealle & þone stede æt Abbodesbyrig Gode
& þam gyldscipe to agenne on dæge & æfter dæge him & his gebeddan to langsumum gemynde.
Se ðe þis awendan wyle hæbbe him wið Gode gemæne on þæm micclan dome.
Ðonne syndon ðis þa forword þe Orecy & þa gegyldan æt Abbodesbyrig gecoren habbað Gode to lofe
Ðæt is ærost, ðrym nihton ær Petres mæssan æt ælcon gegyldan ænne peningc oððe an peningcwurð
& on mæsse æfen æt twam gegyldum ænne bradne hlaf well besewen & well gesyfled to urum
& fif wucan ær Petres mæssedæge sceote ælc gegylda ænne gyldsester fulne clænes hwætes, & þæt beo
Be þæs inganges wite, þæt syndon þry sestras hwætes & se wudu beo gelæst binnan ðrym dagum æfter
And se ðe bryðene underfehð, & þæt gecweme ne deð, beo he his inganges scyldig, & þær ne beo nan
& se gylda þe oðerne misgret innan gylde on eornostne hige, gebete he þæt be his ingange wið ealre
& syððan wið þone man þe he mysgrette, swa swa he hit findan mæge, & gif he nele to bote gebugan,
And se ðe ma manna inlæde ðonne he sceole buton ðæs stiwerdes leafe & ðæra feormera gylde his
And gyf ænigum on urum geferrædene his forðsið getide, sceote ælc gegylda ænne peningc æt þam lice
& gyf ure ænig geuntrumod sy binnan syxtigum milan þonne findon we fiftyne men þæt hine gefeccon.
& gyf he forðfaren sy XXX, & þa hine gebringon to þære stowe þe he to gyrnde on his life.
& gyf he on neawyste forðfaren sy, warnige man þone stiwerd to hwylcere stowe þæt lic sceole, & se
Swa fela swa he mæge mæst to geridan oððe to gesendan, þæt hi þærto cumon, & þæt lic wurðlice
Þæt byð rihtlice gecweden gyldrædene þæt we þus don, & hit geristð þæt well, ægðer ge for Gode ge
Forðan ðe we nyton hwylc ure hraðost forðfærð.
Þonne gelyfe we þurh Godes fultume, þæt us fremige eallon þas forword gecweden on ær gif we hit
Uton biddan God ælmihtigne georne mid inwerdre heortan, þæt he us miltsige & eac swa his halgan
Forðan ðe we for his lufon þis gegyld gegaderodon.
He hæfð þone anweald on heofenum, þæt he mot lætan into heofenon þone þe he wyle, & forwirnan
Swa swa Crist sylf cwæð to him on his godspelle, Petrus ic betæce þe heofenan rices cæge, & swa
& swa hwæt swa ðu wylt habban unbunden ofer eorðan þæt byð unbunden on heofenum.
Uton habban hyht & hopan to him þæt he us bewite a her on worulde.
& æfter forðsiðe ure sawla gehelpe he us gebringe on ecere reste: Amen.
