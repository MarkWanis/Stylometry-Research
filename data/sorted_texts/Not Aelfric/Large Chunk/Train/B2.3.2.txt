Leofan men, us is swyðe mycel þearf on ælcne timan þæt we gemyndige beon þæra þinga þe us for gode
Ðonne is Lenctentid eallum cristenum mannum huru nedbehefe rihtlice to gehealdenne, forðam nis ænig
Ðonne is Lencten us eallum to dædbote gescyft þæt we geornlice on þam fæce þa þing wið God
Us gebyreð þæt we ælces þinges ure teoðunge rihtlice Gode betæcan.
Ðonne syndan þas dagas getealde for teoðing dagas innan geares fæce, & we sculan eac þa teoþunge
Do þonne cristenra manna gehwylc ealswa hit þearf is, healde his fæsten swyðe rihtlice; þæt is, þæt
Ac sece gehwa his cyrican georne mid clænan geðance & dæghwamlice mæssan gestande & hine to his
And manna gehwylc on Godes est his ælmessan bliðelice sylle be þam þe hine onhagie, ær he sylf on
And beorge huru manna gehwylc wið oferdruncen him georne, forðam Crist his forbead sylf on his
& witodlice þeah hwa on dæg gefæste ful lange, gyf he syððan hine sylfne gedweleð mid gedrynce &
Ac þas tide man sceal mid mycelre forhæfednesse healdan, & sceal manna gehwylc hine sylfne georne
Hir is nu swyðe gedafenlic tima þæt we us sylfe georne clænsian & ðurh soðe andetnesse ure synna us
And se ðe openlice befeallen sy þurh deofles scyfe on healice misdæde, he eac þæt georne openlice
And sume men syndon eac þe nyde sculan of cyricgemanan þas halgan tid ascadene mid rihte weorðan
Ne beo þeah æni man ealles to ormod for his misdædan.
Se is swyðe milde þe mancyn geworhte & æfter ðam eft gebohte deorweorðum ceape, & he wile
Leofan men, Adam wearð of myclum myrhðum, swa we ær cwedon, bescofen to hefigum geswincum.
& swa sceal eac of cyrican myrhðe for myclum gewyrhtum se man gebugan sume hwile þe healice
And æfre swa he eadmodlicor þæt deð, swa him God ælmihtig þe raðer gemiltsað.
Leofan men, on Wodnesdæg, þe byð caput ieiunii, bisceopas ascadað on manegan stowan ut of cyrican
And eft on Ðunresdæg ær Eastran hy geinniað into cyrican þa ðe geornlice þæt Lencten heora synna
