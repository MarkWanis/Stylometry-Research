Ðis synd ða friðmal & ða forword, ðe æthelred cyng & ealle his witan wið ðone here gedon habbað, ðe
Ðæt ærost, þæt woroldfrið stande betweox æthelrede cynge & eallum his leodscipe & eallum ðam here,
& gif ænig sciphere on Englaland hergie, þæt we habban heora ealra fultum; & we him sculon mete
& ælc ðæra landa ðe ænigne friðige ðæra ðe ænglaland hergie, beo hit utlah wið us & wið ealne here.
& ælc ceapscip frið hæbbe, ðe binnan muðan cuman, ðeh hit unfriðscyp sy, gyf hit undrifen bið.
& ðeh hit gedriuen beo & hit ætfleo to hwilcre friðbyrig, & ða menn up ætberstan into ðære byrig,
& ælc agenra friðmanna frið hæbbe, ge on lande ge on wætere, ge binnan muðan ge butan.

Gif he liuiendre handa team gecenne, & sy on oðere scire se, ðe he to tymð, hæbbe swa langne fyrst,
Sette on ða hand, ðe hit him sealde, & bidde, þæt he clænsie, gif he mæge.

Hwilon stod, þæt man sceolde ðrywa tyman, ðær hit ærest befangen wære, & syððan fylgean teame, swa
Ða geræddan witan, þæt hit betere wære, þæt man æure tymde, ðær hit ærest befangen wære, swa longe
Swunce mare se, ðe þæt unriht gestreon on his handa stode, & læsse se ðe ðær ariht onspræce.
