Mod steoreð & receð clëne getreowum lichoman se rehta geleafa mid haetu walle facnes atur nyte
& crist us sie mete & drync ur sie geleafa bliðe dricen we geðungenlice drincennisse gastes Xpsque
Bliðe deg ðes leore clënnes sie ðæt on ærmargen se rehta geleafa swe swe midne deg degred mod
Degred ryne forðwegeð degred all forðypeð in feder all sunu & all in worde feder Aurora cursus
Wuldur ðe feder wuldur ðæm ancendan somud mid ðy halgan gaste in ðere ecan weoruldie Gloria tibi
God sceppend alra & heofenes reccere gerwende deg mid wlitige lehte rueht slepes gefe Ds creator
Liomu tolesde ðæt sie rest agefe gewinnes gewunan & mod woerigu gelihte & wopas onlese
Ðoncas gedoenu nu ða dege & naehte upcyme boene gehatum scyldge ðæt ðu gefultume humen
Ðæc heortan ða niðerlican hleoðriað ðec stefn smoeðu hlydeð ðec lufað clëne lufe ðec mod weorðað
& mit te grund biluceð deg dimnes naehta se rehta geleafa ðeostru nyte & naeht getreowum lihteð Et
Slepan mod ne let ðu slepan scyld cunne clæne geleafa coelende slepes smec gemetgie Dormire
Ongered gehygde glidderre ðec heortan ða hean slepað ðy læs fiondes ðes efestgan facne fyrhtu stille
Crist bidden we & feder cristes & feadur gast an maehtig ðorh all hirt biddende ðrines wuldur ðe
Cyning ece dryhten wisena sceppend alra ðu were ær weorulde aa mid feder sunu Rex aeterne dne
Ðu middangeardes in fruman gehiowadas mon ðæm ðinre onlicnisse ondwliotan saldes gelicne Qui
Ðone dioful biswac fiond mennesces cynnes ðes ðu hiow lichoman genioman gemeode ðu were
Ðæt mon ðu alesdes ðone ær soðlice gehiowades & usic to gode gegadrades ðoh flæsces gemænnisse
Ðone acennedne of fæmnan forhtað ylc sawul ðorh ðone usic arisan holde mode we gelefað Quem
Se us ðorh fulwiht forgefe forgefenisse we earun numene mid bendum gebundne mid ingehygde Qui
Ðu fore men onfon gemeodemad were saldes ðin blod ure haelu weorð Qui crucem propter hominem
Weotudlice wagrift ðes temples tosliten is & all eorðe cwaecade du monge slependra awehtes dryhten
Ðu fiondes ðes aldan megen ðorh rode deaðes fordrestende mid ðere usic gesegnade on foranheafdum
Ðu hine from us aa on weg adrifan ðu were gemeodemad ne æfre mege gedergan alesde mid ðine
Ðu fore us to helwearum astigan gemeodemad ðu were ðæt deaðes borggeldum lifes forgefe gefe
Ðe on margentid hymen woepende we singað forgef us dryhten forgef ondettendum Tibi matutino
