Heo genamen hine þa & hine gehæften.
Þa wearð mycel geflit betwux þan cristenan & þan hæðenan.

Jacobus soðlice wæs afylled mid þan Halgen Gaste, & heom to cwæð, Gehyreð nu broðre þa leofeste,
Ure Drihten behet Abrahame ure fæder þæt on his sæde byð yrfeweardod ealle þeoden.

Þa Judees cwædon, Hwæt is se þe ne gelefð on God?
Jacobus andswerode, Se þe ne gelefð þæt on Abrahames cynne byð geerfeweard ealle þeode, & se þe
Þiss he forewitegode þurh God sylfne & þurh his wissunge.
Eft Isaias forewytegode beo ures Drihtenes tocyme & cwæð, Efne mæden byð geeacnod on innoðe, &
Eft Jeremias cwæð, Eala þu Jerusalem, efne þin alesend cumð & þiss byð his tacne.
Blinde eagen he onlihteð, & deafen he forgyfð gehyrnysse, & mid his stefne he awecð deade to life.
Eft Ezechiel se wytega cwyðð, Eala þu Syon, þin king cumð & þe gestaðeleð.
Daniel soðlice cwæð, Swa swa mannes bearn God cumð to mannen, & he geahneð him ealdordomes &
& soðlice se Halga Gast clypode þurh Dauid & þuss cwæð, Min Drihten cwæð to mine Drihtene, þu
Eft fæder stæfne wæs gehyrd of heofone þuss cweðende, He gecegð me his fæder & ic hine asette ofer
To Dauiden sylfen God cwæð, Of wæstme þines innoðes ic sette ofer þinne hehsetle.
Beo his þrowunge soðlice Isaias se witega cwæð, Swa swa unscaðig lamb God wæs gelædd to slege.
Eft Dauid cwæð þurh þone Halgen Gast, Heo adrifen mine handen & mine fet & gerimdan ealle mine
Heo soðlice me ymbsceawodan & me beheoldan & todældan min reaf & hluten heom betwynen.
Eft on oðre stowe se witega cwæð beo ures Drihtenes þrowunge, Heo sænden on minne mete gealle &
Beo his deaðe soðlice is gecweðen, Min flæsc resteð on hyhte, for þan þe þu ne forlætest mine sawle on

Soðlice Isaias cwæð beo þan mycelen dome þe toweard is, þæt deade ariseð of heora byrigene, & byð
Eac Dauid se salmscop cwæð, þæt God hæfð þone mihte þæt he æighwylcen mænn forgelt beo his
Nu bidde ic leofa gebroðre, cwæð Sanctus Jacobus, þæt æighwylc eower dædbote do his synnen, þæt he
Se þe hine sylfne wat beo ænige dæle scyldigne, þæt he þæs þrowunge geyfelode þe ealne middeneard
For þan witodlice ne byð nane mænn dædbote alefd æfter þyssen life, ac hæfð æighwylc an swa swa he
Eornestlice Dauid cwæð þurh þone Halgen Gast beo Drihtene, þæt þa Judees agulden him yfel for gode,
Witodlice he arærde creoperes of heora cynne, þe alefede wæron, & hreoflen he geclænsode, blinde he
& eac þæt wæs forewitegod þurh Dauid hwu his agen discipul hine belæwde & to deaðe gesealde, þa he
Ealle þas þing, leofe gebroðre, Abrahames bearn foresædan þurh þone halgen Gast.
Hwæt wene þe nu gyf ge gelyfen nylleð on God, þæt ge mugen ætwinden þan ecen wite þonne oðre
Ic cweðe, þæt ge wel wurðe syn þæt ge gewitnode beon.
Ac ge ageð mycele þearfe, þæt ge dædbote don eower synnen mid teare gyten, & mid heofunge,
& heo mid lege forbærnde wurdon.
Þas þing & oðre gelice, Jacobus bodede þan folca swa lange, þæt God ælmihtig him getyðede swa
We biddeð eadmodlice nu, þæt þu tæce us dædbote to done.
Sanctus Jacobus heom andswerede, Eale ge leofe gebroðre, ne scule ge nateshwan eow forðæncen.
Gelyfeð on God & byð gefulhtnede, þonne byð ealle eower synnen adilogode.
