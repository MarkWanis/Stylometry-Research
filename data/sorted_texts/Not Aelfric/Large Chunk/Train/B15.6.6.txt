Her is on sio swutelung hu Ælfhelm his are & his æhta geuadod hæfð for Gode & for wurulde.
Þæt is þonne ærest his hlaforde an hund mancosa goldes & twa swurd & feorwer scyldas & feower speru
& he gean for his sawle þæs landes æt Wrættincge into sancte Æþeldryðe buton þam twam hydon þe
& ic gean þæs landes æt Brycandune into sancte Petre to Westmenstre buton ic wylle þæt man mæste
& ic gean Ælfgare minum suna þæs landes æt Hwipstede & þæs æt Wealtune his dæg & æfter his dæge
& ic cyþe hwæt ic minum wiue to morgengyue sealde, þæt is Beadewan & Burgestede & Strætford &
& ic gef hire þa wyt ærest togædere comon þa twa hyda æt Wilburgeham & æt Hrægenan & þæt þærto
& ic gean hire Carletunes & ic gean hire þæs heauodbotles æt Gyrstlingaþorpe & ealra þara æhta þe
& ic gean minum wiue & minre dehter healues þæs landes æt Cunningtune to gedale buton þam feower
& ic gean Ælfmære & his breðer Ælfstane þara twegra landa to gedale æt Hættanlea & æt Pottune
& ic gean Godere þæs þe ic æt Wimunde gebohte.
& ic gean Leofsige Lytlanbyrig æfter minum dæge on þæt gerad þe þæt stande þe wyt beforan þan
& ic gean him & his wiue þæs landes æt Stoctune wið an hund mancosa goldes & ic wylle þæt man
& ic gean minum þrym broþron to gedale þæs landes æt Trostingtune buton þam þe ic gean Alfwolde
& ic gean Ælfhelme þære hyde æt Icelingtune & þæs æt Mawyrþe & ic gean Wulfmære þæs þe ic æt
& ic gean minre scæðe for mire sawle into Hramesege, healfe þan abbode & healue þam hirede.
& ic gean minum wiue healues þæs stodes æt Trostingtune & minan geferan healues þe me mid ridað.
& fo min wif to healuan þe on wealde is & min dohter to healuan.
& ic wylle þæt min wif fo æfre to healfan æhtan on ælcon tune fo to lande se þe fo swa hio to forgyuen
Nu bydde ic þe leof hlaford þæt min cwyde standan mote & þæt þu ne geþauige þæt hine man mid wuo
God is min gewyta, ic wæs þinum fæder swa gehyrsum swa ic fyrmest myhte & fullice hold on mode &
Se man se þe minne cwyde wende buton þu hyt sy, leof, & ic hæbbe geleauan þæt þu nelle, God afyrre
& God & ealle his halgan gehealde æcne þara þe þærto gefyrþryge þæt he standan mote.
