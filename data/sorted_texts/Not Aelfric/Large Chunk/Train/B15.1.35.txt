Ðis is þara X hida boc æt Kenelmestun þe Eadgar cing gebocode Aþulfæ his þegne on ece yrfæ.
Æt Chenelmestune cum licentia Byrhtelmi episcopi et familiæ æcclesiæ in ea ratione ut reddat justiciam
Ðis synd þa land ge mæro to cenelmestune.
Ærest of þære hwitan flodan neah mælan beorge, þonon norð to yfer lea be byrht woldes mearce, to broc
Þurþ hormes wudu utt to þrim æcerun ford bæ þæ wuda to Æpshangran to þam hlince & swa bæ þære
Be norðan eallan forda to þam stapole, þonon east to mearc hlince.
And swa east be þæs bisceopes mearce be niþan cadan hangran to wulfreðes wyrðe.
Þonon to þorn hyrste niþer bæ þryfes dæne be byrhtswyþe mearce upp to sigbrihtes an stige.
Þonon west bæ þære ofer ðone heræpað to þære hwitan dic, wæst andlang dic, æft to þære hwitan
And VIII mæd aceras undær wanexan dune, þa ge byriad to cenelmestune.
And se haga on worte bynnan suþ ealla þæ to þam lande ge byred.
Ðis is þæs wudæs ge mæro bæ suþan melan beorge.
Ærest of þam æscstubbe æt hunlafinghammun suþ andlang mearce to lammære to þam hlidgeate þonon
Þonon norð andlang herpaðes.
Bæ byrht woldes mearce utt on þone feld forð be wyrhttruman æft on ðone æscstyb.
Byrhtelm bisceop & eall se hired on Wintanceastre on ealdum mynstre lætað þæt land æt Cenelmestune
Þonne beode ic on Godes naman & on sancte Petres & on ealra his haligra þæt nan man swa geðurstig
