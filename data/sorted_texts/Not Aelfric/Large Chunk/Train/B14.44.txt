Be ðam ðe beon bewitað: Beoceorle gebyreð, gif he gafolheorde healt, þæt he sylle ðonne lande geræd
Mid us is geræd, þæt he sylle V sustras huniges to gafole; on suman landum gebyreð mare gafolræden.
Eac he sceal hwiltidum geara beon on manegum weorcum to hlafordes willan, toeacan benyrðe &
& gyf he wel gelend bið, he sceal beon gehorsad, þæt he mæge to hlafordes seame þæt syllan oððe sylf
& fela ðinga swa gerad man sceal don; eal ic nu atellan ne mæig.
Ðonne him forðsið gebyrige, hede se hlaford ðæs he læfe, bute hwet friges sy.
Gafolswane: Gafolswane gebyreð, þæt he sylle his slyht, be ðam ðe on lande stent.
On manegum landum stent, þæt he sylle ælce geare XV swyn to sticunge, X ealde & V gynge hæbbe
Gyme eac swan, þæt he æfter sticunge his slyhtswyn wel behweorfe sæncge: ðonne bið he ful wel
Eac he sceal beon swa ic ær be beocere cwæð oftræde to gehwilcon weorce & gehorsad to hlafordes
Ðeow swan & ðeow beocere æfter forðsiðe be anre lage wyrðe.

Gyf we selre geleorniað, þæt we willað georne lufian & healdon, be ðære ðede ðeawe, ðe we ðænne
