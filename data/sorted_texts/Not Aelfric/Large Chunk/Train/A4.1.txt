Ne meahte se snella sunu Wonredes ealdum ceorle ondslyht giofan, ac he him on heafde helm ær
Let se hearda Higelaces þegn bradne mece, þa his broðor læg, eald sweord eotonisc, entiscne helm
Ða wæron monige þe his mæg wriðon, ricone arærdon, ða him gerymed wearð þæt hie wælstowe
Þenden reafode rinc oðerne, nam on Ongenðio irenbyrnan, heard swyrd hilted ond his helm somod,
He ðam frætwum feng ond him fægre gehet leana mid leodum, ond gelæste swa.
Geald þone guðræs Geata dryhten, Hreðles eafora, þa he to ham becom, Iofore ond Wulfe mid
Þæt ys sio fæhðo ond se feondscipe, wælnið wera, ðæs ðe ic wen hafo, þe us seceað to Sweona leoda,
Nu is ofost betost þæt we þeodcyning þær sceawian ond þone gebringan, þe us beagas geaf, on
Ne scel anes hwæt meltan mid þam modigan, ac þær is maðma hord, gold unrime grimme geceapod,
Þa sceall brond fretan, æled þeccean, nalles eorl wegan maððum to gemyndum, ne mægð scyne
Forðon sceall gar wesan monig, morgenceald, mundum bewunden, hæfen on handa, nalles hearpan
Swa se secg hwata secggende wæs laðra spella; he ne leag fela wyrda ne worda.
Weorod eall aras; eodon unbliðe under Earnanæs, wollenteare wundur sceawian.
Fundon ða on sande sawulleasne hlimbed healdan þone þe him hringas geaf ærran mælum; þa wæs
Ær hi þær gesegan syllicran wiht, wyrm on wonge wiðerræhtes þær laðne licgean; wæs se legdraca
Se wæs fiftiges fotgemearces lang on legere, lyftwynne heold nihtes hwilum, nyðer eft gewat dennes
Him big stodan bunan ond orcas, discas lagon ond dyre swyrd, omige, þurhetone, swa hie wið eorðan
Þonne wæs þæt yrfe, eacencræftig, iumonna gold galdre bewunden, þæt ðam hringsele hrinan ne
Þa wæs gesyne þæt se sið ne ðah þam ðe unrihte inne gehydde wræte under wealle.
Weard ær ofsloh feara sumne; þa sio fæhð gewearð gewrecen wraðlice.
Wundur hwar þonne eorl ellenrof ende gefere lifgesceafta, þonne leng ne mæg mon mid his magum
Swa wæs Biowulfe, þa he biorges weard sohte, searoniðas; seolfa ne cuðe þurh hwæt his worulde
