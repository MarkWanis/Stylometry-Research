Ac se papa undergeat and ealle þa þe þis miht cuðen þæt he hit dyde þurh his drihtnes willan.
Eac æfter þisum he begeat earfoðlice geleafan æt þan papan þæt he moste faran ham to his mynstre
Hi betæhton þa heora lif and heora sawle þam ælmihtigum gode and sealde heom betweoxan sibbecos
Ða gewænde se godes deorlincg egidius ut of rome ham to his mynstre weard and eal swa he com to
He gewænde þa fram þære burh mid bliðum mode and eal swa hraðe swa he wæs gecumen ham to his
Ða se eadige egidius þis geherde he wearð þa swiðe bliðe and his drihten þæs miccla þancunge dyde
Se godes man eode þa to þær æstaðe þær se haligdom wæs up gelænt and let hine bringen into his
Drihten wolde þa geglængen his cniht mid cynehelme and forgeldan him his micclan geswinc forþon
And he hit gecydde þa sume his gastlice gebroðre eadmodlice biddende þæt hi for hine gebædon and
He befadode þa swiðe arwurðlice eal þæs mynstres þincg and gewissode his gebroðra mid mycelre
Ða com se time þæt se eadige egidius sceolde underfangen his mede æt criste þæt wæs on kalendas
He geændode his swincfulle lif and drihten betæhte his sawle and sancte michael and his ængla wyrd
