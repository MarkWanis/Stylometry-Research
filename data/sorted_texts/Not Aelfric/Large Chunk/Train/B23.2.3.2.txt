Ða ealdan læces gesetton on ledonbocum, þæt on ælcum monðe beoð æfre twegen dagas, þa syndon
Þæs cunnede sume læce and let his horse blod on þære tide, and hit læg sona dead.
Nu syndon hit þas dagas, swaswa hit her onsegð.
Se forma dæg on Martio, þæt is on Hlydanmonðe and se feorða dæg ær his ende.
On þam oðrum monðe, þe we Aprelis hatað, se teoða dæg is derigendlic and se ændlyfte ær his ende.
On Maiusmonðe se þridda dæg is derigendlic and se seofoða ær his ende.
On Iuniusmonðe se X dæg and ær his ende se XV.
On Iuliusmonðe se XIII dæg and ær his ende se X.
On Agustusmonðe se I dæg and ær his ende se II.
On Septembermonðe se III dæg and ær his ende se X.
On Octobermonðe se III dæg and ær his ende se X.
On Nouembermonðe se V dæg and ær his ende se III.
On Decembermonðe se VII dæg and ær his ende se X.
On Ianuariusmonðe se I dæg and ær his ende se VII.
On Februariusmonðe se IIII dæg and ær his ende se þridda.
We gesetton on foreweardan þissere endebyrdnesse þone monað Martius, þe menn hatað Hlyda, forþam
Nu eft be þam monan is mycclum to warnienne, þæt man on IIII nihta ealdne monan oþþe on V nihta
Ac we gehyrdon seggon sumne þisne mann, þæt nan mann ne leofode, þe him blod lete on ealra
