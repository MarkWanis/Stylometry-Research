To eallum folce.
Eala leofan men, understandað, þæt soð is, hit wæs nu fela geara, þæt on þisse þeode rixode unrihta fela,
Forðam ær þisum wæs gehwar swicdom swiðra, þonne wisdom, and þuhte hwilum wisost, se þe wæs
Ne bearh nu for oft gesib þam sibban þe ma, þe fremdan, ne fæder his bearne ne hwilum bearn his
And þi is þisse ðeode fela hearma onsæge.
Ne dohte hit ær þisum inne ne ute, ac wæs here and hungor, bryne and blodgite on gewelhwilcan ende
Forþam on þisum earde wæs, swa hit þincan mæg, unrihta fela and tealte getriwða æghwar mid
Fela syn forsworene and swiðe forlogene and wed eac abrocene oft and gelome; and þæt is gesyne on
Ac uton don, swa us þearf is, beon ymbe þa bote, geswican unrihtes and gebugan to rihte, þonne
Uton helpan ure silfra þa hwile, þe we magon and motan, þi læs we forwurðan, þonne we læst wenan.
La leofan men, hwæt, we ful georne witan, þæt nu fela geara men na ne rohton for oft, hwæt hi
Ac wearð þes ðeodscipe swiðe forsingod þurh morðdæda and þurh mandæda, þurh stala and ðurh
And eac syn þurh aðbricas and þurh wedbricas forloren and forlogen ma, þonne scolde.
