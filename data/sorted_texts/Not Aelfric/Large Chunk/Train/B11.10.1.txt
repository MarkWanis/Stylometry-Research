Ðys syndon þa godcundan bebodu þe we scylon healdan.
Ðæt ys ærest, seo soðe lufu Godes & manna & clænnysse & fæsten & soðfæstnysse & beon eaðmode &
And þas þing we scylon forgan, þæt ys, oferhyd & gytsunge & æfest & ydelne gylp & stala & reaflac &
