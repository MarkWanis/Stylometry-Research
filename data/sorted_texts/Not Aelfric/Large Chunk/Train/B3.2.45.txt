In die ascensionis domini.
Men ða leofestan, us is micclum to wuldrienne and to geblissigenne on þysum dæge for ures drihtnes
On þysum andweardan dæge se ælmihtiga cyning and ealles middaneardes alysend and ælces siges sige
And syððan he of deaðe aras on þysum halgum feowertigum dagum he getrymmede þæra apostola
And he hi asende geond ealne middangeard þæt hi sceoldon fullian ealle þeoda on ðæs ecan fæder and
And he æt mid him her on life and bebead him þæt hi ne gewiton fram hierusalem þære mæran byrig ac
He sæde him and þus cwæð, geanbidiað nu on hierusalem sume hwile ealle ætgædere for ðam ðe
And þa þa he him þis sæde þa wearð he up ahafen him geseondum and hine onfeng beorht wolcn fram
And þa ða hi beheoldon þa lyfte þa stodon þær twegen weras þa wæron ymbscrydde mid beorhtum
Soðlice þes hælend þe fram eow genumen is and gebroht is on his fæder swyðran healfe mid swa
Þonne is us eallum þylcum wordum him to toclypigenne þus cweþende, eala þu geworhtest heofones
And þu ðe gesceope þone forman mann adam þone þe deofol beswac mid facenfullum swicdome and
And he eft to neorxnawanges gefean wearð gelædd þe he ær of wearð adrifen.
We biddað þe nu ða ælmihtig drihten þæt þu for þinum mæran upstige us ece ræste forgife þonne þu
Men ða leofestan, us gedafenað eac þæt we symble wuldrien and geblissien for ðam ðe hit is gerædd on
Eac swilce we gelyfað þæt he þanon cume on wuldre his mægenþrymmes mid englum and heahenglum
And þæt we sceolon witan and ongytan þæt ær drihtnes þrowunge ealra haligra manna sawla wæron
Ealle drihten on þam mæran dæge his upstiges þe we nu todæg wurðiað he mid him to heofonum
And þonne þa godan of þysum life ferað þonne gelæt hi engla werod on þa heofonlican ricu þæt hi þær
Swa þæra synfulra manna sawla on helle beoð under ege asette and geanbidiað ærist heora lichomena
Men ða leofestan, uton us nu warnian þæt we þonne ne cumon forhte toforan drihtnes domsetle for
Uton besceawian hwæt se mæra godes þegen sæde petrus.
Eala ge leofestan menn, he cwæð, uton ongitan þæt crist for us þrowode.
And he us lærde þa bysne þæt we sceoldon filigan his bebodum and larum and weorcum se þe næfre
Ac he sealde hyne sylfne swiðe bylihwytlice þam ðe hine on unriht demdon.
He cwæð eftsona petrus se gecorena drihtnes þegen and ealdor ealra þæra leorningcnihta þe drihtne
Uton witan, he cwæð, þæt drihten us forlæt þa bysna manigra geswinca næs nanra ræsta and teonena
Men ða leofestan, us is micclum þes dæg to mærsigenne and to wuldrigenne for ðam þe manega
Þæs hælendes leorningcnihtas gesawon on þysum dæge ane lange hlædre betweonan heofonum and
And hi gesawon englas ægðer ge up ge nyðer on ðære hlæddre faran on hwitum gegyrelan and þus
Swa he cymð eft swa swa ge gesawon hine nu faran on heofonas.
Ac gað to hierusalem and wuniað þær oð ðæt eow cume se frefrigenda gast fram ælmihtigum gode se
And eft hi gesawon godes sunu standan ætforan þam healican weofde on heora heannyssum and hi
And eft hi gesawon synfulra manna sawla on witum earmlice fram deoflum gewriðene.
Ealle þas þing, men ða leofestan, him wæron ætywede to ðam þæt hi sceoldon us fore warnian and
And uton na forgytan þæt we symle sceolon gewilnian þa heofonlican and uton us warnian wið ða
On þam is dæghwamlice ece sargung and unrotnys buton blisse and teara genihtsumnyss buton frofre
Sume þær hangiað be þam fotum þæs þe us halige gewritu onwrigen habbað and sume þær hangiað be
And sum þær beoð besenced oð ða cneow on þam hatan pice and on ðam fyre and sum þær bið
Men ða leofestan, uton geþencan þa þing þe rihte synt on ðam bið gegladod god and uton don þæt
And uton beon geæmtigode fram leahtrum and uton beon gefyllede of mægenum.
And uton gewilnian symle þæt we gegyrnon mid ealre hrædnysse þæt heofonlice rice þær se healica
Þær is ece med and þær is heofonlic mete, þær is ece myrhþ and rice buton awendednysse.
Men ða leofestan, us gedafenað þæt we forhicgen þisne middaneard þæt we magon filigan criste urum
And uton warnian þæt we ne forspillon þæt ece lif for þyses middaneardes idelan wuldre ac uton
Se is reccend heofones and eorðan and ealra gesceafta, þone heriað englas and heahenglas, þone
For ðam ðe he is gefyllednys and fulfremednys ealra haligra and hi symle blissiað on heofonum
Eala men ða leofestan, uton geþencan þa heofonlican god and hi geearnian on urum life and uton
Hwæt, he us eallum þone wurðmynt geann þe he his apostolum geuðe and him to cwæð, swa swa min
Wuniað on minre lufe and ge beoð mine frynd gyf ge þa þing doð þe ic eow bebeode.
Eala micel weorðscipe is hit þam nu þe he þa to cwæð, swa hwæt swa ge on minum naman minne

Eala micel wurðmynt ys þam gelyfendum þe godes bearn big sæde.
Eala fæder, he cwæð, þa ðe þu me sealdest, ic wille þæt hi beon þær þær ic beo þæt hi geseon mine
Micel wurðmynt is nu þam ðe se hælend sealde gesibsume cossas þus cwæðende, ic læte to eow sibbe

Eala micel wurðmynt is nu þam ðe he sylf to cwæð, ic astige to minum fæder and to eowrum fæder
And þa ða he þas þing sæde þa wearð he up ahafen on beorhtum genipe him geseondum and he wæs
And þæt genip hine underfeng fram heora eagum and hi stodon and beheoldon þa lyfta.
And hi þa clypedon anre stefne þus cwæþende, eala wuldres cyning drihten mægna, þu ðe nu todæg
Ac asend to us þines fæder behat, þæt is soðfæstnysse gast, þæt he wunige symle on ecnysse mid us
Uton nu men ða leofestan, beon gemyndige urra sawla þearfa and uton wyrcan god on ðam ðe we
Men ða leofestan, uton we nu geornlice geþencan for ðam on urum mode þæt se towearda and se
Gefæstnigen we þonne urne hiht on þone ecan eþel and þa innunge ures modes gestaðelian we on ðam
Nu we gehyrdon secgan, men ða leofestan, hu ure drihten on þysum andweardan dæge on heofonas
