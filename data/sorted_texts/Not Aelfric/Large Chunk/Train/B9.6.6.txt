& he æfter ðære ðreaunge ðe geclænsodra to his ðegnunge eft hwerf, þe he ær wel &
Swylce eac ða gegyrelan & ða hrægl, ðam ðe Gode ðone gehalgodan lichoman Cuðberhtes oðþe
Nis ðæt eac swylce to forswugienne, ðætte nu ær þrim gearum þurh his reliquias geworden wæs,
Wæs ðis gedon in ðæm mynstre, þe big Docore ðære ea getimbred is, & from ðære ea noman
Wæs in ðæm mynstre sum geong monn, ðam unwlitig swylce & atolic his eagan breg wyrde &
& se dæghwamlice wæs weaxende, & ðæs eagan forwyrde tobeotode.
Teoleden his læcas & ðone swylce mid sealfum & mid beþinge geðwænan woldon; ac hie ne
Sume lærdon ðæt hine mon aweg acurfe; sume þæt beweredon for maran frecennesse.
Mid þy ða se foresprecena broðor langere tide ðyllic ungescræpo woon, ne ða tobeotiendan
Forðon ða þa broðor his lichoman æfter manegum gearum his bebyrgednesse ungewemmedne
Ðyssa reliquia dæl in ða tid mid him hæfde sum mæssepreost þæs ilcan mynstres, ðæs noma
Ða wæs he sume dæg in ða cyrican gongende & ða cyste ontynde þara reliquia, þæt he heora
Mid þy he ða se mæssepreost ðone dæl, þe he wolde, his freonde sealde, ða sealde he þæt oðer
Ða he ða þæm feaxe onfeng þæs halgan heafdes, ða wæs he mid halwendre onbryrdnesse
Þa he ðæt gedon hæfde, þa sette he þa reliquias in heora cyste, swa he haten wæs, ond gelefde
Ne hine owiht his geleafa wægde.
Wæs ða seo æftere tid dæges, swa he sylf sægde, ða he þæt dyde; ond þa dyde forð þone dæg,
