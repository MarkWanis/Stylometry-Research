On drihtnes naman ælmihtiges Godes.
Ic æðelred mid Godes gyfe Angelðeode cyning, and wealdend eac oðra iglanda ðe her abutan licgað,
Ic eac ðurh Godes gyfe mine þearfe of ðære godcundan lare and bodunge geornlice understandan wylle,
Eac betwux minum andgyte ic geseo and soþlice understande ðæt þes ðeodscype ðurh preosta
Sæde ic minum witun ðe me for Gode and for worulde rædað þæs him God andgyt sylð mines modes
Cwædon hi ða ðæt hit ðurh Godes fultum betere wære ðæt ic ða preostas of Cristes cyrcean for hiora
Ic ða swa dyde, on Cristes cyrcean munecas gesette of ðære bysne ðe sanctus Augustinus hider
Ðæt wæs ðæt he on Cristes cyrcean be sanctus Gregorius hæse and ðæs mæran cinges geðeahte and
And ða munecas syððan þurh hira haligan drohtnunga to biscopan gewurdan ðære cyrcean landspede.
And ðæra muneca ðe ðær niwon inne gelogode synd ecelice on ðisum sunderfreolse ic gestrangige.
And ðara tuna her on naman awrite þe of ealdum dagum and fram me Angelcinge, and eac of oðrum

Butan þysum þrim ðingum, þæt is, fyrdfærelde, and brigcgewurce, and burhbote.
Gyf hwylc welwillendra manna ðysne minne sunderfreols mid godum ðingum geeacnian wylle,
Gyf ðonne hwylc yfel man of deofle onæled sy ðæt he ðisne minne sunderfreols gewanige oððe
Ðonne hit soðlice betere is, ðæt man wið synne warnige ðonne hi man geswincfullice bete.
