ÆLFRIC GRET EADMODLICE ÆÐELWERD EALDORMAN and ic secge þe leof, þæt ic hæbbe nu
Þu wast leof þæt we awendon on þam twam ærrum bocum þæra halgena þrowunga and lif, þe angelcynn
Nu gewearð us þæt we þas boc be þæra halgena ðrowungum and life, gedihton þe mynstermenn mid
Ne secge we nan þincg niwes on þissere gesetnysse, forþan ðe hit stod gefyrn awriten on ledenbocum
Nelle we eac mid leasungum þyllic liccetan, forþan þe geleaffulle fæderas and halige lareowas hit
Sum witega clypode þurh þone halgan gast and cwæð, Mirabilis deus in sanctis suis, et cet: Wundorlic
We awritað fela wundra on þissere bec, forþan þe god is wundorlic on his halgum swa swa we ær
An woruldcynincg hæfð fela þegna and mislice wicneras; he ne mæg beon wurðful cynincg buton he
