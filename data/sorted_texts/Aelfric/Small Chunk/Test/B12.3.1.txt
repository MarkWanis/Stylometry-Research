Se læssa creda.
Ic gelyfe on God, Fæder ælmihtigne, Scyppend heofenan and eorðan.
And ic gelyfe on Hælend Crist, his ancennedan Sunu, urne Drihten, se wæs geeacnod of ðam Halgan
He wæs dead and bebyrged, and he niðerastah to helle.
And he aras of deaðe on ðam ðriddan dæge, and he astah up to heofonum, and sitt nu æt swiðran Godes
And ic gelyfe on ðone Halgan Gast, and ða halgan gelaðunge, and halgena gemænnyssæ, and flæsces
Sy hit swa.
