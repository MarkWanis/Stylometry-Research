Ðis is seo socn into Scyreburna mid folcrihte, twa dæl of Cauda & Wicstow eal & ufer Seleby eal & twa
& eall Luteringtun & eall Hehferðehegðe & eall Hudelestun.
On Scireburnan toecan þam inlande syndan IIII hida weorclandes & on Luteringatune III hida & on
& on Wicstowe twegea oxnagang & on Cawuda twa dæl þæs landes is agenland into Scireburnan &
Into Ottanleage IIII plogaland & on Bægeltune II, on Hafecesweorðe II, on oðeran Hafecesweorðe II,
Þis is unbesacen agenland.
& þærto eacan hyrað þas socnland into Ottanleage, on Ottanleage II ploh & on Bældune II & on
Æt Rypum ærest milegemet on ælce healfe.
& Biscoptun is in on þam II hida & Carlewic V hida & healf Munecatun his agenland feorðe healf hide
On Herelesho healf hid, on Stodlege III hida, on Suðtune oþer healf hide, on Nunnewic III hida, on
& þys synd weste land, an is Sallege, oðer is Grantelege, þridde is Efestun & feorðe is Wifeleshealh &
Þonne syndan þis preostaland, on Westwic IIII hida, on Norð Stanlege IIII, on Gyðingdale I hide, on
Ðis syndan socnland into Rypum, on Gyþingadeal VIII hida & ofer eall Munecatun VII hida & on
& on Suðtune oðer healf hide, on nyrran Stanlege V hida & on Norð Stanlege I hide & on Nunnewic I
