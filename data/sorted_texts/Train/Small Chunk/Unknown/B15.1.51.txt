Æþelred cynig gret ælfric ealdorman & Wulmær & æþelweard & ealle þa þegenas on Hamtunscire
& ic cyþe þe & eow eallum þæt ælfheah biscop sende to me þæs landes boc æt Ciltancumbe & ic hi let
Þa licode me swyðe wel seo gesetnesse & seo ælmesse þe minne yldran on angunne cristendomes into
