Primitus ad Dumoltan eastan on is esingeburna, ðonne suþan of esingburnan westwardes ondlong dices
Ðæt swa ondlong scæð broces in gemare dic, ðæt swa ondlong in secgmere, ðæt swa of secgmere east
Ðonne sind ðis ða landgemæru æt pid wellan: of pid wellan in crundelu, of crundelum suð ryhte ondlong
