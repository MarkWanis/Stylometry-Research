Þat is þat ic an þat lond at Eskeresthorp into seint Eadmund buten ten acres ic giue þer into þere kirke.
And Lefric habbe þo þre acres þe he on sit.
And ic an þat lond at Lithle Meddeltone into sancte Benedicte & ten acres into þe kirke.
