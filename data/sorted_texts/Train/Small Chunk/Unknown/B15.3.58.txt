Her swutelað on ðissum gewrite þæt Ealdred biscop hæfþ geunnen Wulfgeate sumne dæl landes.
Þæt is oþer healf hid on ðam tune ðe Dicford is genamad to habbanne & to brucane ðreora manna dæg &
& hig syn æfre underþeodde & gehersume & ðam hlafordscipe folhgien ðe ðonne bisceop beo.
& gif hig ænigne frambyge don ðolian ðære are.
& ðisses is to gewitnysse, eall se hired on Wigeraceastre & se hired on Eofeshamme & se hired on
& hredde he ða oðra healfe hida for are ðreora manna dæg to cinges banne.
