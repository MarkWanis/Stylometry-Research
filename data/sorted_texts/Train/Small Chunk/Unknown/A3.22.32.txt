Is þes middangeard missenlicum wisum gewlitegad, wrættum gefrætwad.
Siþum sellic ic seah searo hweorfan, grindan wið greote, giellende faran.
Næfde sellicu wiht syne ne folme, exle ne earmas; sceal on anum fet searoceap swifan, swiþe feran,
Hæfde fela ribba; muð wæs on middan.
Moncynne nyt, fereð foddurwelan, folcscipe dreogeð, wist in wigeð, ond werum gieldeð gaful geara
Rece, gif þu cunne, wis worda gleaw, hwæt sio wiht sie.
