Ðis syndon þa land gemæro to madan leage ærest on witena leage in eardel of eardele in wriman ford of
Of þan bæce on þone hege of þon hege on wilburge wege of þæm wege in cærsihtan wyll of þæm wylle
Of wierdes forda on þone hreodihtan mor of þon more in þa hæþihtan lege of þære lege in þa hyrste on
Þis is þara III hida boc þe Eadgar cing gebocode Aþelwolde bisceope on ece yrfe æt Madan liege.
