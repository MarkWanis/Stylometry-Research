Þis sand þes landes i mare at Stoke up anlang ninge burne oð ðat hrisc lad.
An lang þese richtledes on þane ealde treo stede.
Þanan an lang weies on stanforde.
Þanen an langstreame on botenwelle.
And of boten welle on prestes Setel.
Þanen anlang weges on Wiuelesford.
Þanen up an lang mercdene onne stokebroc.
Þanen anlang stocbroc on hringheburne þar iuarest on feng.
