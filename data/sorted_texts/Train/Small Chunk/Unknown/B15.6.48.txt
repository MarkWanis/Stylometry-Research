Hyer swuteleð on þesen ywrithe hu Wolgiþ yan hire þing efter hire forthsiþe þe hire se almiyti God yuþe
Þet is þanne herest mine louerde his riyte heriet.
And ic yan þet land at Stistede a Godes ywithnesse and mine vrenden into Xristes cheriche þa muneken
Ad ic yan into þare cheriche at Stistede to þan þe ic on lyue yuþe Eldemes land and þertohycken þet þer
And ic yan Wulkitele and Kytele mine sunes þet land at Walsingham and at Karltune and Herlingham
And ic yan Ealgiþe mine doyter þet land at Cheartekere and at Essetesford and þane wde þe ic leyde
And ic Godwine eorle and Harold erle Friþetune.
And ic yan into Xristes cheriche to Xristes weuede ane litlene geldene rode and ane setrayel.
And ic yan seinte Eadmunde tueyn yboned hornes.
And ic yan Seynte Eþeldrithe ane wellene kertel.
And ic yan seynte Osithe half pund fees and þe yeue mine barnes.
And ic yan seynt Austine an regrayel.
And se þe mine quyde beryaui þe ic nu biqueþen habbe a Godes ywithnesse, beriaued he worþe þises
Þisses is to ywithnesse Eadward king and manie oþre.
