Þis wæs gedon ymbe IX hund wintra & IX & sixtig on þy Xan geare þæs þe Oswald bisceop to folgoþe
Sanctae Mariae & sanctus Michahel cum sancto Petro & allum Godes halgum gemiltien þis haldendum.
Gief hwa buton gewrihtum hit abrecan wille, hæbbe him wið God gemæne buton he to dædbote gecyrre
Ðis synd þa land gemære.
To hwitan hlince of portan mære ondlong sices innan þone suð broc andlang broces innan ceolmes
Her is seo hond seten.
