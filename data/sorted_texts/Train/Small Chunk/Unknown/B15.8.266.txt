Ðis sind þara XIII hide land gemæra þe to fif hidum ge byriad ærest on ydeles ige.
Of ydeles ige andlang ge mæres to þat teles wege andlang weges on mearan mores heafod andlang
Of þære scortan dic on ælfðryþe dic.
Andlang dic on holan broc andlang holan broces on wasan.
Andlang wasan eft to ydeles ige.
Þonne is þæs landes ealles XIII healf hid.
