Ðis is þara xxx hida boc to Enedforda, & þara x æt Ceolboldincgtune, & þara x æt Æscmæres wyrðe
Ðis syndon þara xxx hida ge mæra to Enedforda.
Ærest of afenan east staðæ upp on ða dic to þam weallum, of þam weallon andlang hærpaðæs in to
Of ðam hærpaðæ andlang furh on dolh crundæl, of dolh crundæle forð andlang wæges on ðone æþenan
Of igean seaþæ on þone greatan hling, of þam hlince andlang drafæ on þonæ hlinc æt wad dænæ, of
Of þam rugan beorge andlang furh to amwican, of amwican to blacan dænæ, of blacan dænæ andlang
Of þam forda &lanc streames, on þonæ bradan igeoð, of þam igeoðe andlang streames to þære ealdan
Of þam rugan slede on þone flit garan, of þam flit garan on fitelan sladæs crundæl, of þam crundæle
Of þam ealdan lagan to æscdæne of ascdæne andlang þæs smalan weges to rodmundes dæne on þæs
Of þam garan &lang þære ealdan dic on þonæ wide geat, of ðam widan geate forð to þam heafod
