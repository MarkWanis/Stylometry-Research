Ærest on leontan þ cume on blacan mere.
Þonne þ cume in ða geapan linde.
Þonon þ cume on lindwyrðe.
Swa þ cume on ciondan.
Of ceondan þ cume on reodmore.
Þonon þ cume on þa greatan ac.
Þonon þ cume in ða readan sole.
Þonne þ cume on cærspytt.
Swa þ cume in usan mere.
Of usan mere.
Þ cume eft on leontan.
