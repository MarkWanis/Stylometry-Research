Gyf ðe ðynce, þæt ðine fynd þwyrlice ymbe þe ðrydian, ðonne gang þu on gelimplicere stowe and þe ða
Sing ðonne þas salmas oð ende: Domine deus meus, in te speraui, Vsque quo, domine, Exaudi, domine
Stand þonne up sume hwile and sing ðisne sealm oð ende aþenedum earmum: Domine, quid
Sing þisne salm oð ende and sing hine aþenedum earmum swa oft, swa ðu oftost mæge: Ad te domine,
And wyrc swyþe gelome Cristes rode tacen on ðinum heafde and cweð þis gelome: Ecce crucem
