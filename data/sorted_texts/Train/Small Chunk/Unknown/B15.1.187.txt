Willem King gret Willem biscop and Swein scirefen and alle mine thegnes on Estsexen frendlice.
And icc kithe eow that icc wille that tha twa land, Feringe and Wokindon, the ic lede into Westminster
And Sweyn scirefa betace tha land into than halagen Minstre habbe se the hi habbe, and icc beode thatt
And icc nelle se thafian that mann atbrede them halagen minstre any thare thnge thas the icc thider inne
