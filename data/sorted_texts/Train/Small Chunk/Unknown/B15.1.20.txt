Ðis is þe quide þat Alured king ian into Sceaftesburi Gode to loue and seint Marie and alre Godes
Þæt is an hund hide mid mete and mid manne al so it stant and mine dochte Agelyue forð mid þare erie
And þis sent þare landiname þe ic þider iunnen habbe.
Þat is at Dunheued and at Cumtune XL hide and at Hanlee and Gissic XX hide and at Terente X hide &
And þis is to witnesse, Adward mine sune and Aþered arcebiscop and Alcheferd bissop and Adelheach
