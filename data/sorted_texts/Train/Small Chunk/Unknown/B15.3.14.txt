Hindehlyp, Ælfild & Cynelm & Æþelgerd.
Ic Oswald bisceop þurh Godes giefe mid geþafunge & leafe Eadgares Angulcyningces & Ælfheres
& æfter hiere dæge twam yrfeweardum & æfter heora forðsiþe to þære halgan stowe into
Si hit ælces þinges freoh butan ferdfare & walgeworc & brycgeworc & circanlade.
Þis wæs gedon ymbe nigon hund wintra & seox & seoxtig þæs þe Drihtnes gebyrdtide wæs on þy
Sancta Maria & sanctus Michahel cum sancto Petro & eallum Godes halgum gemiltien þis haldendum.
Gif hwa buton gewrihtum hit abrecan wille God hine to rihtere bote gecerre.
Her is seo hondseten Oswaldes biscopes.
