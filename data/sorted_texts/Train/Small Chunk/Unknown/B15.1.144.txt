Eadward kincg grett Willelm biscop & Harold eorl & Esgar stealre & ealle mine þegnas & mine holde
Ic kyþe eow þæt icc wille & icc ann þæt Sancte Peter & þa gebroðra on Westmynstre habben to heora
And icc ann heom eft ealswa þæt hy habben þær to sake & sokne, toll & team, infangeneðeof &
For þan icc nelle nateshwon geþafian þæt ænig man ætbrede oððe geutige mine gyfe & mine ælmesse
& icc wille & fæstlice bebeode þæt þeos mundbyrdnesse beo strang & staþelfæst in to þære halgan
God eow ealle gehealda.
