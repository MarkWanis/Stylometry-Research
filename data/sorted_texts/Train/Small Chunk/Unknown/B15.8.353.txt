Erest at sunemannes wyrðige on of þam wyrðige on gerihte on þa heafud stoccas.
Of þan heafod stoccum on þone rugan hlinc niþewearde.
Þonon on gerihte on bryxstanes garan suþeweardan of þam garan on duddes dene.
Of þere dene on þone ealdan garan suðewearden.
Þonon on ge rihte on þa bradan stanas of þan stanun on hodan hlæw.
Of þan hlæwe on hremnes byrig westwardon of hremnes byrig on lea cumb.
Þonon rihte andlang streames þ on tealeburnan.
Andlang tealeburnan þ on þa riþe.
Andlang riðe on þone þorn stub on bulan dic.
Andlang dices on þæs cinges scypena.
Of þan scypenum on þ riscbed.
Of þan riscbedde on þæs cinges þornas.
Þonon on gerihte on þ bean furlang.
Þonon on snelles ufeweardan.
Of snelles lince.
Þ eft on sunemannes weorðig.
And þær hyrð in an hyrde wic æt bada lacing & an hid landes & XII æceras mæde & an miln æt hyrde
And XII æceres landes.
