Her is geswutelod on þisum gewrite hu Aþelwold bisceop begeat æt his leofan cynehlaforde Eadgare
& he geuðe þæt man þæt land on eallum þingon for ane hide werode swa swa his yldran hit ær gesetton
& he bead on Godes naman þæt naðer ne þære stowe bisceop ne nanes bisceopes æftergenga þæt land
& he bead þurh Godes ælmichtiges myclan mægenþrymm þæt nan his bearna ne nan heora
Ac þæt hit efre mid munecan stode swa swa he hit mid Godes ælmilhtiges fultume gesette þa þa he hit
Ealles þæs landes is an hund hida ac þa godan cynegas & þa wisan ælc æfter oþran þæt ylce land swa
Se þe þysne freols healdan wille God ælmihtig hine gehealde her & on ecnesse.
Gif hwa þonne þurh ænige dyrstignesse oððe þurh deofles lare þisne freols abrecan wille oððe þas
& sy he ascyred fram ures Drichtnes gemanan & ealra his halgana & sy he gebunden þa hwile þe he
& byrne he on þan ecan fyre mid deofle & his englan a butan ælcan ende butan he hit ær his forðsiðe
