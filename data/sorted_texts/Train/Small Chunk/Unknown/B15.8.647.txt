Ðis synd þa landgemæro þæs landes þe eadwig cyning ageaf þam halgan were æt bemtune, & þam
Ærest þær ceoman lace ut scyt, andlang ceoman lace þæt hit cymeð on temese, anlang temese westward
Þonne went hit on ða norðeran ea, þæt hit cymeð to anre lace betwix berhtulfing yge, & hrisyge þæt on
Þæt hit cymeð eft to ceoman lace, & þæt land æt cyngbrycge norð andlang broces þæt hit cymð þær
Andlang weges to fulan broces heafdon, andlang broces on þa stanbricge, of þære stanbricge andlang
Andlang dyc on þone broc, andlang broces eft on cyngbricge, a onecnisse into sancte petres mynstre on
Þis synd þa langemæro þære anre hyde & þære oðerhealfan gyrde æt holacumbe þe læg into doflisc þe
Ærest on crampansteort, of crampansteorte on floraheafdo & of floraheafdo up on þone weg be westa
Of þam ferngara to þam dunnastane, of ðam dunnastane up to þære dyc, & swa east andlang þære dyc
