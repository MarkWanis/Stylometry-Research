Ðis synd þa land gemære æt reodemære leage.
Of sealter forda ondlong glences þ hit cymð þær riþig mæres broc sceot of glencing.
Þonan riht to ceolan heafdan, of ceolon heafdon to longan læge.
Of longan læge to bruttan geate, of bruttan geate to wærlafes dune.
Suþe weardre of wærlafes dune on miclan feld, of miclan feldan on wen broc.
Ondlong wen broces þæt hit cymð to þan mære, on efen cron mere þonon ondlong þæs ealdan gemæres,
Þonne ondlong glencing eft on salter forda.
