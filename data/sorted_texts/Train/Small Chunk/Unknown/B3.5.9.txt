De Inclusis.
Sum deofelgast sæde sumen ancre eall helle geryn and hire tintrege.
He sæde þæt þes middaneard nære na mare dryges landes togeanes þan garsegen, þonne man precode
Eft cwæþ se deofel to þam ancre: Gif ænig man beoð ane niht on helle and beoð eft þonen ofalæd, and
And þa belies blawen, and þa hameres beatan, and se lieg arise.
For ealle þisen broche ne arist seo werige sawle, for þære pine þe hi on helle ane niht þolede.
Georne cunnie ælc man to befleonne hellewite, forþon hit is þærinne sarlic to wunigenne.
Se awergede gast sæde þan ancre þæt he ne mehte all asecgen þa pine þe þa earme sawle þoliað on
Þurh deofles swicdom and Adames gemeleaste we wæren æt frumþe ut of Paradis ascofene, and on þas
Þærfore nabbe we her nane fæstlice stede ne langsume æþele, ac we gelomlice geherað and
Beþænce se man hine, þe him selfes rechð, forþon se man þe hefenerice secen wile, he hit sceal secan
