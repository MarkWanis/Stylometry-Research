Edward king gret Willem biscop & Leofstan & Alfsi porterefen & ealle mine burgþeygnes on Londone
& ic kyðe eow þæt ic habbe geunnen Sancte Petre þam halegen apostle in to Westmunstre fulne fridom
& gif eni man wille þa halegan stowe heonen forð mid lande & mid loge godian icc ann ealswa þæt hit
& ic nelle geþafian þæt enig mann habbe enigne onsting ofer his land ne ofer his menn ne be strandde
& se þe þeas munstres fridom & þas halges wurðscipe ofer þis geeacnie geeacnie God his lif on þissere
& se þe þes hallges wrðscipe & þes munstres fridom & þene broðru note gewani God & Godes moder
