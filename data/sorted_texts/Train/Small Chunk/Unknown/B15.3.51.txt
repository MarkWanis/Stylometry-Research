Her cyð on þysum gewrite þa forewearde þe Ælfwine bysceop & se hyred on ealdan mynstre worton wið
Þæt wæs þæt Osgod bruce þæs landes æt Eadburgebyrig his deg & hæfter his dege ga þæt land eft into
Þyses is to gewitnesse, Eadweard cingc & Ælfgsyfu seo hlefdige & Eadsige arcebiscop & Ælfric
Þyssa gewrita syndon þreo, an is on ealdan mynstre, oþer is mid þæs bisceopes landbocan, þridde hæfð
