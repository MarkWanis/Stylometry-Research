Her swutelaþ on þysan gewrite hu Cnut cyng & Ælfgifu seo hlæfdige geuþan Eadsige heora preost ða he
Þa sealde he hit into Cristescyrican þæt Godes ðeowum for his sawle.
& he hit gebohte þæt æt þam Hirede his dæg & Ædwines mid feower pundan on þæt forwyrd þæt man
& æfter his dæg & Ædwines gange þæt lande into Cristescirican mid mete & mid mannan eal swa hit
& he gebohte þæt land æt Werhornan æt ðam Hirede his dage & Eadwines eac mid feower pundan.
Ðænne gað þæt land forð mid þam oþran æfter his dæge & Edwines into Cristescirican mid ðære tilþe
& he geunn eac þæs landes æt Orpedingtune on his dæge for his sawle into Cristescyrican ðam Godes
& he geun eac ðæs landes æt Palstre & æt Wihtriceshamme æfter his dæge & Edwines forð mid ðam
Ðises cwides he geunn ðam hirede to þam forwyrdan þæt hi æfre hine wel healdan & him holde beon
& gif hi mid ænegan unrede wið hine ðas forwyrd tobrecan þænne stande hit on his agenan gewealde
Ðises is to gewitnesse, Cnut cyng & Ælfgifu seo hlædige & Æðelnoð Arcebiscop & Ælfstan Abbod &
& þissa gewrita synda III, an is æt Cristes cyrican & an æt Sancte Augustine & an hæfð Eadsige mid
