Eadward cingc grett wel mine biscopes & mine eorlas & ealle mine þegnas on þam sciran ðær Sancte
& ic kyþe eow þæt ic habbe gegifen him saca & socne, toll & team griþbryce & hamsocne & foresteal
& ic nelle geðafian þæt æni man habbe ænine onstync ofer his land ne ofer his men be strande ne be
God eow gehealde.
