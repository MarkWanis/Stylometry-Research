Utan gelæstan ælce geare ure sulhælmessan fiftene niht on ufan eastran and ure geoguðe teoþunge be
And healde man ælces sunnandæges freolsunge fram sæternesdæges none oð monandæges lihtincge and
Sunnandaga cypincge we forbeodað eac eornostlice and ælc folcgemot, butan hit for mycelre nydþearfe
And þæt man ælc beboden fæsten healde, sy hit ymbrenfæsten, sy hit lenctenfæsten, sy hit elles oðer
And to Sancta Marian mæssan ælcere and to ælces apostoles mæssan fæsten, butan Philippi et Jacobi
And we forbeodað ordal and aðas freolsdagum and ymbrendagum and lenctendagum and
And beo on þam halgan tidan, eallswa hit riht is, eallum cristenum mannum sib and som gemæne, and
And eac we lærað, þæt cristenra manna gehwylc understande, þæt he æfter forðsiðe butan sawulsceatte
We biddað for godes lufan, þæt ælc cristen man understande georne his agene þearfe, forðam ealle we
Utan beon arfulle fæder and meder and beon on gebyrge earmum wudewum and steopcildum and
Utan hlywan ofcalene and wæfan nacode and syllan mete þam gehingredum and drenc þam ofþyrstum.
Utan frefrian ahwænede and hyrtan ormode, alysan gehæfte, gif us to ðam gehagie; and seoce
Se ðe þus deð, he deð him sylfum micle þearfe.
