In nomine Domini. Denewulf bisceop & þa hiwan in Wintanceastre leton to Beornulfa hiora landes XV
Ðæt is XLV scilling to hærfestes emnihte sie simle agyfen on ða gerad ðæt hine nage nan man of to
& ðis wes gedon on ðara witena gewitnesse & ðafunge ðe hiora naman her benioðan standað awritene,
& ðet wes gedon on þære mæran stowe on Wintanceastre.
Bisceop lyfde Beornulfe his mege þæt he moste þa inberðan menn hamettan to Eblesburnan.
Nu hebbe ic hi hamet, Lufe & hire ðreo bearn & Luhan & his seax bearn.
Ðonne geærendodon me ða hiwan on Wintanceastre ðet þa men mostan on þan londe wunien, hæfde
Þonne weron þær ðreo witeðeowe men burbærde & ðreo ðeowberde.
Ða me salde bisceop & þa hiwan to ryhtre æhta & hire team.
Ðis wes gedon ða man þa cyricean halgode æt Hysseburnan, on ðara manna gewitnessa þe hira naman
