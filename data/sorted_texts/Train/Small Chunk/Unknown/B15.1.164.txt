Edward kynge gret Eldred Erchebissop and Begard Bissop and Harald eorl and alle myne þeynes of
And ich couþe ow þat ich habbe geuen Seynte Marie moder Cristes munstres and myne clerkes of
And ich nulle þolyen þat enyman enyþyng ouersoe nouþer Bissop ne nan oþer man boten lokewo ysself
And ich nulle ge þawyen þat enyman þys abreke by myne froshype.
Sound, ow ge holden.
