Eadweard cing gret ealle mine biscopas & eorlas & þegnas on ælcere scire þer land ligce in to Ealdan
& ic cyðe eow þæt ic hæbbe geunnen ælfwine biscop þæs byscoprices eall swa full & swa forð swa
& ic geann Criste & Sancte Petre & Sancte Paule in to þan halgan stede & him þæt hig beon heora sace
& gif hwa þis awendan wylle sy he fordemed mid Iudan Scariothe butan he ær his forðsiðe hit gebete.
