Ærest of myle forda ondlong erge þ in wæsceford.
Of wæsceforda ondlong erge ymb holaneige ufeweardre.
Of holaneige ufe weardre on þa acecge ufe weardre þ &lang þære acecge ufe wearde þ on snæd weg
Of snæd wege ymb heanlege on æcnabrycge up ondlong broces þ in ða dic.
Ondlong dices in tanes bæce.
Of tanes bæce ondlong mærgeardes.
Þ on lionhina gemære.
Ondlong leonhiena gæmeres þ on aðelwoldes hege.
Of aðelwoldes hege in hean oldan.
Of hean oldan in mærðorn.
Of mærþorne ondlong geardes in ðæt hlid get.
Of ðam hlidgete ondlong stræte on dicesgeat of dicesgeate in ðæt ðridde geat.
Ðæt ondlong stræte þ eft in myleford.
And Eadgar cyning gean Healhstane his þegn hagan on hereforda on boc a on ece yrfe.
Her is Stantunes boc.
