Ðis sind þa land gemæro to Bægen weorþe.
Ærest of gorgræfan up andlang slades on þone þorn þonne on þæt crundel suðeweard.
Þonne to þan wulle þ on læces ford þonne up andlang Riþe on gemærweg andlang weges on þa dic æt
Andlang dic on gemær weg.
Andlang weges on butan þorn hangran on ceobba stan forþ andlang gemær weges on þone weg æt
To yfemestan leage þonne forð ofer sciteres clif to hig wege up andlang weges to þan rahhege andlang
Þonne on wulfstanes dic.
Of þære dic on maduces lea þurh þone lea on þone put of þan putte on þone ellen stub þonne on lippan
Þonne on þæt wiþig bed þonne on dunan ford of þan forda andlang sunnigga wullæ broces on
Þis sint þara mead ge mæra þe to þan lande ge byriaþ.
Ærest of þære brycge on berige up andlang mid stremes oþ foran ongean þa niwan dic þonne oþ midne
Þonne synt þis þa ge mæro þære mæde æt ennan beorgum.
Of þære dice ende ford þon þa ea niþer andlang mid streames up on þa won lace oþ foran ongean þone
