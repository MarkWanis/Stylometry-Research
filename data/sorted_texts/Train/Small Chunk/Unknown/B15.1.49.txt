Her swutelað on þison gewrite hu æðelred kyning geuðe þæt æþerices cwyde æt Boccinge standan
Hit wæs manegon earon ær æðeric forðferde þæt ðam kincge wæs gesæd þæt he wære on þam unræde
& se cincg hit on mycele gewitnysse Sigerice arcebisceope cyðde þe his forespeca þa wæs for ðæs
Þa wæs he þisse spæce ægþer ge on life ge æfter ungeladod ge ungebett oð his laf his hergeatu þam
Þa wolde se cing ða spæce beforan eallon his witan uphebban & cwæð þæt Leofsige ealdorman &
Þa bæd seo wuduwe ælfric arcebisceop ðe hire forespeca wæs & æðelmær þæt hig þone cincg bædon
Þa God forgylde þam cincge, getiðode he ðæs for Christes lufan & sancta Marian & sancte Dunstanes
Þeos swutelung wæs þærrihte gewriten & beforan þam cincge and þam witon gerædd.
Þis syndon ðæra manna naman ðe ðises to gewittnesse wæron, ælfric arcebiscop & ælfheh biscop on
& Leofsige ealdorman & Leofwine ealdorman & ælfsige abbod & Wulfgar abbod & Byrhtelm abbod &
Þissa gewrita syndon þreo, an is æt Christes cyrcean, oðer æt þæs cinges haligdome, þridde hæfð seo
