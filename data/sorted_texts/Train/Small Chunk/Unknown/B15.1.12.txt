In nomine domini et saluatoris nostri Ihesu Christi. þis gewrit cyð ðæt Æþeluulf cyning geuðe Gode &
& he bead on Godes ælmihtiges naman & on ealra his haligra ðæt nan cyning ofer his timan ne nan oðer
& gif hit hwa awende þæt he wære on Godes awyrgednesse & sancte Petres ge on life ge æfter life
& þis wæs gedon ðæs geres ðe wæron agane ehta hund gera & feower & fifti geara æfter Cristes
