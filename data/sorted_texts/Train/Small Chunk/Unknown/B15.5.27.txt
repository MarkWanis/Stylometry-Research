Her switeleþ on þis write þe forwarde þat Ailmer þe biscopes brother hauede wrouht with Vui Abbot and
Þat is þat he selde hem on marc goldes to þe forwarde þat he habbe þat lond his day and ouer his day go
Ðise sinden þe mannes names þe to witnesse sinden þises, þat is Stigande bisscop and Vui Abbot and
Þise write sinden tua, on is binnen seynt Eadmundes biri and oþer haued Aylmer hymself.
