Her cyð on ðysum gewrite hu Godwine biscop on Hrofeceastre & Leofwine ælfeages sunu wurðon
Þa ða se biscop Godwine com to ðam biscopstole þurh hæse his cynehlafordes æðelredes cynges æfter
Ongan ða to specenne on ðæt land, & elles for Godes ege ne dorste, oððæt seo spræc wearð þam cynge
Þa ða him seo talu cuð wæs þa sende he gewrit & his insegl to þam arcebisceope ælfrice & bead him þæt
Þa þæt wæs þæt se bisceop Godwine com to Cantwarabyrig to ðam arcebiscope.
Þa com ðider se scyresman Leofric & mid him ælfun abbod & þegenas ægþer ge of East Cent ge of
& hy ðær þa spæce swa lange handledon syððon se bisceop his swutelunge geeowod hæfde oþ hy ealle
& se biscop þa þæs getiðode on ealra þæra witena þanc þe þær gesomnode wæran.
& he behet þæs truwan þæt land æfter his dæge unbesacen eode eft into þære stowe þe hit ut alæned
& þises loces ærendracan wæran, ælfun abbod & Wulfric abbod & Leofric sciresman & Siweard &
Þonne is her seo gewitnes þe æt þisum loce wæs þæt is ærest se arcebiscop ælfric & se biscop
