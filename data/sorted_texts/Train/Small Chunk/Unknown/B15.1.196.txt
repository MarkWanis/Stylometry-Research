Wilelm kyng gret Osmund bisceop of Seorbyrig & Robert of Eli & Pieðres of Ualoniis & ealle his
And ic kyðe eow þæt ic habbe gegyuen Maurice bisceope of Lundene þæne castel of Estorteford & alle
And eac halle þe land þæt William þe diacon & Raulf his broðer of me healdeð & habbeð.
And forðan ic wille þæt ge don þæt Maurices biscoep ealle þas þing & ealle þa oðre þing þe he hafed on
And ic wille þæt he beo his saca weorðe & his socna & his lagana weorðe swa swa Williames biscop
