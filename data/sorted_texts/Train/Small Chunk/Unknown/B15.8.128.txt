Aerest of Swæðelingforda west to Smerebrocesforda, and þanon westweard andlang herepaðes to
And swa foriþ andlange weges on suþhealfe gætes hlæwe þæt hit cymþ to feower treowum; þonon þanen
And swa forþ andlang weges oþ hit cymþ to fearnbedde, þanon east on þæt slæd oþ hit cymþ to holan
Þonne þanon norþ andlang holan broces oþ þa sand pyttas, þanon on Byrewege oþ hit cymþ on
And swa andlange broces oþæt hit cymþ to Ippingwanne, þonne suþ be Efist oþæt þæt slæde þa scit to
Þanon on gerihte to eastlea wearden þone norþ, swa se haga scyt to Baranleage norþwearden, þonne
Andlange stræte, oþ hit cymþ to grenan leage, þon þær east and suþ oþ hit cymþ to Cynninges dic.
Andlange þere dic, oþ hio bygð ongean mucelinge mæde norþwearde ut on Icenan, þon swa Icenan scyt
Þanon suþ to þane herepaðe þe lyþ to Mannes brycge, and swa weast andlange herepaðes on
And a mylansteall benorþan Mannesbrycge.
