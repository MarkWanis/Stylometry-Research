Sindon þry dagas on twelf monðum, þa syndon swiðe unhalwende men oððe nytenum blod to forlætenne
Þæt is se æftemesta monandæig on Aprilis, ær he gange of tune, and se forma monandæg on
Seðe on ðam þrim monandagum mannum oððe nytene blod forlæteð ær feowerteoðan dæge he sceal
And gif he drenc drincð to læcecræfte, ær fiftene dagan he sceal swyltan.
And gif hwilc man acenned byð on þysum þrim dagum, he sceal swyltan yfelan deaðe.
And seðe eteð flæsc on þysum þrim dagum, he sceal swyltan ær feowertugum dagum.
