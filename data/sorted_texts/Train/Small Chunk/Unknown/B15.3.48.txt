In nomine domini nostri Ihesu Christi.
Ic Eadnoð bisceop cyðe on þisson gewriton þæt ic onborgede XXX mancsa goldes be leadgewihte to
& ic gesealde hym ane gyrde landes to underwedde be Cridian to þam forewerdon þæt he hæbbe his dæg
Ðis sind þa landgemæro þære gyrde be Cridian.
Ærest on Sceocabroces ford, þonne east on herpað on þone lytlan garan easteweardne, suð on þa deadan
Ðisses ys to gewitnisse, Cnut cyning & Wulstan arcebisceop & Lifing arcebisceop & Birhtwold bisceop
& þis cydde se bisceop þam burhwiton on Exanceastre & to Tottanesse & to Hlidaforda & to
Pax sit hoc seruantibus & infernus sit hoc frangentibus.
