Þis syndon þa domas ðe Hloþhære & Eadric, Cantwara cyningas, asetton.
Hloþhære & Eadric Cantwara cyningas ecton þa æ, þa ðe heora aldoras ær geworhton, ðyssum domum
Gif mannes esne eorlcundne mannan ofslæhð, þane ðe sio þreom hundum scillinga gylde, se agend þone
Gif se bane oþbyrste, feorþe manwyrð he togedo & hine gecænne mid godum æwdum, þæt he þane
Gif mannes esne frigne mannan ofslæhð, þane þe sie hund scillinga gelde, se agend þone banan agefe &
Gif bana oþbyrste, twam manwyrþum hine man forgelde & hine gecænne mid godum æwdum, þæt he
Gif frigman mannan forstele, gif he eft cuma stermelda, secge an andweardne; gecænne hine, gif he
Gif ceorl acwyle be libbendum wife & bearne, riht is þæt hit, þæt bearn, medder folgige, & him mon an
Gif man oþrum mæn feoh forstele, & se agend hit eft ætfo, geteme to cynges sele, gif he mæge, & þone
Gif man oþerne sace tihte & he þane mannan mote an medle oþþe an þinge, symble se man þam oðrum
Gif he ðonne byrigan forwærne, XII scillingas agylde þam cyninge, & sio se sacy swa open swa hio ær
Gif man oþerne tihte, siþþan he him byrigan gesealdne hæbbe, & ðonne ymb III niht gesecæn hiom
Gif he þonne þæt nille, gelde þonne C buton aðe, siþþan ane neaht ofer þæt gesem hie.
Gif man mannan an oþres flette manswara hateþ oððe hine mid bismærwordum scandlice grete, scilling
Gif man oþrum steop asette ðær mæn drincen, buton scylde, an eald riht scilling agelde þam þe þæt flet
Gif man wæpn abregde þær mæn drincen & ðær man nan yfel ne deþ, scilling þan þe þæt flet age, &
Gif þæt flet geblodgad wyrþe, forgylde þem mæn his mundbyrd & cyninge L scillinga.
Gif man cuman feormæþ III niht an his agenum hame cepeman oþþe oðerne þe sio ofer mearce cuman
Gif Cantwara ænig in Lundenwic feoh gebycge, hæbbe him þonne twegen oððe ðreo unfacne ceorlas to
Gif hit man eft æt þam mæn in Cænt ætfo, þonne tæme he to wic to cyngæs sele to þam mæn ðe him
Gif he þæt ne mæge, gekyþe ðanne in wiofode mid his gewitena anum oþþe mid cyninges
Gif he þanne þæt ne mæge gecyþan mid rihtre canne, læte þanne an, & se agend tofo.
