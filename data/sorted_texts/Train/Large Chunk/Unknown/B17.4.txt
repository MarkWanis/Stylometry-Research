Þa ðaræfter gefor se abbot Nigel on Byrtune on ðone dæg v No Mai.
Þaræfter forbarn Cicestre & þæt mynster ðar forð mid on ðone dæg iii No Mai.
Þa to ðam Pentecosten wæs se king æt sancte Albanes stowe.
Heræfter he ferde mid his fyrde into Wealan to mide sumeran & macode castelas ðarinne.
And ða Wyliscean kingas coman to him & becoman his menn & him held aðas sworan.
Þaræfter he com to Wincestre & geaf ðar þæt ercebiscoprice on Eoforwic Turstane his clerice.
And þæt abbodrice æt sancte Eadmunde he geaf Albolde wæs munuc on Becc.
