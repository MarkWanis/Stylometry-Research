Be gesceadwisan gerefan.
Se scadwis gerefa sceal ægðær witan ge hlafordes landriht ge folces gerihtu, be ðam ðe hit of ealddagum
Forðam on manegum landum tilð bið redre ðonne on oðrum: ge yrðe tima hrædra, ge mæda rædran, ge
Hede se ðe scire healde, þæt he friðige & forðige ælce be ðam ðe hit selest sy; & be ðam he eac mot, ðe
He sceal snotorlice smeagean & georne ðurhsmugan ealle ða ðing, ðe hlaforde magan to ræde.
Gyf he wel aginnan wile, ne mæig he sleac beon ne to oferhydig; ac he mot ægðer witan ge læsse ge
Forðam to soðe ic secge: oferhogie he oððe forgyme ða ðing to beganne & to bewitanne, ðe to scipene
Ac ic lære, þæt he do, swa ic ær cwæð: gyme ægðer ge ðæs selran ge þæs sæmran, þæt naðor ne misfare,
Swa sceal god scyrman his hlafordes healdan, do ymbe his agen, swa swa he wylle.
A swa he gecneordra swa bið he weorðra, gyf he wið witan hafoð his wisan gemæne.
Symle he sceal his hyrmen scyrpan mid manunge to hlafordes neode & him eac leanian be ðam ðe hy
Ne læte he næfre his hyrmen hyne oferwealdan, ac wille he ælcne mid hlafordes creafte & mid folcrihte:
Æfre he mæig findan, on ðam he mæig nyt beon & ða nytte don, ðe him fylstan scylan; huru is mæst
Me mæig in Maio & Iunio & Iulio on sumera fealgian, myxendincgan ut dragan, lochyrdla tilian, sceap
On hærfeste ripan, in Agusto & Septembri & Octobri mawan, wad spittan, fela tilða ham gæderian,
On wintra erian & in miclum gefyrstum timber cleofan, orceard ræran & mænige inweorc wyrcean,
On længtene eregian & impian, beana sawan, wingeard settan, dician, deorhege heawan & raðe æfter
A he mæig findan, hwæt he mæig on byrig betan ne ðearf he na unnyt beon, ðonne he ðær binnan bið:
He sceal fela tola to tune tilian & fela andlomena to husan habban.
Æcse, adsan, bil, byrse, scafan, sage, cimbiren, tigehoc, næfebor, mattuc, ippingiren, scear, culter & eac
& fela towtola: flexlinan, spinle, reol, gearnwindan, stodlan, lorgas, presse, pihten, timplean, wifte,
And gif he smeawyrhtan hæfð, ðam he sceal to tolan fylstan: mylewerde, sutere, leodgotan & oðran
Man sceal habban wængewædu, sulhgesidu, egeðgetigu & fela ðinga, ðe ic nu genæmnian ne can, ge
Hit is earfoðe eall to gesecganne, þæt se beðencan sceal, ðe scire healt.
Ne sceolde he nan ðing forgyman, ðe æfre to note mehte: ne forða musfellan ne, þæt git læsse is, to
Fela sceal to holdan hames gerefan & to gemetfæstan manna hyrde.
Ic gecende be ðam ðe ic cuðe; se ðe bet cunne, gecyðe his mare.
