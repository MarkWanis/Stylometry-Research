Eala, gefyrn is þæt ðurh deofol fela þinga misfor, & þæt mancynn to swyðe Gode mishyrde, & þæt
Ne ræde we þeah ahwar on bocum þæt man arærde ænig hæðengyld ahwar on worulde on eallum þam
Ac syððan þæt gewearð þæt Nembroð & ða entas worhton þone wundorlican stypel æfter Noes flode, &
Þa syððan toferdon hy wide landes, & mancyn þa sona swyðe weox.
& ða æt nyhstan wurdon hi bepæhte þurh ðone ealdan deofol þe Adam iu ær beswac swa þæt hi
Hi namon eac him ða þæt to wisdome þurh deofles lare þæt hy wurðedon him for godas þa sunnan &
Sume men eac sædan be ðam scinendum steorrum þæt hi godas wæron, & agunnan hy weorðian
Ac hy mihton georne tocnawan, gif hi cuðon þæt gescead, þæt se is soð God þe ealle þas ðing gescop us
Ðas gesceafta eac ealle doð swa swa him gedihte heora agen scyppend & ne magon nan þing don butan
Gyt ða hæþenan noldon beon gehealdene on swa feawum godum swa hy ær hæfdan, ac fengon to
An man wæs on geardagum eardiende on þam iglande þe Creta hatte se wæs Saturnus gehaten, & se
He læfde swaþeah uneaðe ænne to life, þeah ðe he fordyde þa broðra elles; & se wæs Iouis gehaten, &
He aflymde his agene fæder eft of ðam ylcan foresædan iglande þe Creta hatte & wolde hine forfaran
& se Iouis wearð swa swyðe gal þæt he on his agenre swyster gewifode, seo wæs genamod Iuno, & heo
Heora twa dohtra wæron Minerua & Uenus.
Þas manfullan men þe we ymbe specað wæron getealde for ða mærostan godas þa on ðam dagum, & þa
Ac se sunu wæs swaþeah swyðor on hæðenscype gewurðod þonne se fæder wære, & he is geteald eac
And he hatte þor oðrum naman betwux sumum þeodum; ðone Denisca leoda lufiað swyðost & on heora
His sunu hatte Mars, se macode æfre gewinn & wrohte, & saca & wraca he styrede gelome.
Ðysne yrming æfter his forðsiðe wurðodon þa hæðenan eac for healicne god, & swa oft swa hy
& hy gelyfdon þæt he miclum mihte heom fultumian on gefeohte forðan þe he gefeoht & gewinn lufude
Sum man eac wæs gehaten Mercurius on life, se wæs swyðe facenfull & ðeah full snotorwyrde swicol
Ðone macedon þa hæðenan be heora getæle eac heom to mæran gode, & æt wega gelætum him lac
Ðes gedwolgod wæs arwurðe eac betwux eallum hæðenum on þam dagum, & he is Oðon gehaten
Nu secgað sume þa Denisce men on heora gedwylde þæt se Iouis wære þe hy þor hatað, Mercuries
And sum wif hatte Uenus seo wæs Ioues dohtor, & seo wæs swa ful & swa fracod on galnysse þæt hyre
Manege eac oðre hæþene godas wæron mistlice fundene & eac swylce hæþene gydena on swyðlicum
And se syrwienda deofol þe a swicað embe mancyn gebrohte þa hæðenan men on þam healicon
Ac se bið gesælig þe eal swylc oferhogað & ðone soðan Godd lufað & weorðað þe ealle þing gescop &
An is ælmihtig God on þrym hadum, þæt is fæder & suna & halig gast.
Ealle þa ðry naman befehð an godcund miht & is an ece God, waldend & wyrhta ealra gesceafta.
Him symle sy lof & weorðmynt in ealra worulda woruld a butan ende, amen.
