Þis syndon þæs londes gemæru æt mosleage.
Of þæm ældan feldæ west riht on gearuec swa onlong broces on þone gemær hagan.
Þæt ondlong hagæn ofer fild burnan on timber hrycg.
Þonon on beostinc hrycg swæ eastweard on blæcan hrycge.
Þonon eft in þone aldæn feld.
Þis syndon þara halfe hide lond gemæru æt wican.
Ærest of turtlingcforda on horsa croft.
Of horsa crofte be wulfrices gemære in þæt pull.
Of þæm pulle up weard bi þære wellan þæt up in þa lanan.
Ondlong lanan norð weard þæt on æntingc gærstun in þa hegcan.
Andlang hægcan eastweard þæt on lawern.
Ondlong lawern þæt on streon halh, be streonen halæ þæt in temedan upongean stream.
Ondlong temedan þæt eft in turtlingc ford.
