Her synd gewriten þa gerihta þæ ða ceorlas sculan don to Hysseburnan.
Ærest æt hilcan hiwisce feorwerti penega to herfestes emnihte & VI ciricmittan ealað & III sesðlar
& III æceras geerian on heora agenre hwile & mid heora agenan sæda gerawan & on hyra agenre wile on
& IIII foðera aclofenas gauolwyda to scidhræce on hiora agenre hwile & XVI gyrda gauoltininga eac &
& hi sculan waxan sceap & sciran on hiora agenre hwile & ælce wucan wircen ðæt hi man hate butan
Ðis synd þa lang gemero.
Ærest of twufyrde andlang weges to fearn hlince þanan andlang weges to æses beorge þanan on ge rihte
Þonne andlang weges on þa coppedan ac þanan andlang weges of he to wuda lið þonne on ge rihte on
Þonne andlang mearc weges to þan hagan be suþan fearn leage andlang hagan utt to ubban leage stigele
Þonne andlang hagan utt to bitan cnolle on ðone lit hagan andlang þære þorn græfan þwyres ofer
Þonne andlang weges on þone beorg æt wæcces treowe þanan on butan hig dune þornes to brunes
Þanan on ge rihte andlang hagan to hremmes dene þ andlang hagan on ðære ealdan mapolder be suþan
Þonne andlang hagan on sot ceorles æcer þanan ofer ða dæne upp be wyrt walan þonne on butan þone
Þonne andlang mearc weges to wifan stocce þanan andlang þæs ealdan weges to bradan lea þonne an
Þonne &lang dic utt on terstan on þone syþeran steð þonne &lang steþes þ be neoðan beamwær on þone
