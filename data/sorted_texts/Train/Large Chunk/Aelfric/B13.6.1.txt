Leofan Men, In Libro Visionum is awriten, hu þa mæssepreostas and ða diaconas, þe misleofedan her on
Hi stodon gebundenne, þæt hi abugan ne mihtan, to heardum saglum æt heora hricge on ðam hellican
And ða earman wifmen, þe hi hy wið forlagon, stodan ætforan heom fæste getigede on ðam hellican fyre
And se deofol hi beswang, swyðe gelome on heora gecyndlimum, swa swa seo boc us segð and swa swa
Þær wæron gemengde mæssepreostas and diaconas on þære cwylmincge, forðam þe hi Criste noldan
Þa beoð for Gode and eac swa for worulde wurðscypes wyrðe, þa ðe heahhade geþeoð mid rihte and hine
Ac hit is mycel hearm, þæt ðara is to fela, þe heahhades swyþor gyrnað, þonne hy sceoldon; and þæt
Nu wille we heononforð be sumum dæle wið þæt wærran beon, þonne we ær þissum wæron.
Se ðe hades wilnige, cume anum monðe ær þam hadtiman to ðam biscope and beo syðþan on fandunge,
Gyf he þonne mid þæs lareowes tacne to biscope cume, þonne beo he hade þe nyr, gyf he forð on
Þonne is ærest þis ðære fandunge fruma, on hwilcan geleafan he sy and hu he rihtne geleafan oðrum
Þonne, hu he his þenunge cunne, and hu he fulluht understande, and hu he mæssan getacnunge
And hwæðer he canon cunne be ænigum dæle; þonne, hu he on gerimcræfte gearryne tosceadan cunne.
Gyf he þissa þinga ealra gewis bið, þonne bið he hades þe bet wyrðe.
Gyf he þonne þissa þinga to lytel cunne, leornige ærest and afo syððan hades.
And swaþeahhwæðere, gyf man for neode scyle gehadian samlæredne, þe ealles to lytel cann, þonne do
Ðonne se hadtima sy and man to bisceope bringe, þa ðe he hadian scyle, þonne wile he witan, hwærto
Ðæt is, þæt he wite, se ðe hine him to bespece, hu he hades wyrðe sy and hu he hine ærþam gehealden
Gecyþe þæt soðeste be þam, þe he him mid wite, and sette hine syððan þam bisceope on hand, and
