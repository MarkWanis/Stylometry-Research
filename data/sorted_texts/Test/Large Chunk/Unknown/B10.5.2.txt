Her ongynð seo ondebyrdnyss, hu munecum gerist to healdenne þone regollican þeaw geond þæs geares
A kalendis octobris synd to nimene ealle anginnu eawfestlicra þeawa mid Godes fultume, þe synd to
Eornostlice, her sy gehealden seo nedbehefe þeaw þæs halgon regoles mid þære mæstan gymene, ge on
Forþy ma on ælcere tide on nihtlicum timan, þonne se broðer arist of his bedde to þam godcundlican
Siððan he singe þæt fers, Domine, labia mea aperies eal, syððan þone sealm, Deus in adiutorium meum
& þonne asende he his bene to drihtne mid gebigendum cneowum on geþæslicra stowa & on wunelicra,
Eornostlice, on þam forman gebede he singe þa þry þara seofan penitentiales psalmos, Domine, ne
Þingiende for him sylfum ærest, æfter þysum he bidde þis gebed, Gratias, þæt ys, Eala, þu ælmihtiga
Syððan he gange to þam oþran gebede & singe þa twegan æftre sealmas, Miserere mei, Deus &
Syððan he gange to þam þriddan oratione, þære he singe þa twegen æftemestan penitentiales sealmas
Witodlice, hringe me ane bellan andfealdlice, oððæt þa cildru in to cyrcean cuman.
Eac swylce notigean þa sylfan cild þrymfealdum oratione, þonne hi beon inn aganne, þæt seo
Soðlice, þonne þa þreo gebedu beon geendode fram þam cildum, þonne hringe me þa oðre stunde,
Soðlice, betwux þam oðrum stundum & þam geendedum mid þam ilcan fiftynum sealmum ongynnan hi
& swa sy gedon on eallum geendedum regollicum tidum.
Æfter þysum sealmum gewurðe sum litel hwil on eallum sumere, swa swa se regol bebyt.
Ga seo scola mid heora magistrum to þare gewunelican neode & belifan þa oþre innon cyrcean on
Æfter þysan folgian æftersanges, and æfter Miserere mei Deus togeycean hi twegen sealmas for þam
Æfter þyson ys to ganne to þam oðran æftersange, þæt ys De omnibus sanctis, singende ænne
Æfter De omnibus sanctis singan hi for forðfarenum Laudate Dominum in sanctis eius on uhton, & on
Ðæt gif hit byþ geendod be dæges leohte, swa swa hit gebyreð, onginnan hi primsangc butan ælcere
Þysum, soðlice, geendodum, licgan hi ealle eadmodlice astrehte ætforan þam weofode buton ælcere
