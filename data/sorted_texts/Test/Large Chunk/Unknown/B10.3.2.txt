Be godra weorca tolum.
Ærest man sceal god lufian of ealre heortan and of eallum mode and of eallum mægene and æfter
Ne sceal man manslean, ne unriht hæman, ne stelan, ne unalyfedlice gelustfullian, ne leasunga
Man sceal hys agenum lustum wiðsacun and folgian Cristes lare, his lichaman gehwa sceal
Earmne man sceal hyrtan and nacode scrydan; untrume neosian and deade bebyrgean; geswectum
And fram woruldwilnungum hine sceal gehwa fremdian and nan þinc beforan Cristes lufe settan.

Swerigean ne seal man, þe lys man forswerige; soðfæstnysse of heortan and of muðe man sceal
Ne sceal man yfel mid yfele gyldan, ne nanum men nænne teone ne don, ac þone þe him man
