Incipit prefatio Genesis Anglice.
ÆLFRIC MUNUC GRET ÆðELWÆRD EALDORMANN EADMOdlice.
Þu bæde me, leof, þæt ic sceolde ðe awendan of Lydene on Englisc þa boc Genesis: ða þuhte me
Nu þincð me, leof, þæt þæt weorc is swiðe pleolic me oððe ænigum men to underbeginnenne, for þan þe
Hwilon ic wiste þæt sum mæssepreost, se þe min magister wæs on þam timan, hæfde þa boc Genesis, &
Ful soð he sæde, ac he nyste, ne ic þa git, hu micel todal ys betweohx þære ealdan æ & þære niwan.

Ða ungelæredan preostas, gif hi hwæt litles understandað of þam Lydenbocum, þonne þingð him sona
Hi cweþaþ eac oft be Paul, hwi hi ne moton habban wif, swa swa Petrus se apostol hæfde, & hi nellað
Preostas sindon gesette to lareowum þam læwedum folce: nu gedafnode him þæt hig cuþon þa ealdan æ
We secgað eac foran to þæt seo boc is swiþe deop gastlice to understandenne, & we ne writaþ na mare
Þonne þincþ þam ungelæredum þæt eall þæt andgit beo belocen on þære anfealdan gerecednisse, ac hit
Seo boc ys gehaten Genesis, þæt ys gecyndbod, for þam þe heo ys firmest boca & spricþ be ælcum
Heo onginð þus: In principio creauit Deus celum & terram; þæt ys on Englisc, On annginne gesceop
Hit wæs soðlice swa gedon, þæt God ælmihtig geworhte on anginne, þa þa he wolde, gesceafta.
Ac swa þeah æfter gastlicum andgite þæt anginn ys Crist, swa swa he sylf cwæþ to þam Iudeiscum: Ic
Þurh þis angin worhte God Fæder heofenan & eorþan, for þan þe he gesceop ealle gesceafta þurh þone
Eft stynt on þære bec on þam forman ferse: Et spiritus Dei ferebatur super aquas; þæt is on Englisc, &
Godes gast ys se Halga Gast, þurh þone geliffæste se Fæder ealle þa gesceafta, þe he gesceop þurh þone
Eft is seo halige ðrynnys geswutelod on ðisre bec, swa swa is on ðam worde, ðe God cwæð: Uton
Mid ðam ðe he cwæð: Uton wyrcean, is seo ðrynnys gebicnod; mid ðam ðe he cwæð: to ure anlicnysse,
He ne cwæð na menigfealdlice: to urum anlicnyssum, ac anfealdlice: to ure anlicnysse.
Eft comon ðry englas to Abrahame, & he spræc to him eallum ðrym swa swa to anum.
Hu clypode Abeles blod to Gode buton swa swa ælces mannes misdæda wregað hine to Gode butan
Be ðisum lytlan man mæg understandan, hu deop seo boc is on gastlicum andgyte, ðeah ðe heo mid
Eft Iosep, ðe wæs geseald to Egypta lande & he ahredde ðæt folc wið ðone miclan hunger, hæfde
Ðæt micele geteld, ðe Moyses worhte mid wundorlicum cræfte on ðam westene, swa swa him God sylf
To ðam weorce brohte ðæt folc gold & seolfor & deorwurðe gimstanas & mænifealde mærða; sume eac
Þæt gold getacnode urne geleafan & ure gode ingehyd, ðe we Gode offrian sceolon; ðæt seolfor
Man offrode eac fela cynna orf Gode to lace binnan ðam getelde; be ðam is swyðe mænifeald
Nu is seo foresæde boc on manegum stowum swyðe nearolice gesett, & ðeah swyðe deoplice on ðam
Is eac to witene ðæt sume gedwolmen wæron ðe woldon awurpan ða ealdan æ, & sume woldon
God gesceop us twa eagan & twa earan, twa nosðyrlu, twegen weleras, twa handa & twegen fet, & he
We sceolon awendan urne willan to his gesetnyssum, & we ne magon gebigean his gesetnyssa on
Ic cweðe nu ðæt ic ne dearr ne ic nelle nane boc æfter ðisre of Ledene on Englisc awendan; & ic bidde
God ðe sy milde a on ecnysse.
