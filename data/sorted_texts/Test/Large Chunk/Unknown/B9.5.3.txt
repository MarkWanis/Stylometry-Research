Her yrneð up se æftra stream þære godcundan spræce, se cymð of þære rynelan þæs gastlican
Þysum wordum se ilca gyldenmuða Gregorius wæs sprecende & þus cwæþ: sum wæs swyþe
Se sona of þære tide his cnihthades wæs healdende & donde swyðe gedafenlice ylde on his þeawum,
Ac he wæs þa gyt on þyssere eorðan geseted, þæt he mihte hwilwendlice lifigean, swa ma manna doð.
Þa geo sona forseah þisne middaneard mid his blostman, efne swylce he naht & adrugod wære.
He wæs geboren of freon & of æþelum cynne Nursige þære mægðe, & he wæs befæsted to
Ac þa þa he geseah manige men gan þurh þa stæþhlypan heora uncysta, his agnum fet he wiðteah,
Þam he wiðbrægd, þy læs hit gelumpe, þæt hwæt unrihtes hine gehrine of his agenum geþohte &
& þa forsægenum þam onginnum þara boccræfta, he forlet his hus & his fæder æhta & gewilnode,
He sohte & geceas þone had & gegyrelan þæs halgan munuclifes & him þa gewat swiþe
Ne leornode ic sylf na ealle þa dæda þyses godan weres, ac þa feawa, þe ic secge, ic ongæt æt
& eac swylce ic hit gehyrde fram Ualentine, se manega gær wæs fore þam mynstre æt Latrane, &
Witodlice se halga wer Benedictus geþohte, þæt he forlete þa ongin þæra boccræfta.
He gesohte westennu, & his fostormoder ane wæs him fylgende, forþon þe heo hine swyðe geornlice
& þa þa hi becomon to þære stowe, þe is genemned Effide, hi wunodon þær mid manigum
Þa weras hæfdon hi sylfe þær mid lufan in þære cyrican þæs eadigan apostoles sancte Petres.
