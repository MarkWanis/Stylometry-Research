Be Biscephadum.
Leofan men, se halga godspellere Lucas geswutelode on his godspelle hu ure Drihten, ær he up to
Nu syndan we bisceopas to þam sylfan gesette, þæt we bodian sceolan Godes riht georne & unriht
And nu þæt is þæt we habbað on Godes naman weorðunge for cristendomes fyrðrunge bisceop
Moyses hatte, se ðe on geardagum be Godes agenum dihte rihte lage scyfte, & he þa gehalgode Aaron
& of þam cynne æfter þære wisan syððan wurdan manege, oð þæt Crist eft astealde on oðre wisan hu
And Petre þæne ealdorscipe he ærest betæhte & hefenware cæga eac him befæste & het þæt he heolde
And nu syndan biscopas Petres gespelian, & hi sceolon Godes riht geornlice cyðan.
Ðonne lære we georne manna gehwylcne þæt he hyne sylfne gelome beþence & inwerdre heortan lufie
Bisceopas syndon bydelas & Godes lage lareowas, & hy scylon georne oft & gelome clypian to Criste
And se ðe oferhogie þæt he heom hlyste, hæbbe him gemæne þæt wið God sylfne.
And gyf bisceopas forgymað þæt hi synna ne styrað ne unriht forbeodað ne Godes riht ne cyðað, ac
Be þam spæc se witega & grymlice þus cwæð: Haec dicit Dominus: Si non adnuntiaueris iniquo
Gif þu þam synfullan nelt, cwæð ure Drihten, synna gestyran & unriht forbeodan & þam manfullan his
Ðis mæg to heorthoge æghwylcum bisceope, beþence hine georne be þam þe he wille.
And se ðe nele Godes bodan hyran mid rihte ne godcundre lare gyman swa he sceolde, he sceal hyran
Forþam se bið Godes oferhoga þe Godes bodan oferhogað, ealswa Crist sylf on his godspelle swutelice
Se ðe eow hyreð, he cwæð, me he gehyreð, & se ðe forsyhð eow, witod he forsyhð me.
Et item: Quodcunque ligaueritis super terram, et reliqua.
And eft ure Drihten on oðre stowe soðlice þus cwæð: Swa hwæt swa ge gebindað her ofer eorðan
Leofan men, warniað eow be swylcan & uton we ealle don swa us þearf is, beorgan us georne wið
Eala, eala, soð is þæt ic eow secge, swær is seo byrðen þe Godes bydel beran mot gif he nele georne
And þeh Godes bydel misdo, ne beseo man na þær to ealles to swyðe, ac gyme his lare gif he tæla lære,
He cwæð: Filgeað heora larum & na heora synnum.
Ne sceal ænig man æfre for sacerdes synnan hine sylfne forgyman, ac fylge his lare gif he wel lære.
And la, leofan men, doð swa ic bidde, butan gebelge hlystað hwæt ic secge.
