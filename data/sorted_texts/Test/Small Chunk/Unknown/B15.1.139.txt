Eadward kyng gret Stigand biscop on Wintanceastre & Wulfwold abbot on Cyrteseiæ & Leofwine eorll
& ic kyþe eow þæt ic wille ðet þæt plott landes æt Clæigate ligge nu heonon forð inn to Sancte Petre æt
& ic ðes fullice geuðe for minre saule & for hiere & for ealle cwiken & for ealle deaden to alesennesse.
& ic ann heom ðer ofer sakæ & socne, toll & team, infangeneðeof blodwite & weardwite hamsocne &
God eow ealle gehealde, Sancte Petres holde.
