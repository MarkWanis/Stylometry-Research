Hir sinda dam landgemære into Sudwellan.
Of dam forda andlang greotan sid se ealdartreau þo est be eastan & be sudan dam grete þon ge rihte ðurh
Hir sint þa longemaere et Normantune.
Of streatan to þere dichacce andlang þan hagan on þaene broc andlang broces þ on þa dene andlang
Hir sindan hocer wuda of þe fre strat ofer þaene mor to dreas haga nordeweardan þanen up to dare
Hir sint þa landegmare to Fiscetune.
Andlang Streotan on Traentan on haseleford of dam forda andlang streot to moorn dune of sy forndune
Hir sint dam tunes de birad in to Sudwellan mid sacce & mid sacne Fearnes feld.
