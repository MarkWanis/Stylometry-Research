Ic Oswold bisceop þurh Godes gefe mid geþafunge & leafe Eadgares Angulkynincges & Ælfheres
Þæt sint hida on twuam tunum þe fram cuþum mannum Teottingctun & Ælfsigestun sint gehatenne
Æfter þara bearna dæge fo Eadleofu to his gebedde hire dæg.
Æfter hire dæge becweþe hire broþrum twam swilc hire leofest sy.
Æfter hieora dæge eft into þære halgan stowe.
Sy hit ælces þinges freoh butan ferdfare & walgeweorc & brygcgeweorc.
Þis wæs godon ymbe nigon hund wintra & nigon & seoxtig þæs þe Drihtnes gebyrdtide wæs on þy
Sancta Maria & sanctes Michahel cum sancto Petro & eallum Godes halgum gemiltsien þis
Gief hwa buton gewyrhtum hit awendan wille God adilgie his noman of lifes bocum & habbe him
Her is seo hondseten Oswoldes bisceopes & unna þæs hierdes on Wiogernaceastre.
