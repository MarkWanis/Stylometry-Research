Ic Lufa mid Godes gefe ancilla Dei wes soecende & smeagende ymb mine saulðearfe mid Ceolnoðes
Willa ic gesellan of ðem ærfe ðe me God forgef & min friond to gefultemedan ëlce gere LX ambra
End sue eihwelc mon swe ðis lond hebbe minra ærbenumena ðis agefe & mittan fulne huniges, X goes,
Ic Ceolnoð mid Godes gefe ercebiscop mid Cristes rode tacne ðis festnie & write.
Beagmund preost geðafie & mid write.
Beornfrið preost geðafie & mid write.
Ic Luba eaðmod Godes ðiwen ðas forecwedenan god & ðas elmessan gesette & gefestnie ob minem
& ic bidde, & an Godes libgendes naman bebiade ðæm men ðe ðis land & ðis erbe hebbe et
Se man se ðis healdan wille & lestan ðet ic beboden hebbe an ðisem gewrite, se him seald & gehealden
Se his ferwerne oððe hit agele, se him seald & gehealden helle wite, bute he to fulre bote gecerran wille
Uene ualete.
