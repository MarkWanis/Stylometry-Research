On halgum gewritum is geræd þæt se ælmihtiga God þurh XII þing syleð mannum forgifenesse heora
Seo æreste synna forgifenes is þurh fulluhtes bæð.
Seo æftere forgyfenes is þurh Godes lufan and manna, þæt gehwa lufige God of ealre his heortan
Seo þridde forgyfenes is þurh ælmessylene, forþam hit is awriten þæt, eallswa ma mid wætere fyr
Seo feorðe forgyfenes is þurh sealmsang and teara agotenesse, þæt gehwa for his synnum hreowsige and
Seo fifte forgyfenes is þurh andetnesse, þæt gehwa his synna andette mid inweardlicre heortan his
Seo sixte forgyfenes is þæt gehwa for Godes lufan and for his sawle þearfe winne wið his lichaman
Seo seofeðe forgyfenes is þæt gehwa his æhta and his bearn and his eard for Godes lufan forlæte and on
Seo eahtoðe forgifenes is þæt se man of þisse life fare to wite, and his freond þonne þe on þisse life
Seo nigoðe forgifenes is mildheortnes and god geleafa.
Seo teoðe forgifenes is þæt man oðerne gehwyrfe fram his synnum to Godes willan.
Seo endlyfte forgifenes is þæt gehwa for Godes lufan forgife þæs mannes gyltas þe wið hine agylt,
Seo twelfte forgifenes is martirhad, swa þam sceaðan wearð æt Drihtnes þrowunge, þa se hælend cwæð
