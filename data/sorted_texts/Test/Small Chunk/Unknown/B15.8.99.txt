Ðes synd þa land gemaera to Myceldefer.
Arest on Myceldefer, andlang mearc weges on þone sand pytte, þonan anlang þara ealdena dala, þonan
Þonan to Worðigsaetena mearc, andlang Wordihaema mearc on þone hlinc bufan Friþelinga dic, of þan
Of þam fyrdgat on ða hocedan dic, of þaere hocedan dic on Dennerdes treow, of þam treow on weg
