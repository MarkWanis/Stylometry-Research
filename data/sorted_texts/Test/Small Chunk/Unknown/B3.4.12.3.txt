hi ne magon asecgan hungor.
& ðone unablinendan ðurst & ða æwisciban nacodnisse & ða ecan þistru & ðone bryne þæs sweartan
& þæt eara earmlicost is þæt he næfre god meg þence ne god hine ne ðæs nowiht elles bið gehired nimðe
Se ðe wile him ðas trego bebeorgan bewarnige he him unrihtgestreon & unrihthæmed & dierne
