Her cyð æþeric on þissum gewrite hwam he geann ofor his dæig þæra æhta þe him God alæned hæfð.
Þæt is ærest sona minum hlaforde syxti mancusa goldes & mines swyrdes mid fetele & þarto twa hors &
& ic geann Leofwynne minan wife ealles þæs þe ic læfe hire dæig & ofor hire dæg gange þæt land on
& ic geann þæs landes æt Rægene be westan into sancte Paule þam bisceope to to geleohtenne & þar on
& ic geann þarto twegra hida þe Eadric gafelaþ ælce geare mid healfum punde & mid anre garan.
& ic geann be eastan stræte ægþer ge wudas ge feldas ælfstane bisceope into Coppanforde & þæs heges
& ic geann þæs landes æt Norðho healf into sancte Gregorie on Suþbyrig & healf into sancte Eadmunde
Nu bidde ic þone bisceop ælfstan þæt he amundige mine lafe & þa þincg þe ic hyre læfe & gif him God
