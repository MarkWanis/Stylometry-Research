Cnut cing gret Lyfing arcebiscop & Godwine biscop & ælmær abbot & æþelwine scirman & æþelric &
& ic cyðe eow þæt se arcebiscop spæc to me ymbe Cristes cyrcean freols þæt heo hæfð nu læsse munde
Þa lyfde ic him þæt he moste niwne freols settan on minan naman.
Þa cwæð he to me þæt he freolsas genoge hæfde gyf hi aht forstodan.
Þa nam ic me sylf þa freolsas & gelede hi uppan Cristes agen weofod on þæs arcebiscop gewitnysse &
Gyf hit hwa þænne do sy his lif her gescert & his wunung on helle grunde butan he hit þe stiðlicor
