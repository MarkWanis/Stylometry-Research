An swinbroc ærest þ up of swinebroc in on riscslæd of þæs riscslædes byge foran ongean hordwylles
Þ andlang þæs weges oð hit cymð to Iccenhilde wege.
Þonne of þan wege up on þone ealdan wude weg.
Þonne of þæn wude weg be eastan telles byrg on ænne garan.
Þonne of þan garan on ænne gar æcer þ andlanges þære fyrh to anum andheafdum to anre forierðe & seo
Þanon west on anne goran andlanges þære fyrh to anum anheafdum þanon of dune on fearn hylles slæd þ
Þonne on þene hlinc æt fearn hylles slæde suþe wearde.
Of þæm hlince on anon heafde forð þær on ane furh on ane stan ræwe þanon on gerihte on hricg weg.
Þ þanone on ane gar æcer on anon heafde & se gar æcer in on þ land.
Þanone andlanges anre furh oþ hit cymð to anum byge þanone of þaem byge forð on ane furh oþ hit
Þonne on Icenhilde weg be telles burh westan þanone norð ofer Icenhilde weg on sican wylle þ hþweres
An lang þæs broces oþ hit cymð to twam gar æcer.
And þan gar æceras in on þ land þanon on ane forierðe on an on heafde þanon on gerihte on readan clif
Þonne andlang þæs broces on þæt riscslæd.
