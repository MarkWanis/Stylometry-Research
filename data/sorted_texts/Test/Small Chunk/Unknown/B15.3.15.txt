Æt Eanulfestune, Eadrice & Wulfrune.
Albuscente hac consentiente Eadgaro basileo Aelfhere que Merciorum ego Oswold largiflua dei
& healfne þone wudu on easthealfe þæs weges & þone æt þære eorðbyrig & on easthealfe Afene eahta
Þonne is ealles þæs landes þreo hida þe Oswald biscop bocað Eadrice his þegne on þa gerad wyrce þæt
Anno dominicae incarnationis (15) DCCCCLXVI scripta est hec carta his testibus consentientibus
