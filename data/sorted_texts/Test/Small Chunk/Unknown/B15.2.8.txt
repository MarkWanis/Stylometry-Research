Æðelstan ealdorman geboce þis land of Uffentune into sancte Marie stowe to Abbendone be þes kinges
& þæt wes be Winsies biscopes gewittnysse of Bearrucscire & Wulfhem arcebiscop & Rodward biscop
& se arcebiscop Wulfhem & ealla þa biscopes & abbodes þe þer gesomnode wæron amansumeden fram
Beo he ascyred & gesceofen into helle grunde aa buten ende.
& cwæþ eall þæt folc þe þer embstod, sy hyt swa, amen, amen.
