Ðæt is þonne ærest ægelwig abbot on eofesham, & Wulfwold abbot on ceortesige, & ælfsige abbot on
Ðæt is þæt we willað georne gehyrsume beon gode, & Sancta marian, & Sancte benedicte, & us sylfe
And beon swa swa hit awriten is: Quasi cor unum et anima una & we willað urum woruldhlaforde
And habbe we us gerædd betweonan to ure saule þearfe, & to ealra þara gebroðra þe us underþeodde
Þæt is þæt we willað beon on annesse, swylce ealle þas vii mynstras syn an mynster, & beon swa hit her
Ðæt is þæt we ælcere wucan singan ii mæssan on ælcum mynstre synderlice for eallum gebroðrum,
And wite se broðar þe capitula mæssewuca bið þæt þas mæssan geforðige for ða gebroðra þe libbende
Ælc þæra þinga foredon swylce hig ealle ætgædere on anum mynstre wæron.
& nu is þara abboda cwydrædene þæt hig willað beon gode gehyrsume & heora bisceope to heora
Þæt is þæt heora ælc sceal don an C mæssan, & of his agenra handa gebycge & an C þearfendra manna
God us gefultumige, þæt we hit þus motan gelæstan, & mid suman gode geeacnian.
Sic Fiat.
