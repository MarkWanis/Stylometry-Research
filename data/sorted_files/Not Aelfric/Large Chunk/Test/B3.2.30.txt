De letania maiore.
Leofan men, ðas dagas synd gehaten letanie þæt synd gebeddagas.
On þissum dagum we sceolon biddan ure eorðlicra wæstma genihtsumnysse and us sylfum
And we rædað on bocum þæt se gewuna þissa gangdaga wurde aræred on þone timan þe gelamp on anre
Þæt wearð mycel eorðstyrung and feollon gehalgode godes cyrcean & manega hus hruran and comon
And þa bead se biscop mamertus þreora daga fæsten and þæt man halidom sceolde wyrðlice styrien and
And sona swa þæt gedon wæs þa wæs swutele gesyne þæt heom god syðþan uðe lisse and miltse.
Ne wearð næfre syððan þanon forð eft swylc gedrecednys innan þam lande ac wearð þæt to bysne wide
Leofan men, nu bidde ic and lære ælcne cristene man þæt he þissa þreora daga fæsten rihtlice gehealde
Gyf þonne þissa þreora þinga ænig hwylcne man lette þæt hine to þam fæstene ne onhagie þonne is hit
Gyf þonne se hala mid þam unhalan þone mete þigeð þonne wurþað hig begen þurh þæs mettruman
And nis nanum men alefed for nanre neode þis fæsten to abrecane ne mid ælmessan to alysenne butan
Leofan men, se halga apostol iacobus mynegode us ealle freondlice on þam pistole þe man ær rædde
He cwæð, Confitemini alter utrum peccata uestra, ðæt is on englisc, anddettað georne, he cwæð, eowre
And ælc for oðerne he cwæð gebidde georne þæt eow god ælmihtig gesundfulnysse geunne.
For þam ic secge eow þæt rihtwises mannes gebedu fremiað swyðe mycclum.
Elias on ealddagum se halga wæs man eall swa we synd and he abæd þeah æt gode þæt he forwyrnde
And he abæd eft syðþan æt þam soðan gode þæt he renas forgeaf & eorðlice wæstmas.
Ðær is swutol þæt haliges mannes gebedu magan mycles wealdan.
Se halga apostol sæde eac on þissum pistole þæt gyf hwylc man gehwyrfeð oþerne fram gedwylde, þæt
Ac, leofan men, understandað eow sylfe swyðe georne þæt ge rihtlice and wærlice þæt gehealdon þæt
Leofan men, se halga godspellere, lucas, rehte on þissum godspelle þe man nu ær rædde hu ure drihten
He cwæð, gyf besorh freond cymð to oðres huse on midre nihte and he næfð him to donne swa swa he
Ðonne andwyrdeð se oðer þær wiðinnan and cwæð þæt he ne mæge arisan, segð þæt his dyre synd
Gyf he þonne se oþer for ðon georne clypað and cnucað þonne æt nehstan arist he for ðam onhrope and
Be þissere bysne ure drihten lærð us hwæt we don sceolon.
Ðæt is þonne, þæt we sceolon clypian and biddan georne gelome god ælmihtig þæt he ure neoda
And gyf we ægðer ge mid wordum ge mid weorcum rihtlice and geornlice clypiað þonne wile he ure
Ac butan seo heorte rihtlice hycge and weorc þam wordum fylstan ne behealdeð þa word naht swyðe
Swa he sylf on his godspelle swutollice sæde and ðus cwæð, Non omnis qui dicit domine domine, ðæt
Leofan men, utan clypian georne oft and gelome and earnian georne þæt god ælmihtig ure bena
Ðeos woruld is eall wyrse þonne heo wæs and dæghwamlice heo yfelað swyðe for ðam þe hit sceal
Þæt gefyrn awriten wæs, Ueniet tempus quale non fuit, ðæt is on englisc, swa egeslic tima cymð to
Þæt is on antecristes timan and se tima wyrð on worulde sorhful and egesfull for ðam þe antecrist bið
Leofan men, her segð eac on þyssum bocum be ðære mycclan arwyrðnysse ures scyppendes hu
And he wolde þæt seo gesceaft ongeate heora scyppend se ðe he to life gescop on neorxnawanges
And þeah hwæþre þurh deofles beswicenysse gelamp þæt seo mennisce gesceaft of þam undeadlican
Gehyrað nu broðor mine hwylc þa leohtfatu wæron þe he ure drihten onbærnde on þissum
Þæt wæron ærest heahfæderas þa wæron gefylde to godum weorcum and ðeawum þa þe godes folc
Þanon onwocon witegan þa wæron gelærde þurh þone halgan gast and na læs þæt an þæt hy ðæs
And æfter þon wæron halige apostolas þa ðe he sylfa geceas to þam þæt he ure drihten hi of his sylfes
And ða æfter þissum wæron soðlice gesette halige biscopas and mæssepreostas and diaconas and
Gehyrað nu broðor mine and ongytað for hwan us is gesett þeos tid to weorðianne fram þam halgan
Se ælmihtiga godes sunu se ðe undeadlice æt þære tide aras and oft and gelome mid his gingrum wæs
Ðonne syndon us þas þry dagas to þon gesett þæt we sceolon þa halgan cristes þegnas onhyrgan, þæt
For ðon þe nu ealle eorðan wæstmas growende syndon and blowende geopenade and þæt ure bileofan
For ðon us is þeos tid geset ure sawla hælo on to biddanne and ures lichoman bletsunge and ura
Utan we nu symle þang secgan urum scyppende his miltsa for ðon þe he us swyðe lufode ofer ealle
He sylfa us gescop and us geworhte þa we næron and he us gesihte þa we forwordene wæron and he
For ðon we sculon ongytan hu mycel neadþearf us wæs his tocymes to ure alysednysse.
La, hwylc mihte beon hefigre nydþearf þonne se eca deað and hwylc mihta beon hefigre wite þonne
Cuðlice we wæron on deofles þeowdome and we wæron on helle fæðmum of þæm he us alysde and us
Leofan men, se witega cwæð be heofonarice and be hellewite þæt næfre nære gemet on heofonanrices
Swylce he cwæð se ylca witega þæt næfre nære on hellewitum swa mycel wynsumnes swa anes lytles
Wa þam þe geearnoð hellewite.
Ðær is ece bryne grimme gemencged and þær is ece gryre, þær is granung and wanung and a singol
Wa ðam þe þær sceal wunian on wite.
Betere him wære þæt he man nære æfre geworden þonne he gewurde for ðam nis se man onlife þe
Eala leofan menn, uton don swa us mycell þearf is beorgan us georne wið þone egsan and helpan ure
Ac utan lufian god ofer ealle oðre þing and his willan a wyrcan swa we geornost magon.
Þonne geleanað he hit us swa us leofast bið þonne we æfre betst beþurfon.
Him symle sy lof and wuldor in ealra worulda woruld abutan ende, amen.
