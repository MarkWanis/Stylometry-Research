BE MUNECA CYNNE.
Syx synt muneca cynerena, þara synt þreo þa selestan, þa oþere þreo þa forcuþestan and eallum
Þæt forme cyn is mynstermanna, þe gemænan life drohtniað on mynstre, gelice þam halgum, þe
Þurh þara apostola angin and þara, þe him folgodan, munuclif wurdon begunnene.
Oþer cyn is muneca, þæt is westensetlan, þe feor fram mannum gewitað and westestowa and ælætu
Swilce westensetlan eornestlice þisne middangeard forhogiaþ and on westenes wununge
Swa dihlice wuniende hy fram manna gesyhðe ascyriaþ and Godes agenre spræce gelomlice
Þridde cyn is ansetlena, þe on mynsteres wununge fulfremede sindon and hie sylfe on syndrigum
Feorðe cyn is þara, þe hy under leasum hiwe ansetlan teliaþ.
Be swa geradum Sanctus Casianus cwyþ, þæt hi mid ungesceade on þam frumwylme heora
Ne wyrþ næfre fulfremed, se þe on þus niwan anginne ansetla beon wile.
Ne beoð his unþeawas gewanode mid þysum anginne, ac swiðe miclum geeacnode; ðonne heora
Nan man ne dear for arwyrðnesse þæs ansetlan leahtras tælan; him synt eac þa ungeselþa leofran,
Fifte cyn is widscriþelra hleapera, þe under muneces gegyrlan æghwider scriþaþ, swilce hy
Idele and lease spel hi hywiaþ and mannum reccaþ, be wenan hi healdaþ God ælmihtigne; sume
Sume sidfeaxe gaþ, þæt seo bescorene halignes ne sy weorþre, þenne seo unbescorene, þæt hy þurh
Sume secgaþ, þæt hy micelne weordscype and rice magas on heora earde hæfdon and þa eft
Ðonne hy on heora dædum and on yfelum wordum arasode beoð and on sumon gemete onfundene,
Þæt sixte muneca cyn is ealra forcuþost and swiðost forsewen, þæt asprang on anginne cristendomes
Hi tymbriaþ him stowa and hy leasum naman mynstra hataþ, nellað hy ealdras habban, ne be heora
To geflites hi swincað on weorce, no toþi, þæt hi heora geswinces gestreon þearfum delen, ac þæt
Soþlice, se sylfa Hieronimus be heom cwyþ, to geflites hy fæstaþ and, þæt digle þing beon scolde,
Hy gelyst ealces ydeles, habbaþ side earmellan and pohhede hosa, stiþe reaf gelomlice hy
Betweoh cenobium and monasterium þyllic todal wyrhð Sanctus Casianus, þæt monasterium mæg
Þara drohtnunge ic gesweotelige, swa swa ure forþfæderas hit gesettan.
Hi witodlice, swa swa we ær cwædon, ærest þis lif forseoð and ealles þysses lifes unalyfednessa,
Nan heora eorðlices nan þing synderlice nah, mid deorwyrþum reafum and gefagedum ne beoþ hy
Hy nahwider faraþ butan þæs abbodes ræde, ne nan þincg beginnaþ butan þæs ealdres geþafuncge;
Mid heora handum wyrcende heora sealmas singaþ, swylce hy heora geswinc mid godcundre
Þæt þæt hy wyrcaþ, hy betæcaþ hit heora teoþingealdrum, þæt hy butan hoge and care syn ealra
Ða teoþincgealdras þæt ylce þam prafestan betæcaþ, se prafast þonne þæt mid micelre
Þa federas þænne scealon beon foremære on andgite, on þylde, on gesceade; hi þonne eaþmode and
Hy gegaderiaþ hy swiþe gelomlice dæges and nihtes mid micclan ofste to heora tidsangum,
Eft hy gaderiaþ hy on nixtnig, þæt hy raca gehyren æt heora fæder.
Hi þonne be heora race gehyraþ mid micelre gecneordnesse and mid healicum swigean, mid
Hy siþþan heora lichoman mid micelre swigan gereordaþ, swa micel þicggende, swa hy heora hæle
Witodlic fram flæsce and wine hy forhabbaþ, toþy þæt hy ælcne galscype on hy gewylden, na þæt
Eallunga swa hwæt swa hy gespariaþ on heora forhæfednessæ and swa hwæt swa toforan þam
To þes halgan þeowdomes ingehyde cumaþ na þa ane, þe freo synt, ac gyt ma þa, þe æhtborene
Of cyrliscum life and of folclicum gedeorfe gehwelces creftes men to þam ylcum Godes
Gif man swilce underfon nelle, hit byþ swiðe hefigtyme gylt.
Heora formænig foremære wearþ and earfoþe to geefenlæcenne, forþy God þa unstrangan þyses
Þa he eac geceas, þe dyselice and nahtlice geþuhte synt, þæt þa, þe mid ofermettum hy sylfe for aht
Witodlice, þam ylcum gemete wuniaþ and gyt aþolede synt munecena mynstru, þe man hurum
Nis nanra geongra manna neosung to þæm, ne forþon þeah hy fulripode syn and wel afandode,
Heora wealdaþ anlipige staþalfeste and wel afandode and on heora ealdorscype heora underþeodra
Mid willenum reafe heora lichoman gegearwiaþ and gescyrdaþ, hy munecum reaf syllaþ and
Ane þeawas, an lif and ane gesceadwisne hi healdaþ muneca mynstra on eallum þingum.
Munucas þurh heora eaþmodnesse hy beoþ gecorene and Gode andfencge.
Heora formænig mid idelum lofe byþ besmittad and mit þam leasum gylpe beswicen, manige hy
God hy wyrcaþ, ac þæt for idelum lofe and na for ecum life, ac þurh þæt gewilniaþ ideles lofes
Betweoh swylcum ungeþwærnessa aspringaþ; gif hwylc broþor on Gode þyhþ, on þæt hy andiaþ,
Þa þe þyllice beoþ, nateshwon hy na synt to nemnenne munecas, forþy þe hy ne sint Gode

Syn hy clængeorne, geþyldige and eaþmode, bewarnian hy georne wiþ upahæfednesse and
Ne hy nane spræce wiþ þa gebroþra agen; ne hy þærinne leng ne wunien, þonne seo mæsse

Gif þara gebroþra hwylc his diglan synna þæm mæssepreoste andettan wille, sy þæt gedon be þæs
