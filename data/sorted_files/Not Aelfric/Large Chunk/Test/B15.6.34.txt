Her is on þis write Keteles quide.
Þat is þat ic an Stistede after mine tyme for mine fader soule and for Sefledan into Cristes kirke.
And ic wille þat mine men ben alle fre & Mann myne refe þat he sitte on þe fre lond þat ic him to honde
And ic an into þere kirke þat lond þat Withrich hauede vnder hande and Lewine and Siric and Goding so
And ic wille þat alle þo men þe ic an fre þat he habben alle þinge þe he vnder hande habben buten þat
And ic an þat lond at Herlinge Stigand Archebisscop mine louerd so it stant buten þo men ben alle fre
And gif ic ongein ne cume þan an ic him to min heregete an helm and a brenie and hors and gereade
And ic wille after þe forwarde þat ic and Eadwine and Wlfric after mine time fon to alkere þinge þe min
And sithen al þat lond þat him to honde begeð folege mid þe oþere into þe kirke.
Gif Eadwine min em wille helden se felageschipe mid me & Wlfric min em ymbe þat lond at
And þat is min and mine sustres Boten, þat forwarde, gif ic mine day do her his þat ic fon to þat lond at
And þat ilke forwarde ic and Gode mine suster habbed speken, gif he me ouerbide gripe he to þat lond
And ic an Godric mine brother þat lond at Hemfordham so it me on hande stand and Kockeshale.
And he scal for þat lond at Strattune gifen Alwy mine cnihte to pund.
And ic and Algif mine stepdouhter wit habben wrouth þe forwarde ymbe þat lond at Anhus þat so
And gif vnc ban fordsith sceot on Rome weye þanne go þat lond for me and for Sefleð and for Algiue
And ic an Harold erl after mine time þe halue lond at Moran so ful and so forth so ic it richtlike on
And ic it sithen nawer ne forswat ne forspilde.
And ic bidde þe þurh þene Drichtin se þe and alle schefte schop gif ic ongen ne cume þat þu it nefre ne
And ic an þe lond at Fretinge after þat ilke forwarde þat þu þe self and Stigand Archebiscop mine
And ic an Alfric mine prest and mine cuthen þat lond at Rissewrthe.
