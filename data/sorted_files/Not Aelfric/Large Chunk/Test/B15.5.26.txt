Her kyþ on þison gewrite þæt Harold king let beridan Sandwic of Cristes cyrcean him sylfan to handa &
& amanc þisan siþan siðe wearð Ælfstan abbod æt sancte Augustine & begeat mid his smehwrencan &
Þa gerædde Eadsige arcebiscop þa he þis wiste & eall se hired æt Cristes cyrcean betweonan heom þæt
& wæs se king þa binnan Oxanaforde swyþe geseocled swa þæt he læg orwene his lifes þa wæs Lyfingc
Þa com Cristes cyrcean sand to þam biscop & he forð þa to þam kincge & Ælfgar munuc mid him &
Sædon þam kinge þa embe Sandwic þæt hit wæs him to handa geriden.
Þa læg se king & asweartode eall mid þare sage & swor syþþan under God ælmihtine & under ealle
Þa wæs soðlice gesyne þæt hit wæs oðra manna geþeaht næs na Haroldes kinges & soðlice Ælfstanes
Þa sende Harold king Ælfgar munuc agen to þam arcebiscop Eadsige & to eallon Cristes cyrcean
Þa Ælfstan abbod þis ofaxode þam com he to Eadsige arcebiscop & bæd hine fultumes to þam hirode
& hi begen þa to eallon gebroþran & bædon þone hired þæt Ælfstan abbod moste beon þæs þriddan
Ac hy forwyrndon heom ealle togædere endemes þæt he hit na sceolde næfre gebidan & wæs þeah
& þa he ne mihte na forð hermid þa gyrnde he þæt he moste macian fornan gen Mildryþe æker ænne
Ac eall se hired him forwyrnde þæs forð ut mid ealle & se arcebiscop Eadsige let hit eall to heora agene
Þa gewearð se abbod Ælfstan æt mid micelan fultume & let delfon æt Hyppeles fleote an mycel gedelf
Ac him na speow nan þingc þæron forþam he swingð eall on idel þe swincð ongean Cristes willan.
& se abbod let hit eall þus & se hired fengc to heora agenan on Godes gewitnesse & sancta Marian &
Þis is eall soð gelyfe se þe wylle.
Na gebad Ælfstan abbod næfre on nanan oþre wisan þone þriddan penig of Sandwic.
Godes bletsung si mid us eallon a on ecnysse, amen.
