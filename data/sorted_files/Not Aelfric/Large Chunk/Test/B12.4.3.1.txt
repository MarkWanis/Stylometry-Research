Anglice.
Drihten, for þinre þære miclan mildheortnesse & for ealra þinra haligra lufan & geearnunga gemiltsa me
&, min drihten, ne læt me næfre færlicum deaðe of þissum earman life gewitan, Ac, loc hwænne min
Eac ic bidde þe, min drihten leof, for þines sylfes naman & godnysse, þæt þu me of þisse weorulde ne
Heofona heah cyning & ealles middaneardes alysend, gemiltsa me earminge, swa swa þin mæra willa
& min drihten, forgif me soðe hreowe & andetnesse & bote minra gylta & ahwyrf me fram minum
& min drihten, forgif me rihtne geleafan & soðe lufe & eadmodnysse & arfæstnysse & clænnysse &
& min drihten, gehnexa þa heardnysse minre þære stænenan heortan & forgif me teara genihtsum þæt
& min drihten, onhliht minre heortan geþanc mid lifes andgite & onhliht mine word & dæda & minne
Min drihten ælmihtig god, sy ðe lof & wuldor & þanc a on ecnesse & eallum þinum halgum ealra þara
Ic bidde ðe min drihten eadmodlice þæt ðu me gehelpe & ealra minra freonda & maga & eallra ðæra þe
& forgif þam libbendum gesundfulnesse on þisum life ge on þam towardan ece myrliðe & syle þam
Eac ic bidde ðe min drihten þæt ðu gemildsige eallum þam ðat me god dydon & god tæhton & syle ece
Drihten heofona heah cyning, gestranga hi to ðinum willan & gemildsa eallum cristenum folce
