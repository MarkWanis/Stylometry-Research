Sancte Mildryð.
On drihtnes naman Sanctus augustinus gefulwihte æþelbryht cantwara cyning & ealle his ðeode.
Þonne wæs eadbald cyning æþelbryhtes sunu & byrhtan his cwene & æþelburh heora dohtor, oðre naman
& æfter his life hio eft cantwara byrig gesohte & hire broðor eadbald þæne cyning & paulinus se
& hio hyre þa betstan madmas to cantwaran cyricean brohte hire to gebedrædene & þæs cyninges sawle
& he ða paulinus onfeng þa bisceoprice æt hrofeceastre on godes willan & ðær his lif geendode &
Ðonne wæs eormenred cyning & eorcenbyrht cyning & Sancte eanswyð, hi wæron ealle eadbaldes
& Sancte eanswið resteð on folcanstana þæm mynstre þæt hio sylf gestaðelode.
Þonne wæs eormenburh & oðre naman domne eafe & eormengyð & æðelred & æðelbriht wæron
Ðonne wæs domne eafe forgyfon to myrcna landa merwalde pendan sunu cynges to cwene & hi þær
& hi þa æfter ðan for godes lufan & for þisse worolde him todældon & hiora bearn & hiora woruld æhta
& hiora yldeste dohtor & Sancte mildburh resteð æt wynlucan þæm mynstre on mercna lande þær
Sancte mildryð resteð binnan teneð on ðæm iglande & ðær wæron oft hyre mihta gecyþede & get synd.
Sancte mildgyð resteð on norðhembran þær wæron hire mihta oft gecyðede & get syndon þonne wæs.
Sancte merefin þæt halige cild on iogoðhade to gode gelæd.
Þonne wæron æðelred & æðelbryht þa halgan æþelingas befæste egcbrihte cynge to fostre & to lare for
Þa wæron hi sona on geogoðe swyðe gesceadwise & rihtwise swa hit godes willa wæs.
Ða ofðuhte þæt anum þæs cyninges geferan se wæs þunor haten & wæs him se leofestan ðegen to his
Ða ondrædde he him gif hi leng lifedon þæt hi wurdon þam cynge leofran ðonne he.
Ongan hi þa hatian dearnunga & wregean to þam cyninge & cwæð þæt gif hi libban moston þæt hi
Ongan hine ða biddan þæt he moste þa æþelingas dearnunga acwellan ac se cyning him lyfan nolde for
& þa git se ðunor hine oft & gelome bæd þæt he him leafe sealde þæt he moste don embe ða æþelingas
& he ða sona swa dyde swa he ær gyrnende wæs & he hi on niht sona gemartirode innan ðæs cyninges
& he geðoht hæfde þæt hi þær næfre uppe ne wurdan ac ðurh godes mihte hi þanon gecydde wurdon
& he ða se cyning sylf embe forman hancred ut gangende wæs & he þa him sylf geseonde wæs þæt
Þa wearð he afyrht & afæred & het hi hrædlice þæne þunor to feccean & line ahsode hwær he his
He him andsworode & cwæð þæt he sylf wiste & he him secgan nolde buton he nyde sceolde.
He ða se cyning cwæð þæt he be his freondscipe hit secgan sceolde.
He him andsworode & cwæð þæt he hi innan his healle under his heahsetle bebyrged hæfde.
& he þa se cyning swyðe unrot geworden wæs for þæs godes wundre & for þære gesihþe ðe he ðær
& þa on morgen swyðe hrædlice him to gefeccean het his witan & his þegnas þæt hi him geræddon
& he þa & hi geræddon mid ðæs ærcebisceopes fultume Deusdedit þæt man heora swustor on mercna
& hio ða swa dyde þæt hio þæt wergeld geceas þurh godes fultum on ðam iglande þe teneð is nemned,
& hit ða swa gelamp þa se cyning & hio domne eafe ærest þæt land geceas & hi ofer þa ea comon þa
Hio him ða andsworode & cwæð þæt hio his na maran ne gyrnde þonne hire hind utan ymbe yrnan
Cwæð þæt hire þæt getyðed wære þæt hio swa myceles his onfon sceolde swa seo hind hire gewisede.
He ða se cyning hire geandsworode & cwæð þæt he þæt lustlice fægnian wolde.
& hio ða hind swa dyde þæt hio him beforan hleapende wæs & hi hyre æfterfiligende wæron oð þæt hi
& he ða se þunor to ðam cyninge aleat & he him to cwæð, leof, hu lange wylt ðu hlystan þyssum
Wylt ðu hit eal ðære cwenon syllan?
& ða sona æfter þyssum wordum se eorðe tohlad.
