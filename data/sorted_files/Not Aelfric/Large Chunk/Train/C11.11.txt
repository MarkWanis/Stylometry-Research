Swilc swa is se fæder swilc is se sunu swilc se halga gast Qualis pater talis filius talis spiritus
Ungesceapen is se fæder ungesceapen is se sunu & is ungesceapen se halga gast Increatus pater
Unameten is se fæder unameten is se sunu Inmensus pater inmensus filius inmensus spiritus
Ece se fæder ece is se sunu ece se halga gast Eternus pater aeternus filius aeternus spiritus
& ðeah hwæðre ne synt ðreo ecean ac is an ece Et tamen non tres aeterni sed unus eternus.
Swaswa ne synt ðreo ungesceapene ne þry unametene ac is an ungesceapen & an unameten
Gelice ælmihtig is se fæder ælmihtig is se sunu ælmihtig is se halga gast Similiter omnipotens
& þeah æþre ne synt þreo ælmihtigan ac is an ælmihtig Et tamen non tres omnipotentes sed unus
Swa god se fæder is se sunu god swa & is se halga gast god swa Ita deus pater deus filius deus
& þeah hwæþre ne synt þreo godas ac is an god Et tamen non tres dii sed unus est deus.
Swa hlaford se fæder is se sunu hlaford swa & is se halga gast hlaford swa Ita dominus pater
& ne synt þreo hlafordes ac is an hlaford Et tamen non tres domini sed unus est dominus.
Forþi þe swaswa synderlice vel or had god & hlaford andettan cristenre soðfæstnesse we synt
Swa ðry godas oððe hlafordas secgean mid geleaffulre æwfæsnesse we synt forbodene Ita tres
Se fæder fram ænigum nis geworht ne gesceapen ne gestryned Pater a nullo est factus nec
Se sunu fram þam fæder anum is ne geworht ne gesceapen ac gestryned Filius a patre solo est
Se halga gast fram þam fæder & ðam suna ne geworht ne gesceapen ne gestryned ac
An eornostlice fæder na þry fæderas is an sunu na ðry suna is an se halga gast na þry halige
On þissere ðrynesse ænig þing ær oþþe lator nan þing mare oþþe læsse Et in hac trinitate nihil
Ac ealle þry hadas emnece him sylfum synt & gelice Sed totae tres personae coaeternae sibi sunt
Swa þæt þurh ealle swaswa eallunga wiðufan gecweden hit is & seo ðrynnes on annysse & seo
Se þe wile eornostlice hal beon swa be þære þrynnesse undergyte Qui uult ergo saluus esse ita
Ac him is neodþearf to þære ecan hæle þæt flæsclicnysse eac swylce ures drihtnes hælendes
Is eornostlice geleafa se rihta þæt we gelefan & we andetton þæt ure drihten hælend crist godes
God is of edwiste fæder ær woruldum gestryned & man is of edwiste moder on worulde acenned
He is fulfremed god is fulfremed man of gesceadwisre sawle on meniscum flæsce &
Gelice þam fæder æfter godcundnysse is læsse ðam fæder æfter menniscnysse Equalis patri
Þa þeah ðe he god sig & man ne synt twegen þeah hweðere ac is an crist Qui licet deus sit et
He is an soðlice na mid awendednysse godcundnysse on flæsce ac mid underfangennysse
He is eallunga na mid gemengednysse edwiste ac annysse hades Vnus omnino non confusione
Soðes swaswa is sawl gesceadwis & flæsc an is mann swa god & mann an Nam sicut anima
