Mine gebroðru ða leofestan, ge sculon to soðon ðis witan, hwanon wære ongunnen ærest þeos bisne, þæt
Soðlice þa ða ure Drihten, þe is ælmihtig God, gesceop þone forman man Adam, he gesceop hine ealne
Seo halignis & rihtwisnis & seo undeadlicnis didon Adam habban Godes silfes anlicnysse & gelicnesse.
And forðan cwæð God ærðam he gesceope Adam: Uton wircan man to ure anlicnisse & gelicnisse; þæt
And þas his anlicnesse us bebead Drihten to habbenne þa ða he cwæð on his godspelle, Estote sancti,
Eornostlice forðan þe God gesceop Adam swa godne & swa haligne, we rædað þæt he asende hine into
Soþlice on ðam ilcan neorxnawange him forgeaf Drihten ealne wuldor.
Ðær he geseah englas & wið hig spræce, & næfre he ne swulte, gif he ne singode.
Ðær he gehyrde Drihten silfne wið hine sprecende & him bebodende swilce gehirsumnisse þæt he
Ac þa on ðære ylcan tide þe Adam þis bræc & wið God singode, þa adraf hine Drihten, þe is bisceop
Ac hwæt dide Adam siððan he wæs adræfed of neorxnawange?
Gewislice he dide swyðe lange dædbote geond syx hundred geara fæc & þonne gyt mare.
Æt nyhstan æfter his forðsiþe he wæs asend on hellicne cweartern, þær he lange tyd heofode &
And swa lange he wæs ðær besargiende & behreowsiende, oð þæt ure Drihten Hælend Crist, se ðe is
Eornostlice, gebroþru mine þa leofestan, we bisceopas beoð ures Drihtenes Hælendes Cristes speliendas
& forðan us geaf Drihten æfter þam apostolum anweald manna sawla to bindenne & to unbindenne &
Soðlice þus cwæð Drihten þurh Hieremian his witegan to ælcum bisceope: Efne ic gesette þe ofer
Understandað, leofan men, mid godum wyllan þæt þæt we eow willað secgan.
Forðon sende God ælmihtig Adam into neorxnawange, þæt on ðam ylcan neorxnawange wurdon
And gewislice swa hig diden gyf Adam ne agylte.
Mine gebroðru, þa ylcan bisne ures Drihtenes Hælendes Cristes we biscopas habbað on circan, & þa
Witodlice, swa swa Godes sunu lædde Adam haligne & rihtwisne into neorxnawange, swa eac we &
Forhwan læde we þæge men on cyrcan þe ær wæron towordene halige þurh fulluht buton forðam ylcan
Swa eac we sacerdos forðon lædað gefullode men into circan, þæt hig on ælcne tyman wircon on þære
God ælmihtig gesette Adame æ þæge he sceolde healdan þurh gehyrsumnysse.
Hwilce æ, buton þæt he ne æte of ðam treowe ingehydes godes & ifeles?
Swa eac God gesett eow ðurh us æ þæge ge sculon healdan on cyrcan.
Hwilce æ gesett eow Drihten to healdenne þurh us, þæt is, þurh ure mynegunge?
Witodlice þas æ he gesett eow to healdenne on ðare he ðu cwæð: Ne ofsleh þu.
Ne unrihthæme ðu.
Ne stel ðu.
Ne sege þu lease gewytnisse.
Ne swera þu man.
Lufa þinne Drihten God of ealre þinre heortan, & lufa ðinne nehstan swa swa ðe sylfne.
Leofan men, þas bebodu ealle eow gesette Drihten þurh us, þæt ge hig healdon & wyrcon þurh
Ac hwæt dide Drihten be Adame forðon þe he ne heold þa gehyrsumnysse?
Gewislice he adraf hine ut of neorxnawange, wepende & heofigende & swyðe unrotsiende.
Swa eac we biscopas adræfað eow ut of Godes circan, forðon ðe ge ne healdaþ þa gehyrsumnysse
Þa ða he adraf hine of neorxnawange, þa he ðus cwæð: In sudore uultus tui uesceris pane tuo; þæt is:
Ac hwæt dide Adam siððan he wæs adræfed of neorxnawange?
Gewislice he dide mycele dædbote to þon þæt he wære wyrðe þæt hine God eft onfenge into
And ge ealswa þæs þe we truwiað didon dædbote on þyssum lengtenfæstene þæt ge beon wyrðe in to
Ðæt ge sculon swaþeah witan þæt swa swa se eadiga Ambrosius cwæð þæt nan bisceop ne mæg
And forðon we secgað eow ðis, þæt gyf ge nabbað git swylce behreowsunge gedon þe Gode lycie, þæt
And gyf ge ne magon fæstan, ge magon huru don ælmissan seo alyse eowre sawla of helle.
Ðæt ge sculon eac understandan, þæt ge næfre na mare ne don on eowre dædbote buton swa mycel
Ac æfter þære dædbote ge moten be eowrum agenum wyllan mare don, swa swa didon þa halgan þe
Mine gebroðru, efne nu is Adam eft underfangen on þam heofenlican neorxnawange for ðære myclan
And ge, Gode þanc, nu todæg beoð eft underfangene on cyrcan swylce into neorxnawange for eowre
And nu forðon þe ge on þissum dæge hider samod comon to onfonne eft þære halgan cyrcan
Ðæt eow geunne se mildheorta Drihten, þe leofað & rixað on ealra worulda woruld, amen.
