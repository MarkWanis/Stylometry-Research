Ceolwin cyð on ðis gewrite þæt hi an ðæs landes æt Aweltune ðara XV hida ðæ hire hlaford hire læfde &
& hio he bebot on Godæs naman & sancte Petres ðet ða hiwan hit næfre utt ne syllan of hira bæddern
& hiwan habbað hire behaton ðet hi finden ðet Wulfstan hire broðor sunu hæbbe an hiwissce ægefæles
Aweltunæ metæ, ðes londes gemæro sindon to Aweltune, ærest of þam westmæstan æwylle angerihta
Þonne on ænne stan æt ceorlacumbes heafoð, þonne on ænne stan on woncumb neoþeweardne is
Þonne ofer randune to þæm ealdan dic on æfen þær liggeþ on oþre healfe on lytel crundol, þonne on
Þonne on anne micelne stan æt þera hlinca eastheafdum, þonon on oþerne micelne stan on þam wege
Þonne on ænne crundol of þam suðhliðe nioþeweardum, þonne up ofer þa dune on ænne þorn stent inne
Þonne andlang þes hlinces of moxes dune on þane ealdan walweg to wearcingwege nioþeweardan on
Þonon on gerihto to ruwan hlince, þonne andlang þæs hlinces to Heaþobrihtting leage nioþoweardre,
Fram moxes dune suðeweardre on þa dic, þonne andlang dic þæt wið eastan cealfa mære.
Þonne of þære dic on þone midmæstan scrippan, þonne on þawæres leage ufewearde.
Þonne andlang rode on þone litlan garan middeweardne, þonan andlang gewyrpes to herpoðe.
Þonne to þære dice hyrnan, þonne andlang dic to creodan hylle, þonne to herpoðe ongean þa XV
& se heðfeld eal gemæne.
