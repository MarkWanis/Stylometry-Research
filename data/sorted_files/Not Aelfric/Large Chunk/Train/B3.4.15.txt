Hit gelamp hwylan æt suman cyrre þæt an ancra gefing ænne deofol þurh Godes mihte, and he wæs se
Þa se ancra angan þreapian swyþe þone deofol, þæt he him asæde eal hellewites brogan and eac heofona
Þa cwæþ se deofol to þam ancran þus: þeah þæt lengeste triow þe an middangearde is, and hit stode
Þa git cwæþ se deofol to þam haligan lifes men, Wa biþ þam mannum þe sculan habban heora
Þær biþ wop butan frofre, and þær biþ þeowdom butan freowdome and unrotnes butan gefean; þær biþ
Nis þær cyniges weorþung ne ealdormannes werþnes; þer nan man ne mæg his waldend gemunan mid
He cwæþ þa gyt se deofol to þam halgan ancran þus, and sæde to him, þios eorþe nære mid eallum hire
Þonne is se micele garsicg þy þas eorþan utan embligeþ swiþe ormetlice micel, þæt nis eal þios eorþe
Þa cwæþ se deofol þa git to þam ancran þus: þeah mon þane garsicg mid isenan wæalle utan betyne and
Ongytan we nu hu se deofol sæde to þam halgan ancran hyllewite; swa he him eac sæde heofona rices
And he cuþe swiþe wel and he mihte eaþe hit secgan, forþon he wæs hwilan scinende æncgel an
Ac hine awearp Drihten of heofonum for his ofermettum, and þone modigan feond on hellewite forþon
And he ða forþan gewearþ to deofle awend and ealle his geferan and eac ealle þa þe æt his ræde wæron
Ealle hi wurdan of þan engelican hiwe to deoflum awende, and gefeollan þa heom an helle diopnesse
And forþon is eghwilcum deofle swiþe cuþ hwilc hit is an heofona rice mid Criste on þære ecan
Wel is þam æfre to worulde þe on þere stowe wunian mot.
And þa cwæþ se deofal to þam ancran þa git þus: þeah þe sie sum smetegelden dun eal mid gimmum
Ða þæt deofal þis eal hæfde asegd þam haligan ancran þa forlet he hine, and se deofal gewat þa to helle
Ac utan we nu men þa leofestan geearnigan mid godan dedum þæt we to uran Dryhtne becuman motan
Þam Dryhtne sie simle wuldar and werþmend in ealra weorulda weoruld, Amen.
