Þis is Alfwoldes bisceopes cwyde, þæt is ðæt he geann þæs landes æt Sandforda in to þam mynstre in to
& anes hiwscypes he geann Godrice þærof & an sylhðe oxna.
& he geann his hlaforde feower horsa, twa gesadelode & twa unsadelode, & feower scyldas & IIII spera
He is eall gearo butan þam hanon he hine wolde ful gearwian his hlaforde to gerisnum gif him God uðe.
& Ordulfe twegra boca, Hrabanum & martyrlogium.
& þam æþelinge XL mancsa goldes & þæra wildra worfa æt Æscburnan lande & twegra getelda.
& Alfwolde munuce XX mancsa goldes & anes horses & anes geteldes.
& Byrhtmære preoste XX mancsa goldes & anes horses.
& his þrim magon Eadwolde & Æþelnoðe & Grimkytele hira ælcon XX mancsa goldes & hira ælcon
& Wulfgare his mæge twegra wahryfta & twegra setlhrægla & þreo byrnan.
& Godrice his aðume twegra byrnena.
& Eadwine mæssepreoste V mancsa goldes & his kæppan.
& Leofsige mæssepreoste þæs mannes þe he him ær tolet Wunstan hatte.
& kenwolde helm & byrnan & Boian anes horses & Mælpatrike V mancsa goldes & Leofwine Polgan
He lænde Tune & his geswysternon, gehealdon hi hine.
& Eadgyfe his swyster an strichrægl & I hrigchrægl & I sethrægl & Ælflæde offestran V mancsa
& his hiredcnihton eallon V pund to gedale ælcon be þam þe his mæð wære.
& in to Crydian tune þreo þeningbec mæsseboc & bletsungboc & pistelboc & an mæssereaf.
