Ærest ymb min lond þe ic hæbbe, & me God lah, & ic æt minum hlafordum begæt, is min willa gif me
Gif me ðonne gifeðe sie, ðæt ic bearn begeotan ne mege, þonne is min willa þæt hit hæbbe min wiif ða
& min broðar Alchhere hire fultume & þæt lond hire nytt gedoe & him man sëlle an half swulung an
Gif min wiif ðonne hia nylle mid clennisse swæ gehaldan, & hire liofre sie oðer hemed to niomanne,
Gif hire ðonne liofre sie an mynster to ganganne oðða suð to faranne, ðonne agefen hie twægen mine
& mon selle to Folcanstane in mid minum lice X oxan & X cy & C eawa & C swina & higum
Gif higan ðonne oððe hlaford þæt nylle hire mynsterlifes geunnan, oðða hia siolf nylle, & hire oðer
& ic bidde & bebeode swælc monn se ðæt min lond hebbe ðæt he ælce gere agefe ðem higum æt
& swælc monn se ðe to minum ærfe foe, ðonne gedele he ælcum messepreoste binnan Cent mancus
& Freoðomund foe to minum sweorde & agefe ðeræt feower ðusenda, & him mon forgefe ðeran
& gif mine broðar ærfeweard gestrionen ðe londes weorðe sie, þonne ann ic ðem londes.
