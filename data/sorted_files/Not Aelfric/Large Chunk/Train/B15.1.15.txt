Regnante in perpetuum domino nostro Ihesu Christo.
Ricsiendum urum Dryhtne hælendum Criste in ecnisse ðæm hiehstan & ðæm untosprecendlican ealra
Forþon ic Æþelbreht mid Godes gife Westsaxna kyning, witoðlice ic þence & me on gemynde is mid
Forþon ic cuþlice mid geðeahte & mid geðafonge & leafe minre biscepa & ældormanna & nohte þon
Þæt hit sy fæstlice & unanwended & ecelice gefreod alra cynelicra & alra domlicra þeowdoma ge
Gyf hwa þonne sye þæt he hine for Godes lufan to þan geeaðmedan wille þæt he þas ure gyfe geieacnan
Gyf þanne hwilc man to þan geþristlæce oððe mid deofles searwum to þam beswicen sye þæt he þis on
Ðis wæs gewriten þæs gære þe wæs agan fram Cristes acennednesse eahta hund wintra & feower &
Þis wæs gedon in þam cynelican setle on þære stowe ðe is genæmned Dornwaraceaster beforan þissum
Ic Æþelbreht cyning mid þære halgan rode tacne þis het swiþe geornlice getrymman & gefæstnian.
Þa æfter þyssum hit gelamp þan ilcan geare þæs þe þis on midne winter wæs gedon þæt Æþelbreht se
For hine selfne lifigende & for his twegen broþre þe þæt þa andwearde stodon & for hyra ealra fæder
He þisne freodom on ælmihtiges Godes namon & on ealra his halgra fæstlice bebead þæt hine nan his
Ac he swa ecelice forþ þurhwunedæ swa lange swa God wolde þæt cristen geleafa mid Eongolcynne
& se hæbbe Godes miltse & his halgra se þe þis mid Gode trymman wille & healdon.
Gyf þonne hwa sie þæt he þis on ænegum dæle wanian þence oððe brecan þanne næbbe he naþer ne
Þis wæs gedon beforan ðære gewitnesse þe hyro naman her bynyoþan gemearcode standað.
