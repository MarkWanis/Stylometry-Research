Leofan men, ic wille cyðan eow eallum, & þam huru þe hit ær nystan, hwanan seo bysn ærest aras þæt
Ure Drihten gescop & geworhte Adam þone forman man haligne & clænne & synleasne him sylfum to
He cwæð: Estote sancti, quia et ego sanctus sum.
Leofan men, we rædað on bocum þæt for Adames godnesse & for his halignesse God hine gelogode on
& næfre he ne swulte ne deað ne þolode ne sar ne sorge næfre ne gebide, nære þæt he syngode.
Ac sona swa he syngode & ðurh deofles lare breac forbodenes, sona God sylfa, þe is ealra bisceopa
Leofan men, bisceopas syndon to þam gesette on þisre worulde þæt hy georne sculon be Cristes bysene
And æfter þære bysne þe God sylf on Adame astealde, þe he hine for his halignesse & for his godnesse
And we lærað þæt ælc gefullod man sy æfter his fulluhte halig & ðurh þæs halgan fulluhtes halignesse
And we lærað þæt gehwa eac þa fæste swyðe rihtlice healde & hi swyðe georne smeage gelome.
And gif hwylc man þonne Godes lage swa swyðe abrece þæt he hine sylfne openlice wið God forwyrce
Understande eac cristenra manna gehwylc þæt þæt forbod huslganges & inganges into cyrican is eal
And ðeah æfre swa se man sy swyðor forsingod, swa he geornor & gelomor Godes hus sece dæges &
& æfre, swa he hine sylfne swyðor geeadmed on his dædbote, swa byð his dædbot Gode andfengre, &
And ure ælc mæg be woruldlican þingan eac georne gecnawan þæt gyf hwa hæfð his hlaforde sare
Ne huru ne bið na gebeorhlic þam þe wið God hæfð forworht hine sylfne ealles to swyðe mid openlicre
And ðonne mæg se biscop eac þæs mannes syngrina þurh Godes þafunge þe swyðor geliðian þe þus
Eala, leofan men, utan don ealle swa swa us þearf is, utan helpan ure sylfra, & utan anmodlice eallum
He is swyðe milde & him symle sy lof & wyrðmynt æfre to worulde, amen.

Ad hec igitur quis non tremescat, nisi qui uerbis diuinis non credit?
Non simus igitur tam saxei uel tam ferrei pectoris ut nos ista uerba non terreant.
