Þis synd þa madmas þe Adeluuold bisceop sealde into þam mynstre þe is Medeshamstede gehaten Gode
Þæt is þonne an Cristes boc mid sylure berenod & III rode eac mid sylure berenode, II sylurene
And antwentig is þara boca þe Adeluuold biscop gesealde into Burch.
Þæt is þonne Beda in Marcum, Liber miraculorum, Expositio Hebreorum nominum, Prouisio futurarum
Þonne is æt Farresheafde XVI weorcwurðe men & VIII iunge men & Witlesmere halfendel.
Þonne is Vndelum & to berewicum þarto gebyreð.
Þonne is Keteiringan.
Þonne sind þa fennas þe he bohte æt Æalsige & æt Vfige manige æt Wellan mid XIII oran.
Of þam twam hundredum þe secæð into Normannescros man ageaf to tioðunge into Medeshamstede
Of þam twam undredum ute on þam nesse þe Medeshamstede onstent man ageaf of six tunan swa man
Þonne letan þa tioþunge of þan XXIIII tunan man ageald to mynstre twa hund æcera sæd & III.
