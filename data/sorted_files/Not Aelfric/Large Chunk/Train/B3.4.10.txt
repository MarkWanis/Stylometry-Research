Incipit narrare miracula que facta fuerant ante aduentum Saluatoris domini nostri Iesu Christi.
Her sagað ymb ðas mæran gewyrd þe to þyssum dæge gewearð, þætte ælmihtig dryhten sylfa þas world
& to þyssum dæge, þe dæg is ælmihtiges dryhtnes gebyrdtid, ealne þysne middangeard mid niwan leohte
And be þysse gewyrde, þe we todæg weorðiað, fram worulde fruman ealle Godes halige sædon hie þæt
& þeah ðe hit ealle sædon & bodedon, hwæðre an Godes þæra halgena þæt eallra swiotelecost gesang be
Utan we blissian & gefeon on him.
For ðan ðe on þyssum dæge ealra cyninga cyning & ealra wealdendra waldend þysne middangeard for
Mitte þe hit þa þære eadegan tide nealæhte þætte dryhten lichomlice wolde wesan geboren, swiðe
Ærest geeode to þam ge þe he on geboren wæs þæt nænige men mid wæpnum gefeohtan ne meahton,
Swylce þæt eac geeode þætte siofon nihtum ær Crist geboren wære, þæt sio sunne æt midre nihte ongan
Þæt tacnode þæt he þas eorðlican sunnan nihtes scinende him to gisle beforan sende.
Swylce þæt eac geeode unmanegum nihtum ær Crist geboren wæs, onsprungon þry wyllas, & of þara
Swylce manega oðeru wundor ðær wæron gewordene ær þære tide þe he on geboren wæs, ða nu uneaðe
Ða þæt geeode þy sylfan dæge þe gyrsandæg wæs, þæs ðe dryhten on niht geboren wæs ær
Mitte þe hit þa wæs sio þridde tid þæs dæges þæ gyrsandæg wæs, he ða beseah on þa lyft ongean þa
Mitte ðe Agustus se casere þæt geseah, he cigde ofer eall þæt werud, & cwæð: Þæt is gesyne þæt þis is
For ðam he fram fruman middangeardes sæd wæs, on oðrum wundrum þa ymb his gebyrd acweden
Us ðonne nu gedafenað þæt we weorðien þone wuldorfæstan cyning se us his tacn swa swiotolice
& þa hraðe he abead þæt sended wære to stowa gehwylcre þara þe to his rice belumpe, & abeodan het
& þa to þære sylfan niht ær morgensteorra upeode, dryhten wæs geboren on ðysne middangeard, &
Mitte ðe Herodes se cyning geascode þæt Crist wæs geboren on Bethlem, Iudea byrig, he hine het secan
Ane dæge ær þan þe he Erodes se cyning hete þa cild cwellan, dryhtnes engel ætywde Iosepe on
Iosep þa dyde swa him beboden wæs.
& mitte þe hy ferdon, hy cwomon to sumum scrafe, & hie woldon restan on him.
Maria þa astag of ðam nytene, hæbbende hælend þæt cild on hire fæðme.
Þær wæron þry cnihtas mid Iosepe & mid Marian þære fæmnan samod siðiende.
Semninga þa uteodon of ðam scrafe manige dracan, þæt mitte ðe denum hy locedon on Egypta dune, &
& mytte þe hie comon to þære ceastre, hie nænigne cuðne næfdon mid hwam hie wunian meahton.
Hy becyrdon to þara goda temple, & mitte þe hio ineode, Maria hæbbende hælend þæt cild on hyre
Ðæt mitte wæs sæd Afradisio þam heretogan.
He þæder cwom mid ealle his werode, & he wæs ingangende on þæt templ, & he geseah eal þa
He eode to Marian þære fæmnan, & he gebæd hælend þæt cild, & he spræc to eallum his werode, &
& for þan us is þearf þæt we don swa ura god, þy læs his yrre & deaðes frecnes ofer us cume.
Æfter þan ða dryhtnes engel æteowde Iosepe & Marian, & him to cwæð: Hwyrfað eft to Iudea lande.
Ealle syndon deade þa þe sohton þæne cniht to acwellanne.
Hælend þa weox, & he wæs gestrangod gaste, & mægene & snytero he wæs gefylled mid Gode & mid
Him anum is wuldor & weorðmynd on worulda woruld.
Hwæt, we nu gehyrdon secgan hwylcnehwegu dæl ymb usses dryhtnes gebyrd, swylce eac ymb þa wun
Utan we nu eorne tilian þæt we þe selran syn þonne we þylleca bysena usses dryhtnes beforan us reccan
Utan we healdan sybbe & lufan betwiohs us, þonne gyldeð us God ece mede æt ussum ende.
