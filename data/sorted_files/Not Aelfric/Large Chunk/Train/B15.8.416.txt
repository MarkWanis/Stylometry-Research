Þonon on ðone norðmæstan weg.

Þonon andlang mearce on ðet wot treow æt ðære baran fyrhðe.
Þonnon on ðet fæster geat swa on ðet deope del ðonon on icæles æwilmas to æðelbrihtes mearce æt
Ðonon ut on ðone hæðfeld on fugel mere swa on bromhyste.
Þ andlang burnan on bedecanlea.
Ðonon ofer ealne ðære hæðfeld up to hnæfes scylfe.
Ðanan west andlang mearce to strete.
Þ west to ceolbrihtes stane.
Þ west on þa festændic.
Swa on ða mearce on eferæs cumb.
Ðæt andlang mearce on mules fen.
Þ on duddan broc andlang streames on brydan ford.
Þ on fearnlea ford.
Swa in on æscæsslep ford.
Andlang streames in on hrunigfealles wæt.
Ðonon andlang streames op to æmices oran.
Þ andlang weges to ðære wulfruscan.
Ðonan forð to ðon hæðfeld heale.
Andlang mearce oi ðet hig geat.
Ðon on lilles beam.
Ðonan forð on ða mearce to beonet legæ gæmære.
Swa on ðone hæðenan byrgels.
Ðonan west on ða mearce þær ælfstan lið on hæðenan byrgels þ on badecan dæne.
Swa forð on sibbes weg.
Ðonan wulfstanes mearce æt weargeburnan.
Ðon on cannæn dene westewearde ðon on pattan dene westewearde.
Ðon on heglea to ceoleages treowe.
Ðon forð on ða dupan furh þær deocca berena stodan.
Swa inon wifæles mære.
Þ in on ðone to brocænan beorð.
Swa on hamstedes wyllas norðewearde.
Ðon eft inon isenhyrstengeat.
