Ic Wulfwaru bidde minne leofan hlaford æþelred kyning him to ælmyssan þæt ic mote beon mines
Ic kyðe þe, leof, her on ðisum gewrite hwæs ic geann into Baðum to Sancte Petres mynstre for mine
Þæt is þonne þæt ic geann ðæder into ðære halgan stowe anes beages is on syxtigum mancussum goldes
And ic geann Ælfere abbode þæs landes æt Ferscesforda mid mete & mid mannum & mid eallre tylðe
And ic geann Wulfmære minum yldran suna þæs landes æt Clatfordtune mid mete & mid mannum and
& þes landes æt Budancumbe ic geann him healfes mid mete & mid mannum & mid eallre tilðe healfes
& dælon hi þæt heafodbotl him betweonan swa rihte swa hi rihtlicost magon þæt heora ægðer his gelice
And Ælfwine minum gyngran suna ic geann þes landes æt Leage mid mete & mid mannum & mid
And ic geann Godan minre yldran dehter þes landes æt Wunfrod mid mete & mid mannum & mid
And Alfware minre gyngran dehter ic geann ealles þæs wifscrudes þe þer to lafe bið.
And Wulfmære minum suna & Ælfwine minum oðrum suna & Alfware minre dehter, heora þreoðra
And ic geann Wulfmære minum suna anes heallwahriftes & anes beddreafes.
Ælfwine minum oðrum suna ic geann anes heallreafes & anes burreafes mid beodreafe & mid eallum
And ic geann minum feower cnihtum Ælmære & Ælfwerde & Wulfrice & Wulfstane anes bendes on
And ic geann eallum minum hiredwifmannum to gemanum anes godes casteneres wel gerenodes.
And ic wylle þæt þa þe to minre are fon þæt hi findon twentig freotmanna, tyne be eastan & tune be
Swylc heora swylce þis gelæste hæbbe he Godes milze and mine.
And swylc heora swylce þis gelæstan nelle, hæbbe he hit him wið ðone Hehstan gemæne þæt is se soðæ
