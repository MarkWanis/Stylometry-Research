& god cwæð to him ic eom se stranga god þines fæder; Ne ondræd þu þe Ac far to egypta lande
Ic fare nu ðyder mid þe & þanon ic þe læde.
Iacob aras þa fram þam pytte & his suna hine naman mid litlingum & mid heora wifum & mid þam
& ealle þa þing þe he ahte on chanaan lande & he com þa to egypta lande mid eallum his kynne.
Soðlice þis synd israhela naman þe uoran in to egypta lande he mid his sunum Se frumcenneda ruben.
& rubenes sunu Enoh & fallu & charm.
Simeones suna Gamuel & Diamin & achod & iachim & Saher & Sauw, Chana Nytedes suna.
& Leuies suna ierson & caað & merari.
Iudas suna Her & onham & Sela & fares & saram her & onham forðferdon on chanaan lande; &
Þis wæron isachares suna, Thola & phua & iob & semron.
& ðis wæron zabules suna, Sared & elon & iaelel.
Ðys synd lian suna þe heo akende on mesopotamia sirie myd dian hire dehter þæt wæron ealra III &
Ðis wæron gaades suna, sephio & Thagis Suni & essebon her & arodi & areli.
Ðis wæron asseras suna, Gamme & iessui & iesua & bæria & sara heora sweostor.
Ðis wæron racheles suna Iacobes wiues, Iosep & beniamin.
& Iosep gestrynde suna on Egypta lande be Asenehte Putiphares dohter þæs sacerdes of Eliopoleus
Ðis wæron Beniamines suna, Bela & Bethor & Arbel Iera & Naam, Rehi & Rosmoim & Opfam &
Rachel akende be Iacobe XIII suna.
Þus hatte Danis suna, Vsim.
Þis wæron Naptalimes suna, Hasiel & Gum & Iesser & Salem.
Witodlice Iacob ferde LXX sum to Egypta lande.
Þa sænde he Iudas beuoran him to Iosepe þæt he sceolde cyðan him þæt he come ongean hine to
Þonne he ðyder come Iosep þa gearwode his carm & for ongean his fæder & þa he hine geseah he
& Iacob cwæð, nu ic mæg swyltan blyðelice nu ic þe geseo & þe mine æhta betæce.
& Iosep cwæð þa to his gebroðrum & to ealle his hiwrædene, Ic fara nu & cyðe faraone þæt ge
& ic secge him þæt ge synd hyrdas & habbað gebroht mid eow eal þæt ge ahton.
& þonne he eow clypað & axað eow hwæt ge don cunnon.
Þonne secge ge him þæt ge synd sceaphyrdas fram cildhade ge we ge eowre fæderas oþ þisne
Ða eode Iosep in to faraone & cwæð to him, Min fæder & mine gebroðra & hyra sceaphirdas & hyra
Witodlice þa his V gingestan gebroðra beuoran þam kyninge.
Ða axode he hi hwæt hi wyrcan cuðe hi andswerodon him & cwædon we synd sceaphyrdas þine
We comon to þam hider þæt we wunedon on þinum lande forðam þe we nabbað nan gærs urum
Ða cwæð se cyningc to Iosepe, ðin fæder & þine gebroðra syndon cumene hyder.
Þu canst Egypta land geloga hig on þam selestan healf & syle heom gessen land gyf þu wite þæt hig
Æfter þisum lædde Iosep his fæder in to þam kyninge & sette hine beuoran him; þa bletsode se kining
Ða andswerode he him & cwæð an hund wintra.
& se cyningc hine bletsode & he eode ut.
Iosep sealde his gebroðrum tun on Egypta lande on þam selestan ende Ramases eal swa farao him
& fedde hi & ealle his fæder hiwrædene.
Soðlice hlaues wæs wana on eallum ymbhwyrftum & hungor fornam swyðost Egypte & Chanaan
& Iosep hæfde gegaderod eal þæt feoh þe hi ahton & gebroht on þæs kynges madmhuse.
Þa þa heora feoh ateorade þa com eall Egipta folc to Iosepe & cwædon to him, Syle us mete hlauord
He andswerade heom þa & cwæð, driuað hider eower orf gif ge feoh nabban & ic eow sylle mete þar
& hi dydon swa & he sealde heom mete wið horsum & wið hryðerum & wið sceapum & wið assum
Þa comon hi eft to him on þam æftran geare & cwædon, Ne hele we þe hlauord þæt we nabbað naðer
