Þeah we nenge þinga magen becuman to eallum þam megenum his weorca forþon þe in him swa swiðe

Ða com þeodorus se ercebiscop on breotone ealond.
He wes onsended fram þam apostolican selde & mid þy he manegu ealond geondferde & ealle þing
He in gelumpenlicum stowum biscopas halgede & þa þe he unmedume gemette þes godes geleafan he
He eac Ceaddan þone biscop erest swiðe þreade & segde þet he unrihtlice gehalgod were.
He him Ceadda andswearede þere eadmodestan stefne & cweð, gif þu me on wite þet ic unrihtlice þone
Ac ic for hersumnesse intingan geneded þone had underfeng.

Ða ontynde se biscop Ceadda þet egðyrl þes gebedhuses & hof his honda upp swaswa he foroft

Mittes heo þa swa comon þa manode he Ceadda heo erest þet hi lufan & megen & sibbe him
Syððan he him þa underþeodde & cyðde þone deg his forðfore þe þa iu him neh stod.

Ða answarude se biscop Ceadda him & cweð, Gif þu songes stefne geherdest & heofonlicne þreat

Heo comon to þere gemyndæ þes arwurðestan biscopes Ceaddan.
Ða cweð sanctus ægberht, Ic wat enne man in þisum ealonde mittes se halga wer Ceadda se biscop
Ðonne hweðere us þet wunað uncuð hweðer he hit be him seolfum segde hweðer þe be oðrum men
Sancte Ceadde forðferda in þam dege sexta nonas martis.
& he wes erest bebyrged be sancta Marian cirican ac efterþon þer mon getimbrade cirican &
In gehweðre þera stowa gelomlico helo & wundra beoð gewrohte to cuðnesse his megena.
Hit gelomp þet sum woda se eall þet lond dwoligende geondearn þet he on efenne þider becom swa þa
& he cudde eallum þam mannum wundrigendum hwet him þer drihten to helo forgifen hefde.
Seo stow þere byrgene wes treowene þruh & heo wes gewarht ufan on huses gelicnesse.
& þer stondeþ wigbed be þere þruh þet hafeð þyrel on þam wage þurh þet gewuniað þa men þe þa
& mittes hit mon in weter sendað & seleð untruman horsum oððe nutenum oððe mannum to byrgenne
In þere stowe þeodorus gehadode Wynferð, godne wer & gemetfestne swa his forgengan mercna
Se Wynferð wes of preosthade þes biscopes þe he æfterfylgede & he diacones þegnunge under him
Genihtsumien us nu, men þa leofestan, þas þe us segd earun be þam arwurðan biscope sancte Ceaddan
Eale, þet wes eadig wær in þam ne wes enig inwit ne he nenigne fordemde ne he nenigne gehende ne
& mittes he wes heh biscop on orleahtre & swilce þeah þe he fram untrumum & unwisum preostum
Ac he a an & þet ilce sume gemete heofonlice blisse ber on his onseone.
Nes nefre in his muðe nympðe crist nympðe mildheortnis.
