And mid ðy ðe heo ðys gecweden hæfde, ða com ðær sona se eadega Iohannes, and slog on ða duru
And geseah þæt Sancta Maria wæs gedrefedu on hire gaste, and heo sworette; and heo ne mihte
He gewat fram us, þæt he wolde ðrowian for middaneardes hælo.
And ða cwæð se halga Iohannes to hire Hwæt wilt ðu þæt ic ðe doo?
Ða andsworede him seo halige Maria, and wæs cwæðende, Ne bidde ic ðe nanes ðinges elles buton þæt
Ða gehyrde se halga Iohannes þæt ða Iudeas cwædan, Uton we nu gan, and acwellan ða apostolas, and
Mid ðy ðe ðis gehyrde se eadiga Iohannes, ða wæs he cweðende, Of ðissum dæge he gewat fram us of
And ða cegde seo halige Maria to ðem halgan Iohanne on hire hordcofan, and him æteowde ealne hire
And heo him æteowde ealle hire medomnesse, and cwæð to him, Ic ðe bidde, Fæder Iohannes, þæt ðu
Ða cwæð se halga Iohannes to hire, Ne mæge ic þæt ana don; ac her cumað mine efnapostolas to me,
Mid ðy ðe he ðis gecweden hæfde, ða wæs he gongende of hire hordcofan; ða wæs semninga
And ða semninga ealle ða apostolas tugon hie upp mid wolcnum, and sume hie wæron gesette beforan
