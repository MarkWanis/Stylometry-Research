Ic Agatho papa of Rome grete wel seo wurðfulle æðelred Myrcene kyning & se ærcebiscop Theodorum
& seo Myrcene biscop Saxulf seo ær wæs abbot.
& alle þa abbotes þa sindon on Englalande Godes gretinge & minre bletsunge.
Ic haue geheord seo kyninges æðelredes geornunge & þes ærcebiscopes Theodorus, & þes biscopes
And ic bebeode of Godes half & Sancte Petres, & ealra halgan, & ealre hadode heafde, þet ne kyning ne
Ic beode æc þæt þe scyrbiscop ne seo swa dyrstlece þæt he na hading ne haleging ne do on þis abbotrice
& ic wille þæt seo abbot beo gehealden for legat of Rome ofer eal þæt iglande, & hwilc abbot þe beþ
Ic wille & tyðe þæt whilc man swa haued behaten to faren to Rome, and he ne muge hit forðian, ouðer
& haue þæt ilce forgiuenesse of Criste & Sancte Peter, & of þonne abbot & of þone muneca þæt he
Nu bidde ic þe broðer Theodorus þæt þu lete bedon geond æl Englelande þet seo sinað wurðe
Al swa ic beode þe Saxulf biscop þæt swa swa þu hit geornest, þæt seo mynstre beo freo, swa ic
Nu wille ic hit segge mid worde þæt hwa swa halt þis write & þis bode.
Þa wurðe he efre wuniende mid God ælmihti on heuenrice, & hwa swa hit to breceð, þa wurðe he

Ða heot seo kining þone ærcebiscop Theodorus þæt he scolde setton ealle gewitenemot æt þone stede
Ða hi wæron þær gegaderod, þa leot he rædon þa gewrite þe seo papa þider seonde, & ealle hit getton &
Ða seide se kyning, Ealle þa þing þe min broðer Peada, & min broðer Wulfere, & mine swuster
& ic wile on min dæi hit æcon for here sawle, & for minre sawle.
Nu gife ic Sancte Peter to dæi in to his minstre Medeshamstede þas landes, & eal þæt þær to liggeð, þet
Ðas landes ic gife Sancte Peter eal swa freolice swa ic seolf hit ahte, & swa þæt nan min æftergengles
Gif hwa hit doð, þes papa curs of Rome & ealre biscope curs he habbe, & here ealre þe her be
& þis ic festnie mid Cristes tacne.
