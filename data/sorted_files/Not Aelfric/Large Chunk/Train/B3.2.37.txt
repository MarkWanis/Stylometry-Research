Feria Tertia De Letania Maiore men ða leofestan þys syndon halige dagas and halwendlice us to
Utan geþencan hu ælmihtig God for us þrowode and geþafode: ærest þa he wæs on heofona rices hyhðu
And he ða her on worulde wæs þrowigendlic monn, and gyt he hine sylfne geeadmette to þon þæt he
Ærest he þæt geþafode his agenum willan, þæt hine mann for ure þearfe genam and geband, and he
And eall he þæt eadmodlice for ure þearfe geþrowode and micle maran and mænigfealdre þing þonne ic
And þæt her ealra mæst is, swa ic ær sæde, þæt hine man for ure ealra neodþearfe and for ealles
And he þa his þæt halige heafod onhylde and his gast on his Fæder hand ageaf and on his geweald
And eac to helwarum nyþer astah and ðone ealdan wiðerweardan feond þær geband and getigde; and
Forðon, men þa leofestan, us is swyðe mycel þearf þæt we ealle þa þing geþencan and asmeagan
And we symle geornlice his þa halgan and þa godcundan bebodu agan to gefyllanne and to gehealdenne
Ne syndon nænie on þissere worulde ne on þissum life þæs fæstlice and ðæs mislice þing þæt hi symle
Ac uton we micle swyðor habban godcunde lufe betwyx us; forðam þe ic sæde ær, for urum lufon
Uton we þonne forðon, men þa leofestan, geornlice tiligean þæt we for his lufe and for his willan us
Ac utan hyhtan us and þeodan us to ælmihtigum Gode and to his are and to his forgyfnysse.
Forðon se sealmscop þæt lærde and swa cwæð, Cumað ge and uton us gebiddan and forðgangan
And uton aweorpan fram us þone yfelan and ðone grimman gewunan and us mid godcunde mægne
leofan men, ac efston we mid fæstenum and mid ælmesdædum þæt us seo hell ne forswelge.
God ælmihtig is swyðe mildheort and manþwære.
Ac hwæðere he on domesdæg byð swyðe reþe and swyðe rihtwis; and þy he bið domesdæg gehaten
Send þonne his englas to feower dælum þisses middaneardes and ðær his ða heofoncundan byman
Þonne bið eal þes middaneard swyðe onhrered and onstyred; duna myltað swa weax deð on fyre, sæs
And þonne oðre siðe ure Drihten hider on þisne middaneard becymeð to demanne mid mycelre menio
And he þonne his þæt heofonlice werod him on þa swyðran healfe gesetteð and þa manfullan beoð on
And he þonne on his fotum and on his handum þa dolhswaþu forð æteoweð, and ðus sprecende bið and
Oððe forhwan noldest ðu þysne þyrnenan helm me of þæm heafde alysan, to þan þæt ic ðe of
And he þonne eft oðre siðe swyðe mycclum and swyðe reðum wordum sprecende bið to þæm þe him
Ne cweð he þonne na þæt eow wæs gegearwod fram fruman þisses middaneardes, ac he cwyð þæt wæs
Ðær is þonne heof and wop and cyrm and granung and cwanung and toða gristbitung, and ðær is seo
And ðær is wanunga and wita ma þonne æniges mannes earan ahlystan magon ne nanes mannes tunga
And nan þæra wita mare ne byð þonne þæt ure Drihten him næfre eft on gemyndum ne byð ne ne
þæt is yrres dæg and æfstes dæg and þæt is susle dæg and þæt is se bitera bifigenda dæg and se
Tylian we nu forðon, men þa leofestan, mid fæstenum and mid ælmesdædum and mid cyriclicum
He cweð, Cumað ge gebletsode, þe mine bebodu heoldon, and onfoð freolice mines Fæder rice þæt
