Rixiendum ussum Dryhtene þem Helendan Criste, ëfter ðon þe agan was ehta hund wintra & syx &
& heo þa þær smeadan hu heo ryhtlicast heora þeodscipe ægþer ge for Gode ge for weorlde gehealdan
Ða cydde Werferð biscop þam weotum, þæt him wære forneh eall þæt wudulond ongereafad þe to
& þæt sæde þæt hit wære sum genumen to Bislege, sum to æfeningum, sum to Scorranstane, sum to
Þa cwædan alle þa weotan þæt mon uðe þære circan ryhtes swa wel swa oþerre.
& ða sona was Eðelwald þæs wordes þæt he no þes rihtes wiðsacan wolde, & sæde þæt Aldberht &
& heht his geneat, Ecglaf hatte, ridan mid Ceastersetna preoste Wulfhun hatte.
& he hine þa gelædde all ða gemæru swa he him of þam aldan bocum rædde, hu hit ær æþelbald cyning
Ða wilnede æþelwald swa þeh to þam biscope & to þam higen, þæt heo him mildemode alefdan þæt he
& heo hit woldon habban on his læne & hina.
& he næfre ne heora nowðer hine bereafian wolde þære mæstreddene þe he him alefed hæfde on
Ond he þa æþelwald þæt word gecwæð þæt hit aa hæfde ofer Godes est, þe hit hæfde butan þære circan
Gif hit þonne geberie þæt Alhmund swa ða freondreddene haldan nolde, oððe hine mon oferricte þæt he
Þis wæs gedon on æþelredes gewitnesse aldermonnes & on æþelflæde & æþulfes aldermonnes &
& þus se Ceastersetna preost hit gerad & se æþelwaldes geneat mid hine.
Ærost on Gemyðlege & þanon on Roddanbeorg silfne, þanon on Smececumb, þanon on Sengetlege,
Þus him gewisede se æþelwaldes mon þa gemæru swa him þa aldan bec ryhtan & wisedon.
