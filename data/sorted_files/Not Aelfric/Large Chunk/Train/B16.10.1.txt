Her swutelað on þissere Cristes bec hwæt Leofric biscop hæfð gedon innto sancte Petres minstre on
Þæt is þæt he hæfð geinnod þæt ær geutod wæs þurh Godes fultum & þurh his forespræce & þurh his
Ðonne ys þis se eaca on landum þe he hæfð of his agenum þæt mynster mid gegodod for his hlaforda
& he ne funde þa he to þam mynstre feng nan mare landes þe ðider ynn gewylde wære þonne twa hida
Ðonne ys þis seo oncnawenn is þe he hæfð God mid gecnawen & sanctum Petrum into þam halgan
Þæt is þæt he hæfð þider ynn gedon ii biscop roda & II mycele gebonede roda butan oðrum litlum
And III cantercæppa & III canterstafas & V pællene weofodsceatas & VII oferbrædelsas & II tæppedu
And II mycele gebonede candelsticcan & VI læssan candelsticcan gebonede & I silfren storcylle mid
And nu þær sind XVI upphangene & XII handbella & II fulle mæssebec & I collectaneum & II
And II salteras & se þriddan saltere swa man singð on rome & II ymneras & I deorwyrðe bletsingboc &
And martyrlogium & I canon on leden & I scriftboc on englisc & I full spelboc wintres & sumeres &
& he ne funde on þam mynstre þa he tofeng boca na ma buton ane capitularie & I forealdodne nihtsang
& þus fela leden boca he beget innto þam mynstre, liber pastoralis & liber dialogorum & libri IIII
& ofer his dæg he ann his capellam þider binnan forð mid him silfum on eallum þam þingum þe he silf
