Ðis synt þa land ge mero ðara XXX hida æt Wyttannige.
Ærest of hafoces hlewe on wenrisc on þa wiðig rewe on hnut clyf.
Of þam clyfe on hean leage.
Þ on lungan leage weg.
Andlang weges.
Þonne on swon leage þonne on swon weg.
Andlang weges þ hit sticað on norðe weardum cynges steorte.
Ða non on suga rode.
Andlang rode on huntena weg.
Andlang weges þ hit sticað æt wic ham.
Ða non a be wyrt wale on ofling æcer.
Þo non on ealdan weg Andlang weges on cycgan stan.

Andlang weges.
Þonne on yccenes feld.
Of yccenes felda on ða hege rewe.
Andlang hege rewe on met sinc.
Andlang met sinces on ecgerdes hel ufeweardne æfter wyrt walan on wenric andlang wenricces on
Þo non æfter ge mære on tidreding ford.
Ðanon on occan slæw.
Þa non on wittan mor suðe wearðne þo non on colwullan broc of þam broce on þa ealdan dic.
Of ðære dic on fugel sled of þam slede on þa stan bricge.
Æfter broce on þane ealdan weg of þam wege on horninga mære ða non on wæredan hlinc suðeweardne
Ðanon on lythlan eorþbeorg of þære byrig on þa onheafda of þas onheafdon on cytel wylle of ðas wylle
Andlang strete on hafoces hlew of þam hlewe eft on hnut clif ðer hit ær aras.
