In nomine Domini. Ic Eadwerd cyning begeat æt Denulfe biscepe & æt ðæn hiwun on Winteceastre þa
& ic let be ealra Westsexna witena geðeahte & leafe to biscepe & to þan hiwun Sancte Andreas cirican
Ðonne is ðis se eaca ðe eallra Westseaxna witan ðærtoeacan me gebocodon on ece erfe.
Ærest suðrichte fron ðan beodærn to Sancte Gregories cirican, ðonne from ðære suðwesthyrnan Sancte
Ðonne is ðæs ymbganges ealles ðrio furlanges & ðreo metgeurda.
