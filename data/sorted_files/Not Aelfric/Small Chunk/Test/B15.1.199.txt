Willelm kyng gret his biscopes & his eorles & his scyrgerefan & ealle his thegnas frencisce & englisce
And ic kythe eow thaet ic ann & wille thaet Walkelin bisceop beo his bisceoprices weorthe & aelc thaera
And his sace & his socne toll & team & infangene theof binnan burh & butan nu swa full & swa forth
And ic nellan gethafian thaet aenig man beo swa deorf that him fram hande drage heonanforth aening
Forthan the ic wille thaet se bisceop habbe his land & his men & ealle his thing mid micelan
