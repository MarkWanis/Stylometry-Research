On þ gerad wyrce þ he wyrce.
Þ þæt land sy unforworht into þære halgan stowe.
Si hyt ælces þinges freoh buton ferdfore & walgeweorce & brycgeweorce.
Sancta Maria & sanctæ Michahel cum sancto Petro & eallum Godes halgum gemildsige þis
Gif hwa butan gewyrhtum hit abrecan wylle hæbbe he wið God gemæne on þam ytemestan þisses lifes
Her is seo hand seten.
