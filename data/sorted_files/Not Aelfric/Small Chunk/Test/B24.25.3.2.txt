Her onginð embe þises middaneardes yldo.
Seo forme yld is fram Adame oð noe, þæt is þonne IX hund wintra & LXXVI wintra.
Ðonne is seo oþer yld fram noe oð abraham þæt is þonne CCC wintra & XCVII wintra.
Ðonne is seo þridda yld fram abrahame oð moisen þæt is þonne IX hund geara & LXX geara.
Ðonne is seo feorðe yld fram moysen oð dauid, þæt is þonne CCCC geara, & XCIIII geara þæs
Ðonne is seo fifte yld fram dauid oð Cristes tokyme þæt is þonne fif hund geara & LXXXIX geara.
Ðonne is seo sixte yld fram Cristes akennednysse oð þissere worulde ende.
Ðonne fram middaneardes fruman oð Christes akennednysse, þæt is þonne six þusend geara & C geara
