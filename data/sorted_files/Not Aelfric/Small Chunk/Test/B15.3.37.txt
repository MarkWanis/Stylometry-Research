On Tætlinctune & æt Neoweboldan, Eadrice.
In usses drihtnes noman Hælendes Cristes, ic Oswald arcebiscop mid geþafunge & leafe þæs arwyrðan
Þæt is þæt he hæbbe & wel bruce his dæg & æfter his dæg anum erfewarde þæm þe him leofest se & him
& he hit hæbbe tofrion ælces þingces butan geweorce & brycggeworce & ferdsocne.
Þis wæs gedon þu geare þe agan wæs fron Cristes gebyrdtide nigon hund wintra & an & hundnigonti
Ic Oswald ercebiscop mid Cristes rode tacne þas sylene gefestnade.
