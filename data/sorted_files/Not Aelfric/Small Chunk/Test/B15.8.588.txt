Ærest on eoccen æt sandfordinga gemære.
Swa ut on gean stream to snitan ige.
On norð healfe þæs igeðes.
And swa on baccan mor middeweardne.
Swa andlang mores on fisclace innan crypeles heale.
And þ andlang fearnbroces.
Þ hit sticað up on þa readan dic.
Swa andlang dic þ on hlydan.
Andlang hlydan on bradan mor.
Of bradan more to preosthamme up on myos wyllan.
Of myos wyllen on þene hricg weg.
Andlang hrycgeweges to þam cristes mæle.
Þonne suð þ hit sticoð eft on Eoccen þ hit onfeng.
