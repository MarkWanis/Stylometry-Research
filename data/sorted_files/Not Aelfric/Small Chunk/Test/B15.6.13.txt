Her swutelað on ðam cwide ðe ægelfled gecweden hæfð God to lofe and hire saule to þerfe and hire
Þæt is ðonne ða feower hida landes at Lagefare and twa hida æt Cochamstede ðe hy gean for hire saule
Þæt is ðonne ægelnoð arcebiscop and Wulfstan arcebiscop and ælfun biscop on Lundene and ælfric
And swa hwilc man swa þisne cwide awende sy he Iudas gefere ðe urne Drihten belewde on helle wite.
