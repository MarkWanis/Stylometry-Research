H þurh godes gevu ænglelandes cining grete ealle mine biscopes & mine eorles & sciregereuan &
And ic kyðe eow þæt ic hæbbe heom geunnon þæt hi by on ælce þare lande wurþe þe hi hæfdon on
And saca and socnæ on strande & on streame on wudan and on feldan, tolnes & teames griðbreces &
Forþan þingan þe ic hæbbe criste þas gerihte forgeuen minre sawle to alysednesse ealswa Eadward
And ic nelle ge ðauian þæt ænig man þis to brece be minan fullan freondscipe.
God eow gehealde.
