Se ðe me rædan ðance.
He in me findan mæg, gif hine feola lysteð gastlices lifes godre biesene, þæt he ful eaþe mæg upp
Þæt mæg se mon begytan, se þe his modgeðanc æltowe byþ and þonne þurh his ingehygd to þissa
Me awritan het Wulfstan bisceop, þeow and þearfa þæs, þe alne þrym aof, and eac walden is wihta
Bideþ þe se bisceop, se þe ðas boc begeat, þe þu on þinum handum nu hafast and sceawast, þæt þu him
And þæt him God ællmihtig forgyue þa gyltas, þe he geworhte, and eac resðe mid him, se ðe ah ealles
Her ongynneð se æresða stream þære clænan and hlutran burnan þurh þone halegan breosð ures
