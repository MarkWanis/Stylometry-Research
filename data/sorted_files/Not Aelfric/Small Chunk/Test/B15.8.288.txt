Þis synd þa land gemæra to Hamtune.
Vpp of afene on mærbroc on ðryllen stan upp on gerihte on herces næs.
Suðe wearde andlang mærhagan on herces dic.
Andlan dic on herces get.
Of þam gete on Cyninga þyrnan.
Eastewerde andlang weges on hemlec lege eastewearde andlang stige on ulfan treow west andlang weges
Upp andlang stremes þ eft upp on mær broc.
