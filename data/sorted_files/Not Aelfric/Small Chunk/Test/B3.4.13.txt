Sanctus beda was iboren her on breotene mid us & he wisliche writen awende þæt þeo englisc leoden
& he þeo cnotten unwreih þe questiuns hoteþ þa derne digelnesse þe deorwurþe is.
Ælfric abbod þe we alquin hoteþ he was bocare & þe fif bec wende, Genesis, Exodus, Vtronomius,
Þurh þeos weren ilærde ure leoden on englisc þet weren þeos biscopes þeo bodeden cristendom, wilfrid
Næs deorc heore liht ac hit fæire glod.
Nu beoþ þeo leore forleten & þet folc is forloren, nu beoþ oþre leoden þeo læreþ ure folc & feole of þen
Nu sæiþ ure drihten þus, Sicut aquila prouocat pullos suos ad uolandum & super eos uolitat.
Þis beoþ godes word to worlde asende, þæt we sceolen fæier feþ festen to him.
