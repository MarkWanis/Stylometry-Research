Þis is ealhhelmes land boc þe eadgar cing him gebocode on ece yrfe æt eastun.
Ærest on wulferdes treo of þam hlawe.
Andlang þæs leas on þa stræte.
In þæs cynges ge mære of þære stræte in ebban mor.
Of æbban more in þara hina ge mære.
Swa andlang hina ge mæres in burcels.
Of þam byrcelse on wrocene.
Swa andlang wrocene in upping hæma ge mæra.
Swa andlang þæs ge mæres in þa wellan.
Swa of þæra wyllan in þone mor.
Swa of þam more in þone broc.
Swa andlang þæs mores in þone dic.
On upping hæma ge mæra.
Andlang dices on wæclinga stræte.
Swa of þære stræte on þone lea.
Swa andlang þæs leas.
Þ hit cymð æft on þone stan æt tan hlaw æt wulfherdes treo.
