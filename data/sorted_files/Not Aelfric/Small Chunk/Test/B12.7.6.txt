Ad uesperum..
On æfen we sculon God herian, on ðone timan man offrode on þære ealdan æ and mid recelsreocan on
And on æfentiman ure drihten offrode æt his æfengereorde and dælde his discipulum þurh halig geryne
And on æfentiman hit wæs þæt Ioseph Cristes lichaman of rode alinode.
Þonne we agen myccle þearfe þæt we swylc gemunan and Gode þancian and on þone timan ure gebedu
