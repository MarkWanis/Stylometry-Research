Willem king grett Elred arcebiscop and Wlstan biscop and Willem eorll and alle mine thegnes on
And icc kithe eow that icc wille that tha land Perssore and Dorhurst the Edward king se uthe Crist and
And icc nelle se thafian any mann that thas se utthe any thare thnge the thar to se birth be minen fullen
God eow alle se healde.
