Ðis sindon ða land gemæro ærest on ðane blacan pyt of ðan pytte þ upon þ eaþland eal swa ðe oxa went
Of ðam wege in ðone lytlan graf ðonene in ða ealdan dic.
Ðonene þ in grobbes dene.
Ðanene on ða portstræt of ðan wege in mærford of ðan forda in hnutfen.
Of ðan fenne in eald cotan þ to graf hrygce.
Of ðan hrygce in ðane wic weg of ðan wege on uffan lege.
Of ðære lege on lytlan broc ðanene on hymelbroc andlang broces þ op of ðan broce in Egcbyrhttigc croft
