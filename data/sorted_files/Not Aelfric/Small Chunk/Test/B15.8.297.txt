Þis is þara V hyda boc æt eblesburnan þe eadwig cing gebocode ælfrice his þægne on ece yrfe.
Ðis synd þa land ge mere to eblesburnan þonne geð hit ærest on bican bricge of þere bricge on beor dune
Of menandene on land sceargeat.
Of land sceorgeate on ludan beorh.
Of ludan beorge on eblesburnan up on stream æt bican bricge.
