Ðis is ðæt frið, ðæt ælfred cyninc & Gyðrum cyning & ealles Angelcynnes witan & eal seo ðeod ðe on
Ærest ymb ure landgemæra: up on Temese, & ðonne up on Ligan, & andlang Ligan oð hire æwylm,
Ðæt is ðonne, gif man ofslægen weorðe, ealle we lætað efen dyrne Engliscne & Deniscne, to VIII
& gif man cyninges ðegn beteo manslihtes, gif he hine ladian dyrre, do he þæt mid XII cininges ðegnum.
Gif ma ðone man betyhð, ðe bið læssa maga ðone se cyninges ðegn, ladige he hine mid XI his gelicena
& gyf he ne dyrre, gylde hit ðrygylde, swa hit man gewyrðe.
& þæt ælc man wite his getyman be mannum & be horsum & be oxum.
& ealle we cwædon on ða dæge ðe mon ða aðas swor, þæt ne ðeowe ne freo ne moton in ðone here faran
