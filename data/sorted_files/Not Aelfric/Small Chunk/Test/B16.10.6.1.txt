Her cyþ on þissere bec þæt Gedmer Spernægles sune hæfð alised Leofilde his maga ut of Toppesham
And þærto is gewittnes, Ælwine preost, & Ailword Pudding, & Heording, & his broðras, & Wulword at
And eall þæt hundred at Toppeshamme.
And se þe þis undo, habbe Godes curs, & Sancta Maria, efre butan ende.
