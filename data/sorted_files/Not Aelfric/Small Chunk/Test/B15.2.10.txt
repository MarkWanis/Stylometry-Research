Ðis is sio gerædnes ðe Ælfwold & Eadwold & Æðelflæd geræddan.
Þ is þ Æðelflæd & Eadwold geuðen Ælfwolde anes dænnes wið his licwyrðan scætte ðe hio anfeng þ is
Ðis dæn is genamod Hyringdænn.
His terminibus circum acta ab oriente fram ðæm stænenan stapole on Tenet wara brocas .
& on hyring brocas a meridie hyringburne oð æscburnan.
Ab occidente escburna oð æscbrygg.
Ab aquilone sia rode fram æscbrygge eft an ðane stænenan stapol.
& se hioræd to Cristes ciricean.
& se hioræd to sancte Agustine & ða þreo geferscipas innan burhwara & utan burhwara & micle
& se hioræd to Apuldre.
& Eadelm, Birhtfreð, Ealdelm, Ealhfreð, Sielm.
& Ælfwold gesealde Eadwolde & his sunum C panæga anuppan all ðis oðer wiððan ðe hit hiora unna
Ðis is hyring dænnes boc ðe Eadwold & Æðelflæd geuðan Ælfwolde wið his licwyrðan scætte.
