Ðis swutelunge gedihte Oswald arcebiscop & awritan het.
Þis syndon þa tunas þe syndan adon of Ottanlege.
An ys Haddincham, oþer ys Hyllicleg, þridde Mensinctun, feoþe healf Burhleg, fifte Gislicleh, sixfe
Þas tunas sindan adon of Rypum.
I ys Heawic, oþer Heawic, III Ansætleh, IIII Stanleh an hid, V Helperby, VI æt Myðtune, of Popeltune
Þis is genumen of Scirebunnan, Ceoredesholm healf, & Cawudu healf & Gisferþesdæll hyrðe æfre into
Þis syndan þa land þe Oscytel yrcebiscop begeat on Norþhymbralande mid his feo & him mon geald for
An ys Æffeltune, he gebohte mid XXIIII pundun æt Deorwulfe, Yferingaham, he gebohte mid feower
& æt Heolperbi him wæs gegolden for unrihtan hæmede, wæron twegen gebroþra hæfdon an wif, &
& æt Scyteby he gebohte mid XX pundun.
& þreo hida æt Bracenan he gebohte æt Eadgare cinge & he hit him gebocode into sancte Iohanne.
Ic Oswold arcebiscop swutelige þæt ealle þas land þe se yrcebiscop Oscytel begeat on
Þa wæs sancte Peter bereafod syððan, wrece God swa he wille.
