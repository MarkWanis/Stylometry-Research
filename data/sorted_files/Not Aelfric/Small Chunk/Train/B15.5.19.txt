Her swutelað on ðysum gewrite ymbe ða forwerda ðe Wulfric & se arcebisceop geworhtan ða he begeat
Þæt is ðæt he behet hyre þæt land æt Ealretune & æt Ribbedforda hire dæg.
& he behet hire þæt land æt Cnihtewican þæt he wolde hit hire begytan ðreora manna dæg æt ðam hirede
Nu wæs ðyses to gewitnesse Wulfstan arcebiscop & Leofwine ealdorman & Aeþelstan biscop &
