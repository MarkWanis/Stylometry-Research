In nomine domini.
Her swytelað on þisym gewryte hu Ælfsige abbod & se hired on niwan mynstre alendan Wlfmære an
& þisra twegra hida landgemæra sind, on ðæra bec ðe Drægtune uton be lið.
& his is gedon be Æðelredes cininges unnan & gewitnesse & Ælfrices arcebisceopes & Ælfheges
& his is on þæt gerad gedon þæt Wlfmære sy freond & hold into ðem mynstre on ælcere stowe ægðer
