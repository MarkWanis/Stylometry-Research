Her Eadgar wæs, Engla waldend, corðre miclum to cyninge gehalgod on ðære ealdan byrig,
Þær wæs blis micel on þam eadgan dæge eallum geworden, þone niða bearn nemnað and cigað
Þær wæs preosta heap, micel muneca ðreat, mine gefrege, gleawra gegaderod.
And ða agangen wæs tyn hund wintra geteled rimes fram gebyrdtide bremes cyninges, leohta hyrdes,
And him Eadmundes eafora hæfde nigon and XX, niðweorca heard, wintra on worulde, ða þis
