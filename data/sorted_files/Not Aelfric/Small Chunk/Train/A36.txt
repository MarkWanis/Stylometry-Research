Þis ærendgewrit Agustinus ofer sealtne sæ suðan brohte iegbuendum, swa hit ær fore adihtode dryhtnes
Ryhtspell monig Gregorius gleawmod gindwod ðurh sefan snyttro, searoðonca hord.
Forðæm he monncynnes mæst gestriende rodra wearde, Romwara betest, monna modwelegost, mærðum
Siððan min on englisc Ælfred kyning awende worda gehwelc, and me his writerum sende suð and norð,
