Aefter þam þe se mettruma his þing becwedene hæfð, underfo se mæssepreost his andetnesse and
And understande se sacerd hwæt se hælend þurh þone witegan cwæð, Gif þu þam unrihtwisan nylt his
And wite se sacerd þeahhwæðere, andette se untruma, þæt he andette mid Godes fultume þurh
Þæt he hine ne ofersyme ne on orwennesse ne bringe, ac him þus tocweðe, swa swa se hælend þurh
And eft he cwæð, þonne se synfulla his synna begeomrað, þonne wurð he gehealden.
And þy dæge þe se synfulla gecyrð fram his synnum to dædbote, þy dæge he leofað and nane swelteð.
Gyt he cwyð, Andette þe sylf þine unrihtwisnessa, þæt þu mæge beon gerihtwisod, for þam þe se sealm
Ðonne æfter þisse timbrunge þises drihtenlican behates, syððan se untruma nammælum his synna and
Nu andette ic minum drihtne and eallum his halgum and þe, þe min gastlice scrifte eart, þæt ic
Geswicenesse ic behate and æfter þinre tæcinge dædbetan wille mid eadmodlicre onhnigenesse, gif min
And ic bidde þe, þe min gastlica lareow eart, þæt þu me foreþingie and beforan mines drihtnes
Ðonne bletsige se preost axan and þa sceolon beon, gif man hæfð, of wingearde geworhte.
