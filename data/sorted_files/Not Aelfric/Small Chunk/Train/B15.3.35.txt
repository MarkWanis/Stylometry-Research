Æt Cumtune, Æþelmære.
In usses Drihtnes noman Hælendes Cristes, ic Oswald arcebisceop mid geþafunge & leafe þæs arwurðan
Þæt is þæt hæbbe & wel bruce his dæg & æfter his dæge twam erfeweardan þam þe him leofest sy &
& hio hit hæbben tofrion ælces þinges butan wealgeworce & brygcgeweorce & ferdsocne.
Þis synd þære anre hide landgemæru on Mersce, ærest of Æþestanes gemære to þam wylle on Biles
Þis wæs gedon þy geare þe wæs agan fram Cristes gebyrdtide nigon hund wintra & hundnigontig wintra
Ic Oswald arcebiscop mid Cristes rode tacne þas sylene gefæstnode.
& ic gean him þæs worðiges æt Brynes hamme þe Æþelm ahte & þæs croftes þærto be eastan þære
