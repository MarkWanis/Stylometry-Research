Ic Eadward king kiðe on ðise write alle lefful manne ðat ic red habbe wið mine erchebiscopes and
Ðat is ðat ic wille ðat seynt Eadmundes minster mine meyes, and se tun ðe ðat minster inne is, be ekelike
Ne neuer se stede ne wrðe loged mid oðere hodes manne ðanne mid moneke, ne non ðere biscop ðe
And ðo monekes chesen hem alder of hem seluen so so riht is.
And ic wille ðat se socne and se sake ðat ic hem unnen habbe stonde unawend euer so wer so he nu
Nu bid ic alle Godes frend eiðer ðe ðat nu libben ore ðe ðat after hus schulen comen ðat he for Godes
