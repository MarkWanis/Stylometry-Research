Her Eadward kingc, Engla hlaford, sende soþfæste sawle to Criste on godes wæra, gast haligne.
He on worulda her wunode þrage on kyneþrymme, cræftig ræda, XXIIII, freolic wealdend, wintra
Wæs a bliðemod bealuleas kyng, þeah he lange ær, lande bereafod, wunode wræclastum wide geond
Syððan forð becom freolice in geatwum kyningc kystum god, clæne and milde, Eadward se æðela, eðel
