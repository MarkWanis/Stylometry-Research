In ures Drihtnes naman Hælendes Cristes, ic Leofinc bisceop mid þafunge & leafe Hearðacnutes cynges
II hida æt Eadmunddescotan hæbbe he & wel bruce for his eadmodre gehersumnysse & for his licwurðan
& he hit hæbbe to freon ælces þinges butan wallgeweorce & brygcgeweorce & ferdsocne.
God ælmihtig þone gehealde þe þas ure sylena & ure gerædnyssa healdan wylle on ælce healfe.
Gif ænig þonne sy uppahofen & inblawen on þa oferhyda þære geættredan deofles lare & wylle þas ure
Ðis wæs gedon þy geare þe wæs agan fram Cristes gebyrtide an þusend wintra & twa & XLII wintra.
