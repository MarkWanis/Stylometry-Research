Ðis sind þa land gemæra æt Wilmanlehtune ærest æt cranmere & of þæn mære andlang broces.
And swa on þone þorn.
And of þan þorne andlang ðære stræte in on hore pyt of þan pytte on ðane blacan pyt þonan on langdune
Of ðæn hlæwe to þære sealtstræt & efter stræte to wilman forda.
Of wilman forde on cyneferþes pyt þonan in þa hwitan dic.
Of ðære dic andlang hricg weges þ in cran meres broce andlang broces in to cranmere.
