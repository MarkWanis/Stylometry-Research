Ic Oswulf aldormonn mid Godes gæfe ond Beornðryð min gemecca sellað to Cantuarabyrg to Cristes
Ond mid micelre eaðmodnisse biddað ðæt wit moten bion on ðem gemanon ðe ðaer Godes ðiowas
Ic ðonne Wlfred mid Godes gaefe archiepiscopus ðas forecuaedenan word fulliae, & bebeode ðæt mon
Ðonne bebeode ic ðaet mon ðas ðing selle ymb tuælf monað of Liminum, ðe ðis forecuaedene lond to
Ond of higna gemënum godum ðaer aet ham, mon geselle CXX gesuflra hlafa to aelmessan for hiora
Ond ðas forecuëdenan suësenda all agefe mon ðëm reogolwarde & he brytnië swæ higum maest red sie
Aec mon ðaet weax agæfe to cirican & hiora sawlum nytt gedoe ðe hit man fore doeð.
Aec ic bebeode minum aefterfylgendum ðe ðaet lond hëbben aet Burnan ðaet hiae simle ymb XII
Aec ic bidde higon ðette hie ðas godcundan god gedon aet ðere tide fore hiora sawlum, ðaet ëghwilc
Hire ond ëghwilc Godes ðiow gesinge twa fiftig fore his sawle, twa fore hire ðaette ge fore weorolde
Aec ic biddo higon ðaet ge me gemynen aet ðere tide mid suilce godcunde gode suilce iow cynlic
Valete in Domino. þis is gesetnes Osulfes & Biarnðryðe.
