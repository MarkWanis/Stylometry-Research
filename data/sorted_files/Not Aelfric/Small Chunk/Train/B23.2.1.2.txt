Ðry dagas synd on XII monðum, þa synd swiðe unhalwende monnum oððe nytenum blod on to
Þæt is se æftemæste monandæg on Aprilis, ær he gange of tune, and se forma monandæg on
Seðe on ðam III monandagum mon oððe nytene blod forlæteð, on þone ðridan dæg he sceal sweltan,
And gif he drinc drinceð to læcecrefte, ær XV dagan he sceal sweltan.
And gif hwylc man acenned bið on þissum III dagum, he sceal sweltan yfele deaðe.
And seðe et goseflæsc on þisson III dagan, ær XL dæge he sceal sweltan.
