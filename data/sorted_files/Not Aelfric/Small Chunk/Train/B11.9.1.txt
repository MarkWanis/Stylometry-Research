Ic andette þe, Drihten ælmihtig God, & sanctam Marian, þinre haligan modor, & eallum haligum & þe,
For þam ic bydde þe, sanctam Mariam, ures Drihtenes modor & sanctum Michaelem þone heahengel &
Gemyltsige eow se ælmihtiga God & forgife eow ealle eowre synna & alyse eow fram eallum yfelum
& gelæde us Crist, þæs lifiendan Godes sunu, into þam ecean life, Amen.
