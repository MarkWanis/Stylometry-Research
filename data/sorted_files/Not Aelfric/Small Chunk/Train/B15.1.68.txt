On eallweldandes drihtnes namen þe ealle þing geworhte and him seluum to anwelde hyld, and seld þam
Ic edward of his agenum þe he for his godnesse butan minre geearnunge me wealden led.
Ic sille and scher cweðe widuten ende for minre saule alysednys se ealle þa kirken tunes.
Landes, fredomes, ealswa hy hyranuppen on leden gewretene standeð in to holme ministere, þam abbete
