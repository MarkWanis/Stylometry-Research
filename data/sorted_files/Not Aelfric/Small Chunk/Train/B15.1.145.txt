Eadward kingc gret mine biscopes & mine eorles & ealle mine þegnas on Wigerccestresciren & on
& ic kiþe eow þæt ic habbe gegifan Criste & Sancte Petre into Westminstre Perscoran & Deorhurst &
& ic nelle geþafian þæt þar anigne man ænigne onsting habbe buton se abbut & þa gebroðera to Seinte
God eow gehealde.
