Gif mon biþ on wæterælfadle, þonne beoþ him þa handnæglas wonne and þa eagan tearige and wile
Do him þis to læcedome: eoforþrote, cassuc, fone nioþoweard, eowberge, elehtre, eolone, merscmealwan
Ofgeot mid ealaþ, do hæligwæter to, sing þis gealdor ofer þriwa: Ic benne awrat betest beadowræda, swa
Sing þis manegum siþum: Eorþe þe onbere eallum hire mihtum and mægenum.
Þas galdor mon mæg singan on wunde.
