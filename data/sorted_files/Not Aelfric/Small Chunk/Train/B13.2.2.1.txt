Be Godes þeowum.
Leofan men, ic bidde gehyrað, hwæt ic wylle secgan þurh Godes gyfe us eallum to þearfe, gecnawe, se
Eallum cristenum mannum is mycel þearf, þæt hi Godes lage fyligean and godcundre lare geornlice
And huru gehadodum is ealra mæst þearf, forðam þe hi sceolon ægðer ge bodian ge bysnian Godes riht
Nu wille we læran Godes þeowas georne, þæt hi hy sylfe wærlice beþencan and ðurh Godes fultum
And þæt hi bocum and gebedum geornlice fyligean and bodian and bysnian Godes riht georne.
And þæt hi læran gelome, swa hi geornost magan, þæt gehadode menn regollice libban and læwede
And gif hit geweorðe, þæt folce mislimpe þurh here oðþon hunger, þurh stric oððe steorfan, þurh
And æfre hi sylfe beon heom geþwære and swiðe anræde for Gode and for worulde, swa swa hit awriten
And gyf mæssepreost his agen lif rihtlice fadige, weaxe his wyrðscype, and gyf he elles do, gebete hit
