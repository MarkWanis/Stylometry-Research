Her sweotelað hu Aðelwold biscop & se hired on Winceastre on ealdan mynstre be Eadwardes cyninges
Þæt is ðonne þet se biscop & se hired him sealdon XII hida landes æt Mordune þe his yldran heora
& Ælfsige his agen fæder þet ylcæn land æft ðem hyrde on ealdan mynstre þær eal his forðfædren rest
& he him on þæt ylcan gera þær togeanes gesealde binnan Wintanceastre twegra æcera gewirde landes &
& þa boc þærto agæf ðe Ælfred cining his yldran gebocode.
Ðonne wes ðises gehwerfes to gewitnesse Eadward cining & Aþelwold biscop & Æþælmær ealdorman
