Þis syndan þa langemera to Roluestune.
Ærest of dufan on hæcce, of hæcce on þone greatan þorn, of þan þorne to ðan hæg stowe.
Æfter hæg stowe to dottes hlawe of dotdes hlawe to þere strete þær on ansidelege, of ansidelege on þa
Of ðan dice on þan fulan sich of ðan fulan siche on wattiches æces of wattices æces on þan holan gryfe
Of ðan holan on þan ufer ende balcan of balcan to middel lege, of middel lege to ðan scid hæge.
æfter scid hæge þwers ofer bracan hyrste, þ on mæran broce dune mid broce to þan stanigan forde þe
Æfter ofesan to ðan fulan syce þ on þan broce of ðan broce on stoc legan ford of ðan forde abiðan
Of ðan dice on þan fulan syce æfter sice on pilebroc dun æfter broce on þan fulan sic þe scot betux
Of ðan hæg stowe on þa deopan dæle of ðan dæle on dufan, on þa bradan strete æt burtone, anlang
Æfter þære mæd eafden on þ wuda a æfter ofesan þ eft on ðan bradan stræte.
