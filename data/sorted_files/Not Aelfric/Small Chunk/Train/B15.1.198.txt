Carta Willelmi Regis Senioris de libertate ecclesie.
Willelm kyng gret Walchelm biscop & Hugan de Port & Eadward scirgerefan & Odan & Ægelsi & Saulf
And ic kyðe eow þæt ic wille þæt Sancte Peter & Walchelm biscop beon ealra þare lagena weorðe þe
Nu for þig bidde ic eow & festlice beode þæt ge ne latan for me ne for nanan oðran men þæt ge ne kyðan
