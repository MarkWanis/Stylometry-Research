On almiðin drichtines name.
Ic Eadward king kyðe alle manne ðe red ðat ic mid alle mine redgiuen red habbe for mine soule þerfe
Ðat is ðat ic an eke fredames ðan halegen kinge seint Eadmunde so forð so he fermest hauede into ðere
An so wille him ðat se fredom stonde on his welde, ðat so fele siðe so men gildeð hire gilde to heregilde,
And ic suðen eac swilke mid ðere schirebiscopes rede, and mid mine wedde, and alle mine biscope, and
And ic an ðan halegen kinge ðat land at Mildenhale mid mete and mid manne and mid sokne so it me
And ic han hem al here tune sokene of hale here londe ðe he nu habbeð oðer giet bigiten schullen on
God how healde.
