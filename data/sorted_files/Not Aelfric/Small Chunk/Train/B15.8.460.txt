Þis synt ðara tweira hida land gemæro æt Sandforda.
Ærest on herpoð of crydian bricge, þonne on þone norðran weg andlang herpoðes bufan cealdan wylle,
Þonne west andlang dic be þam wege oþ weard setl, of weard setle norð oþ hroces ford.
Of hroces forda norð rihte oð henne stigle, of henne stigle norþ on wyrtruman, of wyrtruman norð on
Of þel bricge adune andlang streames oð crydian, þonne adune andlang streames oð crydian bricge.
Ðys ys seo wude rædden.
Ærest of hwytan wege to þam wurmstealle, fram þam wurmstealle to hagan gete, fram hagan gete to
Adun on þa ea to hwyta weges lace, upp on þa lace eft to hwyta wege.
