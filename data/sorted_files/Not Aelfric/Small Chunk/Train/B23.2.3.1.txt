Syndon twegen dagas on ægwylcum monðe, swahwætswa on þam dagum ongynð, ne wurð hit næfre
On Ianuarius, þonne se mona bið III nihta eald and IIII.
On Februarius, þonne he bið V nihta eald and VII.
On Martius, þonne he bið VI nihta eald and VII.
On Aprelis, þonne he bið V nihta eald and VIII.
On Maius, þonne he bið VIII nihta eald and IX.
On Iunius, þonne he bið V nihta eald and XVII.
On Iulius, þonne he bið III nihta eald and XIII.
On Agustus, þonne he bið VIII nihta eald and XIII.
On September, þonne he bið V nihta eald and IX.
On October, þonne he bið V nihta eald and XV.
On Nouember, þonne he bið VII nihta eald and IX.
On December, þonne he bið III nihta eald and XII.
And swa hit bið, gyme, seþe wylle.
