In die depositionis beati augustini anglorum doctoris.
Men ða leofestan we wyllað eow sume gereccednysse cyðan embe ðyses þurhhaligan symbeldæges
Aerest on frymðe us gedafenað to herigenne & to wuldrigenne ælmihtigne scyppend þurh þæne & on
He eac swylce hys gecorenan & his haligan on heofena rices gefean fægere gelogað.
Sume synd gecwedene englas, sume heahenglas.
Ðæge wæron on fruman of godes oroðe & blæde beorhte gesceapene.
We na durron embe þæt deoppur gereccan for ðon hit ys unriht ricum ge heanum þæt hi godes diglu
Ac we wyllað embe þæt secgan & reccean þe we of boca latæ leorniað gelome þæt he heonon geceas
Sume wæron heahfæderan healice.
