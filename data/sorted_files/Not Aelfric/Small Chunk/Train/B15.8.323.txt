Ðis is þæra land boc to hamme.
Þæ eadgar cing ge bocade aðelstane bisceope on æce yrfe.
Ærest of eadelmes dic on stoc fliot.
Of þam fliote to ilmere andlang wynsies mearce on þone langan þorn.
Of þam þornæ on wehinc leage þanon on byrc mære andlang mære on þara hina mearcæ andlang mearæ
Andlang wylle þæt innan hile andlang ealdan hilæ æt þæra hina mæde þanon innan fleot andlang þes
