Ðis sint ða land gemara to Scroppen þorpe.
And yuresby.
Of langanforda dic andlang dices þ on þa miclan dic andlang dices on blidan et celfa se yeade.
Andlang blidan on beo lese suðerewearde þ up da miclan strat andlang strat ut þurh beolease norð ut
Up be iddel on þone fulan broc andlang brocci eft on langanford.
Ðis sint ða land gemaree to Suttune.
Of fulanforda andlang stret to þam drape þ on geriht mid þone draf on þone broc andlang broces on
On gerihte of scyrinda midde weardne on iddel at brodan fleote þ up a be iddel eft on fulan forde.
Ðonne is sisotef land binnan ðisum gemaree.
