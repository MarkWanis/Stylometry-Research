To mæssepreostum.
Eala, feala is þara, þe sacerdhades on unriht geornaþ mæssepreosthades for gitsuncge woroldgescryna
Be þam spræc se witiga swiþe egeslice and þus cwæþ: ue sacerdotibus, qui comedunt peccata populi.
Þæt is on englisc: wa þam sacerdum, he cwæþ, þe fretaþ folces sinna.
Þæt sindon þa, þæ na cunnan folce wiþ sinna gewarnian and sinna gestyran and nimaþ heora sceattas to
Ah hit is swiþe micel þearf, se þe ær þysum misdyde, þæt he hit heonanforþ hit georne gebete and
Bitere scel hit him wyrþan forgolden, þæt he for deadum mann nimþ, buton he hit mid mæssan and mid
Hali drihten gemiltsige us eallan, amen.
