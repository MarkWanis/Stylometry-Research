In nomine domini nostri Ihesu Christi. Her swutelað on þisum gewrite hu Wulfric abbod & Ealdred
Þæt he gebeh for Godes ege & for sanctus Augustinus & for hys freonda mynegunge mid lande into
Þises ys to gewitnesse se hired æt sancte Augustine & se æt Cristes cyrcean & Lifing his fæder &
& þis sy gedon for Siferð & for his ofsprincg to hyra sawle ðearfe a butan ende, amen.
