Id est an wiwarawic quae ante subjecta erat to wii & to leanaham & et febresham: i sealtern & ii wena
& iiii oxnum gers mid cyninges oxnum, an wiwarawic xxx statera kasei et item x statera in alia
Ab occidente cyninges folcland quod abet wighelm & wulflaf ab aquilone cuðrices dun heregeðeland.
Hec sunt prata to wassingwellan stocmed healf be norðan hegforde be sturemeda sue ðer to limpað.
Ðis siondan ðes landes boec et Wassingwellan ðet Eðelbearht cyning Wullafe sealde his ðegne wið
Se cyning sealde & gebocade Wullafe fif sulung landes et Wassingwellan wið ðem fif sulungum et
& se cyning dyde ðet land et Mersaham him to folclande ða hie ðem landum iehwerfed hefdan butan
