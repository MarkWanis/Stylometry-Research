Her swutelað on þisum gewrite hu Godric æt Burnan begeat þæt land æt Offaham.
Þæt is ðonne þæt he sealde Eadgyuan his sweostor an marc goldes & XIII pundan & LXIII penega on
Þes ceap wæs geceapod on Wii ætforan ealra scyre.
Þises is to gewitnesse, Eadsige arcebiscop & Siward biscop & Godric decanus & eall se hired æt Cristes
Nu synd þissa gewrita þreo, an is æt Cristes cyricean & oþer æt sancte Augustine & þridde hæfð Godric
