To folce.
Leofan men, understandað ærest, hu mycel þearf is cristenum mannum, þæt hy heora fulluhtes gescad
Forðam nah ænig man mid rihte to fullianne hæþenne man, gyf he ylde and andgyt hæfð, þæt he
Ðonne agan þa micle þearfe, þe on geogoðe gefullode weorþað, þæt hi georne leornian, þæt hy heora
Forðam se man, þe ne can þæs gescad, he ne færð naht, butan he geleornige, þæt he cunne his
Ac þæt bið cristenes mannes þearf, þæt he smeage aa ymbe cristendom georne and acsie georne, hwæt
On þam dome witodlice he eowað us his blodian sidan and his þyrlan handa and ðæne þyrnenan helm
Wel þam þonne, þe hyne wel gehealden hæfð, and wa ðam þonne, þe his ær nolde giman, swa swa he
Ac utan don, swa us mycel þearf is, habban æfre fulne hiht on god ælmihtigne and rihtne geleafan and
