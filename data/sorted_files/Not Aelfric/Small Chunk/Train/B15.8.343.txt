Ðis sindon þa land gemæro to sunnanbyrig.
Ærest on sunnan hyg þanon andlang streames on crudan scypsteal þanon ofer ða mæde on eclesbroc
&lang dices on hwæte dene norðewearde of hwæte dene on þa oðre mearcdic &lang dices on cottes
Of þam hlæwe on þone ellen stub ðonon on mearcwill of mearcwille on duddes byre on þone clofenan
And her hyrdð to tynn gyrda of þære mæde to halgan forde & ælce geare into sunnanbyrig of burhwuda
Þis is þara X hida land boc æt Sunnanbyrig ðe Eadgar cyning gebocode ælfhege his mæge on ece yrfe.
