Ðis sand ða landimare to Corf and to Blechenenwelle ðare VII hide.
Ærest of wikenforde, anlang wiken of Scylenford, of Scylenforde on richt wege, of ðanne weie on olle
Ðanen sant ðis ðat westrene landimare.
Of se one ðe stoddic, ðanen forð be wertrumen on anne stanweal.
Of ðanne walle þweres ouer smalen cumbe, of ða þorne upwarde, ðanen forð be euisc one ðat norðene
