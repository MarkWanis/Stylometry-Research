In nomine domini Ihesu Christi. Ic Eadgar cing cyðe on þisse bec þæt is Cristes boc þæt ic habba þa fif
& ðis ic habbe idon for ure ealra saule lufon to ecre reste.
Gif hwa beo swa dyrtig þæt þis abrece oððe awenden wylle, þas mine gife & sylene mid ænegum
Ic Eadgar cyning mid þara halgan rode tacn ðis hate swiþe geornlice getrymman & gefæstnian.
