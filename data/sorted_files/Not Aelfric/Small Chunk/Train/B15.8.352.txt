Dys synt dara XX hyda Landgemara to scireburnan ðæt inlandes.
Arest on sude yfardan þ est andlang brotty on done herepoð nord andlang weges to scearpan brycge.
East danan andlang sledes þ hit cinð on done weg & healfe hide on hibaldes tofte & ane hide on frysse
And on on Lundby twegra oxena gang & dridde healfe hyrde on mysen forda & on stiuetune & on
