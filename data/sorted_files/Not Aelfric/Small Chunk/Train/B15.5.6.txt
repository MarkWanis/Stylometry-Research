Ic denewulf bisceop kyðæ Eadwarde kyninge minum hlafurdæ ymb þæt land on Beaddinctune þæ ðu mæ
Þonnæ min leof hæbbe ic nu æt ðam hiwum fundæn on Wintæceastræ gæ æt gieldran gæ æt giengran
Þonne his þæs lon æs hund seofontig hida, and is nu eall ge wæred and ðu hit æst min laford mæ to læt.
Þu wæs hit ierfælæas and mið æðnum folce aburod.
And ic ðu sælf þæt ierfæ to ge strindæ, þæt ðær mon siððun bi wæs.
And wæ his þæ nu swyðæ eadmodlicæ unnon.
Þonnæ min leof siondon hiwun nu firmdige þet hit æfter þinum degæ to þæræ stowæ æft agyfæn sie.
Ðonnæ is þær nu irfæs þæs þæs stranga wintær læfæd hæfð nigon ealð hriðru, and feower and hund
Ðara is twæntig ealdra.
And þær is hund endlæftig ealdra sceapa, & seofæn þeowæ mæn, and twæntig flicca, and næs þær
