Her geswutelað on þisum gewrite þæt Cnut kynig læt þæt land æt Folkenstane into Cristes cyrcean on
And he hit ne mæg naðor ne gyfen ne syllan ne forspekan ne forspillan ut of þam halgan mynstre.
Ac æfter his dæge ga þæt land þam hyrede on hand þe þonne Criste þeniað innan þam mynstre mid sake
Nu geuðe he þis land into Cristes cyrcean his sawle to ecere alysednisse.
And gyf ænig sy swa dyrstig ongæn God þæt þis awændan wille awænde hine God ælmihtig fram
Ðis wæs gedon innon Suðrie on Kyngestune on þone halgan dæg pentecosten.
And þises wæs to gewitnesse, ælfgyua Imma seo hlæfdige & ægelnoð arcebiscop & ælfwine biscop on
& ælmær abbud æt sancte Augustine & Wulnoð abbot æt Westminstre & ælfwine abbot æt Niwan
& þored steallara & Agamund & Osgod Clapa & Tofig & ægelwine ælfhelmes sunu & Siword æt
And þissera gewrita synd þreo an is innon Cristes cyrcean & oðer æt sancte Augustine & þæt þridde is
