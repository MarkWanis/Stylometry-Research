Þis sind þa land gemæra to Boxoran ærest æt Uuines treowe þ andlang herpaðes to dene pytte þanon up
Of þam þorne þ andlang mearce on lamburnan on clatford of clatforda on meos broces heafod þ þanon to
Þanon andlang her paþes ut on þone mersc þ a be mersce to þam tune on þa hege ræwe þ on þa dic ut on
Of þære græfan on rinda crundel of þan crundle to þrim þornan þ andlang weges eft on wines treow þær
