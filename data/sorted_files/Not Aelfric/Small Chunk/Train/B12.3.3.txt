Ic ileue on enne god, fæder almihti, wurchend heouene & eorþe, & alle iseienliche þing & vniseienliche
He þrowede eæc swulce on rode ahongen for us & he was iburied.
& he aros on þan þridde dæie, so so iwrite siggeð, & he asteih to heouene, & he sit æ riht hond his
& he eftð cumeð mid wuldre to deminde þen cwike and þam deaden, and his riche ne bið non ende.
And ic ileue on þene holi gost, þene liffestan god, þe geð of þen fæder and of þen sunu and he is mid
Ic andette þa onan halwen and þa ileaffulle and þa apostolican ilaþunge, & on fulluht on forgiuenesse
& ic abide ariste deadre monne & þes eche liues þære touwarde worlde.
Beo hit so, amen.
