Ðis sind þa land ge mearo to Pyrianforda.
Ærest of pyrianforda andlang wægan on fisces burnan.
Andlang burnan on þone mearc hagan up be hagan oþ þa gedela west on gerihta to snawes mere
Up be hagan on weorþe hege forð be þan hege on weorþ apeldre þonne on per leage þonne suþ to efes on
Þonne a be hagan to mint felda þonon on gerihta on mint byrge þær to burnan &lang burnan on þa flex
Andlang twicene on weahhes fen þonon ut on wegan andlang wegan on pyrianford & si þæt wuda
