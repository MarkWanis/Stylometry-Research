In usses Dryhtnes noman Hælendes Cristes ic Uuerfriþ biscop mid alles ðæs heoredes leafe on
Nu gewrite ic hit eft hire mid hina leafe ðaet ðreora hida lond on ðreora monna daeg.
& heo hæbbe ða wuduraeddenne in ðæm wuda ðe ða ceorlas brucaþ.
& ec ic hire lete to ðaet ceorla graf to sundran.
& elles ðæt twega hida lond & ða ceorlas & se Alhmunding snaed here into Preosdabyrig ða hwile hit
Ond Cyneswið hit to nængum oþrum men ne lete ða hwile hit unagæn se butun to hire bearna sumum,
Gif heo ðonne ne lifigen lete hit to sweolcum hire mega swelce hit hire to geearnigan wille.
Ond ic Uuerfrið biscop biddu & halsigu ðæt ðis ðreora hida lond & ec ðæt twega ðonne hit agæn seo
Ond ec ic Uuerfriþ biscop & all higen halsigaþ usse æterfylgend ðæt heora nænig ðas gefe gewonige ær
& all higen eodan to minum bure on Weogornaceastre & me saldan heora hondsetene ðisse
Ðara noman her beneoþan awriten stondaþ.
& heo hit haebben eghwæs to freon butun agefen elce gere ðreo mittan hwætes to ciricsceatte to Clife.
