On drihtenes naman, hælendes Cristes.
Ic Willelm kyng, on þam oðeran geare þæs þe ic to kinge gehalgod wæs, on Cristesmæsse dæg, þa þa ic
Criste and Sancte Marian to lufe and to wurðmynte and Sancte Martine, þe sylf Ingelric and Æirad his
And unne þas land þider ingelegdon, þæt is Estre in Eastsexa, mid þære berewica æt Mæisbyrig, and
And þærto eake on minre healfe ic heom geaf and geuþe, for mines fader sawle and minre moder, and
To eake þam, ic heom geuðe ealle þa cyrican and teoðinge and land and hus binnan Lunden oððe buten
And eall þæs þing habbe and healde Sanctes Martines mynster and þa canonichas a on ecnesse wurðlice
And ða canonicas behedan of heomsylfan ænne treowne, þe heore ealra þing healde, and be þæs geares
Seo gewislice þis bufen cweden Sancte Martines mynster and þa canonikes and eall þæt þærto lið freols
Se þe þas gesetednesse and unnan þa we geset and geunnan habbað undo oððe awerde, þolie he on
