Her kied on þise iwrite þat hich luuig bischop at mine fuyrsiþe wram crediton hi ministre to þare cherche
And ido in on oþe masse boc at sainte marie weuede to gadre hi broght þar ma hit schel i su.
And in on oþer boke þat hatte penitencialis bodde to hourde and ich hit itrimde mid on hondreit dages
