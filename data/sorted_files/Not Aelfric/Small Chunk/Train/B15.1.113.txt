Eadward king gret Harold erl & Aylnoð abbot & Godwine schyrereuen & alle mine þeynes on
And ich cyðe eow þat ich habbe geunnan Gisan minan presteo þes bissopriche her mid eow and alre þare
And gif her ani lond sy out of þan bissopriche gedon ich wille þæt hit cume in ongeæn other þæt man hit
And ich bidde eou allen þat ge him fulstan to driuan Godes gerichte lock huer hyt neod sy and heo
And ich nelle nanne man geþefien þat him vram honde teo anige þare þinge þas þe ich him unnen
