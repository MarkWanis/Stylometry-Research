In nomine patris et filii et spiritus sancti. Ceolred abbot & ða higan on Medeshamstede sellað Wulfrede
End forðon we him ðis land sellað ðet he ðes landes fulne friodom bigete in ece ærfeweardnisse æt
& bruce þere cirican lafard on Medeshamstede ðes landes æt Slioforda & Wulfred ðes on Sempingaham.
& he geselle eghwelce gere to Medeshamstede tua tunnan fulle luhtres aloh & tua slegneat & sëx hund
& hi sion symle in allum here life eadmode & hearsume & underþeodde.
& ofer here tuega dëg þonne agefe hio þet land into þere cirican to Medeshamstede mid freodome.
& we him þis sellað mid felda & mid wuda & mid fenne sua þerto belimpeð.
Sið heora tuuege dæg agan sie þonne agefe mon tuuenti hida higuum to biodland & þere cirican
& he wes feormied tuuege hida landes æt Lëhcotum his erfeweorda sweolcum swelce him ðonne
Anno uero dominice incarnationis (25) DCCCLII Indictione XV hoc factum est. Her sindan ða naman
Ic Ceolred abbod þas ure selene mid Cristes rode tacne trymme & festnie.
