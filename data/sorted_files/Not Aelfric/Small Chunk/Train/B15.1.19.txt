Deormod gebohte æt Ælfred cyninge mid fiftigum mancessan arodenes goldes fif hida land on ece yrfe
Ærest on þa lace þæ lið be westan dyrnangelade andlang lace up on ane furh þ on widan geat andlang
Þ on þone mor ðe led betwux suðtune & wittanhamme & swa forð be more þ on þa ealdan dic þæ lið
And of þære ealdan dic to dices wyllan of dices wyllan to sand gewyrpe.
And of sand gewyrpe þ utan temese.
