Ærest on blacanford.
Þanon andlang broces on tan.
Þanon andlang tan on þa ealdan earace.
Of beadding broc.
Upp andlang beaddinge broces on beadding bricge & þanon upp andlang on halgan wylle.
And þanon on ge rihte on þa dic & ðanon norð andlang ricges on gean ða II þornas to broce.
Andlang broc rewe on blacan forð.
