Ðis sint þa land ge mære þe hirað in to Wellig forda of wines treowe andlang weges on þane holan weg
Of dyrnan treowe on ylfing dene on ænne ele beam of þan ele beame on cedboldes wylle of cedboldes
Of cytan seohtres forda andlang hricgges on syntri mæde norþe wearde.
Of syntri mæde on flex hammas of flex hamman on mint hammas on cylman stane.
Of cylman stane on þone þryscytan crundel of þan crundele on þone rugan crundel of þan ruwan
Of þan hangran andlang rode ut on mules dene andlang dene utan lam burnan andlang burnan on wæter
Of þan wæter wege on waterhammes of þan hamman on grenan beorh of þan beorhe onlang heccan on
Of þan treowe onlang gemæres to wines treow.
