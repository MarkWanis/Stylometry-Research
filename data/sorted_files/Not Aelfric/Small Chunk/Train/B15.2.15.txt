Her suyteleþ on þise wrtte wilke forwarde Stigand and Alger his prest Wrouhten þo he let him þat lond
Þat is þat Alger prest habbe þat lond his day and ouer his day fonge Stigand þerto gif he leng libbe.
And ouer here boþere day go þat lond into seynt Eadmunde mid mete and mid manne and mid alle þinge
And he it ne may neyþer ne forsegen ne forwerken.
Þis aren II witnesse, Alfric bisscop & Vui Abbot & al se hird binnen seynt Eadmundes biri and Stigand
Þise wrtte sinden tua, on is binnen seynt Eadmundes biri & oþer hauið Stigand.
