Willelm kyng gret Willelm bisceop & Gosfregð portirefan & ealle þa burhwaru binnan Londone,
And ic kyðe eow þæt ic wylle þæt get beon eallra þæra laga weorðe þe gyt wæran on Eadwurdes dæge
And ic wylle þæt ælc cyld beo his fæder yrfnume æfter his fæder dæge.
And ic nelle geþolian þæt ænig man eow ænig wrang beode.
God eow gehealde.
