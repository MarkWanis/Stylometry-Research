Ic Badanoð Beotting cyðo & writan hato hu min willa is ðet min ærfe lond fere ðe ic et Aeðeluulfe
Ic wille ærist me siolfne Gode allmehtgum forgeofan to ðere stowe æt Cristes cirican & min bearn ðër
& hie brucen londes hiora dei & higon gefeormien to minre tide swæ hie soelest ðurhtion megen & higon
& ðonne ofer hiora dei wifes & cilda ic bebeode on Godes noman ðæt mon agefe ðæt lond inn higum to
& ic biddo higon for Godes lufe ðæt se monn se higon londes unnen to brucanne ða ilcan wisan leste on
Ðonne is min willa ðæt ðissa gewriota sien twa gelice, oðer habben higon mid boecum oðer mine
Ðonne is ðes londes ðe ic higum selle, XVI gioc ærðe londes & medwe all on æce ærfe to brucanne ge
Ceolnoð ðiss writo & festnië mid Cristes rode tacne.
Alchhere ðiss writo & ðeafië.

Hysenoð ðiss writo & ðeafie.
