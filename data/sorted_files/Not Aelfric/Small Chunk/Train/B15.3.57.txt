In nomine domini.
Her swutelað on ðissum gewrite þæt Ealdred biscop hæfð geunnen Æþestane fættan sumne dæl landes.
Þæt synd twa hida mid þam þe he ær hæfde & mid þam hrofleasan lande, & he hig eac werige for twa
& þiss land is on þam tune þe fram cuðum mannum Hylle is gehaten.
& æfter þissera þreora manna forþfore gange þæt land eft into Wigeraceastre to þam þingan þe se wylle
& þiss wæs gedon be Eadwardes cynges leafe & be his fullan unnan.
& þisses is to gewitnysse, eall se hired on Wigeraceastre & Manni abbot & se hired on Eofeshamme &
& sy þiss land ælces þinges freols butan weallgeweorce & brygcgeweorce & ferosocne & ciricsceatte.
God ælmihtig þone gehealde þe þiss gehealde.
