In nomine trino diuino qui est deus benedictus in sæcula amen. ðy gere þe wæs from Cristes geburde
Þa wæs tiolo micel sprec ymb wuduleswe to Suþtune ongægum West on Scirhylte.
Waldon þa swangerefan þa leswe forður gedrifan & þone wudu geþiogan þonne hit ald geryhta weron.
Þonne cuæð se biscop and þara hira wiotan þæt hio him neren maran ondeta þonne hit aræded wæs on
Ða geræhte Wulfred arcebiscop & alle þa wiotan þet se biscop & þa higen moston mid aþe gecythan þet
And he þa sona se biscop bewæddade Eadwulfe ðæm aldormen þæs aþes biforan allum þæm wiotum &
In þa tiid wæs Hama suangerefa to Suðtune.
& he ræd ðæt he wæs æt Ceastre and þone aað gesæh & gesceawade sua hine his aldormon heht
hii sunt nomina et uocabula qui in synodali concilio fuerunt congregati
