Fæder manncynnes, frofres ic þe bidde, halig drihten, þu ðe on heofonum eart.
Þæt sy gehalgod, hygecræftum fæst, þin nama nu ða, neriende Crist, in urum ferhðlocan fæste
Cume nu to mannum, mihta wealdend, þin rice to us, rihtwis dema, and ðin geleafa in lifdæge on urum
And þin willa mid us weorðe gelæsted on eardunge eorðan rices, swa hluttor is in heofonwuldre,
Syle us nu to dæge, drihten gumena, heofena heahcyning, hlaf urne, þone ðu onsendest sawlum to hæle
Forgyf us, gumena weard, gyltas and synna, and ure leahtras alet, lices wunda and mandæda, swa we
Ne læd þu us to wite in wean sorge ne in costunge, Crist nerigende, þy læs we arlease ealra þinra mildsa
And wið yfele gefreo us eac nu ða feonda gehwylces; we in ferhðlocan, þeoden engla, ðanc and wuldor,
Weorðe þæt.
