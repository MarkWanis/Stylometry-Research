Eadgares cyninges freols boc to Tantune.
Her ys geswutelod on þysum gewrite hu Eadgar cyning mid geþeahte his witena geniwode Tantunes
& man ealle spæca & gerihtu on þæt ylce gemet gefe to Godes handa þe man to his agenre drifh.
& þes tunes ciyping & seo innung þara port gerihta gange into þere halgan stowe ealswa heo ær dyde on
Se þe þisne freols geycean wille geyce God his gesynta to langsumun life her & on ecnesse.
Gif hwa þonne þurh gedyrstignesse & deofles oþþe his lima lare þysne freols abrecan wille oþþe on
Ðonne gesealde Aðelwold biscop his cynehlaforde twa und mancussa goldes & anne sylfrene lefel on
& ær wænon wiþ freolse gesealt on Eadwardes dege cinges syxtig hide landes, X æt Crawancumbe & X
& eft Eadward cyning gesealde þæt land æt Cumbtune & æt Bananwille þan hiwon æt Ceodre wiþ þan
Þis wes gedon æt Ceodre on þere halgan eastertide.
