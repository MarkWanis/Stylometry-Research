WIÞ DEOFLE AND UNGEMYNDE: Wiþ deofle liþe drenc and ungemynde, do on ealu cassuc, elehtran
Gesing XII mæssan ofer þam drence and drince.
Him biþ sona sel.
Drenc wiþ deofles costunga: þefan þorn, cropleac, elehtre, ontre, bisceopwyrt, finul, cassuc, betonice,
And sie se drenc þær inne, þær se seoca man inne sie, and simle ær þon þe he drince, sing þriwa ofer
