Ðis is Wudetunes boc þe eadmund cing bocode eadrice his þegne þreora mannæ deg þam þe him leofost
Þis seondon þa lond gemero to Wudutune.
Eræst of þæm æsce andlong æge on tigel leage and swa ondlonges þæs gemæres þet hit cymþ to þæm
Þonne on þa dic on þæt londe gemere on medestran.
Þæt swa andlang streames on ballestran þæt hit cymþ to huntena forda þæt of þam forda on hwingles
Up andlang streames swa up to þæm æwylme.
Þæt swa on þone ealdan brac weg þet hit cymþ to hnibban lege.
Swa ofer þe legæ þæt ofer nybban beorh easteweardne on þone weg andlang weges to rah slede.
Þonne to dofer lan andlang dofærlan on aler cymbe midde weardne andlang cymbes to wræccena
Þet hit to cymþ to þa ric hide.
And seoþþan þa ciric hida & eall þæt þær to gebyreð & oslan wyrþ & eall þæt þær to gebyreþ.
And an feorðan dæl æt byrnes wyllan into wudetune.
