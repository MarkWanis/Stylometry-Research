Hec fynteali mearc þis sindon Swalewanclifes land gemero.
Ærest of þære se.
To þam burnan of þam burnan be þere eastmearke.
Swa to ælfwoldes mearke.
Of ælfwoldes mearke.
To kynges mearke to se.
Et seo wudu reden þe þer mid rihte to gebirede.
Et wdændere cæt hærst hatte.
And þ sealttærn steal æt here pit.
