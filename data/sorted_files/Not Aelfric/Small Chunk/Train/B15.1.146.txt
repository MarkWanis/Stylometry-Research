Eadward king gret mine biscopas & mine eorles & ealle mine þeingnes on Wigercestrescire & on
& icc kyþe eow þæt ic habbe gifen Criste & Sancte Petre into Westminstre Periscoran & Deorhyrst &
& ic hate & beode þæt ealle þa þeignas of þam landen hynenforð understanden to þan abbote & to þam
God eow ealle gehealde.
