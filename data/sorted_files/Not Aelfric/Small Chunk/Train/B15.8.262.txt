Þis is ðara X hida boc æt Cittandene þe edwig cing gebocode æþelgerde his þegne on ece yrfæ.
Ðis synd þa land gemera to Cittandene.
Ærest on þone þriddan hlinc þe to hamelandunæ hyrþ of þam hlince on mearcdene andlang dene to þam
Þonon east andlang weges on þonon hwitan weter weg.
Of þam wege forþ inon mearclea.
Þonon east andlang hlinces on þa here stræte.
Þonon to cittanwara becum.
Andlang beca forþ to wealtham inon þa mearce þe to hamelandune hyrþ.
