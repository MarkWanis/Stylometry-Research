Ðis is þara VII hidæ boc æt harewellæ þe eadgar cing gebocodæ ælfricæ his þegne on ece yrfæ.
Ðis syndan þara VII hida land ge mæro.
Ærest of ðam ællan stubbe on þone oðerenne elle stub.
And swa on þa ealdan firh.
Þanon to smalan wege & swa ymbutan flegges garan.
Þanon andlang þæs smalan weges utt on humbracumb swa on ycenilde weg a forh be wege eft on þone
Þa non on þone hærepað.
On harandune.
And swa on cranwylle.
Þa non on heslea broc.
And swa on hreo mæde þa non on þa ealdan cot stowa.
And swa on smalan broc.
Þa non on þæt lange furlang swa on cylmæscumb on snelles garon.
Ðis syndon þæra mæda ge mæra.
Ærest andlang þæs ealdan broces of suðtuniga lace and swa on stocwylle broc þa non on þone ealdan
And swa and wudubrigge.
