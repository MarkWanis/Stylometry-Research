In nomine domini nostri Ihesu Cristi. þys is Leofwines cwide Wulfstanes suna.
Þæt is þonne ærest þæt ic gean Criste & Sancte Petre for minre saule in to Westmynster ealra þara þinga
& ic gean of Purlea in to Hnutlea healfere hide landes on easthealf stræte for mine sawle þam Godes
& minre faþan Leofware þæs heafodbotles on Purlea & ealles þæs þe me þær to locaþ.
& gif Eadwold længc libbe hire suna þonne heo fo he þærto.
Gif heo þonne læng beo, & þæt God wille, sylle hit on þa hand þe hire æfre betst gehyre on uncer bega
& ic gean minum hlaforde Wulfstane bisceope þæs landes æt Bærlingum.
Þys wæs gedon þæs geares fram ures Drihtnes gebyrdtide, DCCCCXCVIII XI XX V VIII XVII
Þis is Leofwines cwide, Wulfstanes suna.
