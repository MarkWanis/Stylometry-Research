Aefter þisse collectan sceal se mæssepreost bigan his cneowa ætforan þam untruman mid gelimplicre
Ðonne andwyrde se mettruma and him þus to cweðe, To þan þæt ge me smyrian.
Ðonne mid ealre liðnesse se mæssepreost mid feawum wordum godcunde lare hine getrymme and
Gif he þonne hwæt geandettan wille þæs þe he ær geandet næfde, þonne tæce se mæssepreost him,
Gif he þonne wuruldcund man sy, þonne secge him se mæsse preost, þæt he mot ærost his
Gif þonne se untruma cweð, þæt he naðer ne mæge ne his synna geandettan, ne oðrum gemiltsian, ne
Gif he þonne cwyð, þæt he ægðer eadmodlice don wylle ge his synna geandettan ge oðrum gemiltsian,
And fo he syððan on þa letanias, and þa hwyle þe man þa letanias singð, asmeage se untruma geornlice
