Wulfringctun, Æþelwold.
Ic Osuuold arcebisceop þurh Godes giefe mid geþafunge & leafe Eadwardes Angulcyninges & Ælfheres
Sie hit ælces þinges freoh buto ferdfare & walgeworc & brygcgeweorc & cyrcanlade.
Þis wæs gedon ymbe VIIII hund wintra & VII & hundseofantig þæs þæ Drihtnes gebyrdtide wæs
Gief hwa buton gewrihtum hit abrecan wylle God hine to rihtere bote gecyrre, amen.
Her is seo hondseten.
