This synd there are hyde land gemæru to Monawurthige ærust an Monawurthiges ford.

Of there dic suth on thæne herpoth.
Thenne and lad herpothes to Heantune suth weardon.
Of Heuntune on riht gemære to thære bradan apulduran, on geriht mære on thet hwite styb.
Of than stybbe an clenan mor wæstwearne of clæne more wæsteweardan an thane stream.
