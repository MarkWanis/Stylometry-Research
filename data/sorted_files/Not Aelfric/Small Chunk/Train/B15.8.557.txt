Eowlangelad Ealhstan.
Æþelstan.
Ðis synd þa land gemære æt eowlan gelade.
Of eowlan gelade andlang bladene.
Of bladene andlang riþiges þ on fugel mære midne.
Of fulgel mere þ on þone oþerne fugel mere.
Of fugel mere þ on gild beorh.
Of gild beorh andlang sealtstræte to þan stane.
Of þan stane to þan oþeran stane þ swa to þan þriddan stane, & to þan feorðan stane.
Of þam stane to þære grenan stige.
Of þære stige to lafercan beorh.
Of lafercan beorh on þone ealdan weg þ ofer þone broc þ eft on þone ealdan weg.
Of þam ealdan wege þ forð onlang dune a bi heafdan þ hit cymð to þan ealdan slo.
Of þan slo to þan lytlan beorhe, of þan beorge on þ sic.
Ondlong sices ofer þone broc to heort wellan.
Of heort wellan to hwete wellan, & þreo æceras earðlandes of hwete wellan a bi þan earðlande to þan to
Of þan beorge forð on geriht on þæt sic.
Of þam sice be þan heafdan þ hit cymð to mules hlawe, & þreo æceras þonne adune andlang þære furh
Swa andlang bladene þ hit cymð eft to eowlan gelade.
