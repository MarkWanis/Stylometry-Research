Henri king gret ealle his biscopes & eorles & his scirgereuan & þeinas & his wicneras Frencisce &
Ic kywe eow ðæt ic habbe geumen gode & Sancte Petre & þan biscope on Winceastre Willelme & his
And ic forbeode þæt nan mann ne beo swa dyrstig þæt heom fram handan drage heononford ænig þæra
And gif hit swa do wite he þæt hyt binnon þrym nihton ongean cume be eallon þam þe manage.
