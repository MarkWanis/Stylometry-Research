Þis sindon þa land gemæro to austan.
Ærest of sæfern & swa forð be tatan grafes wurt wale.
And þonne swa forð þ hit sticað on miclan cumb.
And of miclan cumbe þ hit sticað on litlan cumb.
And of litlan cumbe þ on irfurlanges dic.
And of irfurlanges dic þ on mær dic.
And of mær dic.
Þ on ealdan dic & of ealdan dic.
Þ on bradan bricge.
And of bradan bricge þ on holan pyl.
And of holan pylle on wincan hammes dic.
And of wincan hammes dic innan miclan pyl.
And of miclan pylle þ æft on sæferne.
