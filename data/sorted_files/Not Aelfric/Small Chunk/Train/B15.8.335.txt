Ðis synd þa land gemæra to rimecuda.
Of þære bricge on west ea andlang ea on biccan pol of þam pole on dyrnan ford þonne is seo mæd
Of þæm forda on þone sæ troh of þæm troge on þone hæþenan byrgels of þam byrgelse up to wind geate
Of þam burnan on leofan mearce on þa dic of þære dic on þa brembel þyrnan of þære þyrnan on
Þonne is seo mæd gemæne of þam wiþige on afene up on sandford of þam forda on aðelwoldes mearce
And þisses landes is ealles XXII hida.
