Terram quoque duorum aratruum in locis nominatis illic ðæt Suiðhunincg lond æt Grafon aea.
Duas possessiunculas et tertiam dimediam, id est in nostra loquella ðridda half haga.
Ærest on easthealfe betwynan leage & elmanstede swa ofer scæt.
Oþ stænincgmearce, swa andlang dices west on burhrode.
Ofer burhrode west on boddesham.
Þanon norð east on burhstræte.
Andlang stræte norð on denewarafeld.
East on stræte on þ ealde þrymyrce lehwara, and denewara, and elmes stedes.
Swa dic sceadeð on burhrode.
