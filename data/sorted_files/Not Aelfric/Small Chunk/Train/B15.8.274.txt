Ðis is ðara V hida boc æt tadmertune de eadwig cing gebocode beorhtnoðe ealdermen ece yrfe.
Ðis synt þa land gemæro to tadmertune þara V hida ærest on halhford þ andlang wo burnan þ on eow
Of sandforda a be gemære þ on ða heafod stoccas.
Of ðan stoccan on ðone mær stan.
Of ðan stane a be gemære þ on landgares bricge.
