Þis wæs gedon ymbe VIIII nigon hund wintra & LXVIIII & on nigoþan geare þe Oswold bisceop to
Sancta Maria & sanctus Michahel cum sancto Petro & eallum Godes halgum gemiltsie þis
Gif hwa butan gewyrhtum hit abrecan wylle hæbbe him wið God gemære buton he ær to dædbote
Her is seo hond seton.
Þis syndon þa landgemæru twegra hida æt Stoce.
Ærest on æscwellan, of æscwellan west on þa halas þanon on dynninces grafes wyrttruman þ swa on
Of swepelan streame west be wudu riman on readan wege.
Suðweardan of readan wege west eft in æsc wellan.
