Her is geswitulod on ðysum gewrite hu Eadgar cining mid rymette gedihligean het þa mynstra on
For ðy ðonne Aþelwold bisceop on þes cinges gewitnesse & ealles þæs hiredes his bisceopstoles
& se abbod Æþelgar mid geðeahte ures cynelafordes & þes bysceopes Aþelwoldes & ealles þæs hiredes
& he gesealde þam cinge hundtwelftig mancæs reades goldes to ðance beforan Ælfdryðe ðære hlæfdian
Ðyses ic geann Æþelgare abbode & þam hirede into niwan mynstre for his gecwemre gehyrsumnesse a
& ic halsige ælc ðara ðe eftær me cynerices wealde þurh ða halgan ðrynnesse þet hyra nan næ undo þet
Se þe ðis þonne awendan wylle ðe ic to sibbe & to gesehtnesse betweoh þam mynstre geradigod hæbbe
