Ðis syndon þa land gemæro to hymel tune.
Þæt is of baddon æsce on þæt brade gemære of ðam brade gemære on wad lege.
Of wad leage þurh ut þone wudu, norð big þam wuda oð hit cymð to hymel broce.
Æfter hymel broce oð hit cymð on ennanpol, of enna pole on wynna stigele, of wynna stigele þurh efna
Of þære brycge inan þone hæge, æfter þæm hæge on þa þreo gemæru, of þam gemæran on langa læge.
Of þære lege on þa dic, of þære dic on wymingc wyllan, of þære wellan on langan æcer.
Of þæm æcere þurh þone wudu on ufa læge, andlang hreawe on lytlan broc, ondlang þæs broces on
Andlang brokes, and of þam broce on þa stræte, andlang stret in hennuc.
