Edward king gret Harold erl & Touid minne schyrerefen & alle mine þeynes inne Sumersæten
And ich keþe eu þat Ælfred hauet yseld Gise bissop his land æt Hlytton sacleas and clane toforan me
Nu wil ich þat se bissop beo þas londes worthe into his bissopriche þe he under honde hauet and alch
And gyf þar sy anni þing out gedon þas þe þar into hyrð ich beode þat man hit lete in ongean comen
