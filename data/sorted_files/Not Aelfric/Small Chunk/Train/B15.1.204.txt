Henric king gret his scirereuan & his þeinas of Lundene & on Essaxan & on Hortfordscire & on
And ic kyþe eow þet ic habbe geunnan Maurice bisceope þæt bissceoperice on Lundone mid saca and
And on ælces oðres mannes lande ofer eall þæt bisceoperice on morðspecce & on unrichthæmed & on
God eow gehealde.
And herto is gewitnesse Robert bisceop of Lincolne.
And þæt wæs gehaton Westminynstre on Cristesmessa.
