De officio tertio hore..
On undern we sculon God herian forðam on underntiman Crist wæs ðurh þara Iudea dom to deaðe
And eft æfter his æriste on Pentecostenes dæg com se halga gast on underntiman ofer ða apostolas þær hi
Undern is dæges ðridde tid, þonne is eac rihtlic þæt we to þære þriddan tide þa halgan ðrynnesse
