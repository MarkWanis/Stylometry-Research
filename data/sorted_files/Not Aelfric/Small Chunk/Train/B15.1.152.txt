Edward king gret Willem biscop & Leofstan & Ealfsi porterefen & alle mine burchþeignes on Londone
& icc kiþe eou þat ic habbe geunnen Sancte Petre into Westmunstre at þar kirkhaleginge fulne fridom
& gif ani mann hennen forð wille þa halegen stowe mid land & mid loge godian icc ann alswa þæt hit si
& icc nelle geþafian þæt eni mann habbe ani onsting ofer his land ne ofer his men ne be strande ne be
God eow gehealde, Amen.
