In nomine domini.
Ic Byrhteh biscop mid Godes geðeahte & þæs arwyrðan hiredes on Wigernaceastre & on ealre þæra
& under me syðþan hæbbe he & wel bruce þreora manna dæg to rihtere geyrsumnysse into ðære halgan
Ðæs is to gewitnysse, se hired on Wigraceastre & on Glæaweceastre & on Eofeshom & on Prescoran.
