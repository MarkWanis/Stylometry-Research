Ðis synd Wihtrædes domas Cantwara cyninges.
Dam mildestan cyninge Cantwara Wihtræde rixigendum þe fiftan wintra his rices, þy niguðan gebanne,
Ðær wæs Birhtwald, Bretone heahbiscop, & se ær næmda cyning; eac þan Hrofesceastre bisceop se ilca
& cwæð ælc had ciricean ðære mægðe anmodlice mid þy hersuman folcy.
Þær ða eadigan fundon mid ealra gemedum ðas domas & Cantwara rihtum þeawum æcton, swa hit hyr
