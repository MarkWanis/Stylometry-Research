Ðis is þæra landes boc æt Wudutune þe eadgar cining gebocode æþelrice his þegne on ecen yrfe.
Ærest æt meolforda upp andlang streames oþ ramma ford of þam forda andlang ramma dæne to
Andlang glim adune on þone stream oþ hit cymþ to þam stream þæ scyt fram meolcforda up andlang þæs
And ymb þone wudu swa ælfsige & æþelricg wurð.
