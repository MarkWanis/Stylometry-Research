Þis forward was makid wid Ordric hordere, þat es þat aelc man in sancte Eadmundes Byri husfast on his
An sea þat sit on oderes land sea sceal gifen oan halpeni for þat he aalle scolden sceren þe halegenes
Fram þis sceal naefre no maen be scyr buton sancte Eademundes þewes & sea cnytes and þe preostes
God seonde heore frieond þe worden sancte Eadmund.
