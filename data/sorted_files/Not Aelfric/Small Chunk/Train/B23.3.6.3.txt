Gif he on monandæg oðþe on niht acenned bið, he bið acweald fram mannum, lewde swa clæroc sweþer
Gif he on tiwesdæg bið acenned oþðe on ða niht, se bið æwerd on his life, & bið man & ðwære.
Gif he bið on wodnesdeig oþðe on da niht acenned, he bið scarp & biter & swiðe wær on his wordum.
Gif he bið acenned on þurresdæg oþðe on þa niht, he bið swiðe gesibsum & wæleði, & wel geweaxeþ, &
