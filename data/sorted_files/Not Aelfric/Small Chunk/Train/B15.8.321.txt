Þis sind þa land gemæra to gaing.
Ærest of lillan læwes crundle middewærdan to loddere beorge þonon to grenan lince westewardan of þan
Þonon to holan dic eastwærde andlang þære dic twa furlang norþweard þonne east be heafdan twafurlang
Of þære dene to þan readan stane of þæn stane eft on linlan læwes crundele.
Þis sind þa langemæra to gosige.
Ærest on þa mærdic estwærde þ innan tealeburnan andlang tealeburnan þ innan eccen.
