Æt Witlea, Wulfgare.
Ic Osuuald þurh Godes giefe arcebisceop mid geþafunge & leafe Eadwardes Angulcyningæs & Ælfheres
Sit autem terra ista libera omni regi nisi ecclesiastici censi.
Sancta (10) Maria & Sanctus Michahel cum sancto Petro & allum Godes halgum gemiltsien þis
Gief hwa buton gewrihtum hit abrecan wille hæbbe him wið God gemæne on þam ytemestan dæge
Þis sindon þæs landes gemæra þe gebyriað into þære westmestan hide æt Witleage, feldlondes &
Of ðrym gemæran westrihte on þa dic.
Andlang dices on kyllan hrigc.
Of kyllan hrigce on sylweg.
Andlang weges on þa hæþihtan leage & swa on þæt fule sloh.
Of þæm slo ofer buttingc graf on eccles broc.
Andlang broces on doferic.
