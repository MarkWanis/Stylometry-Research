Se halga papa Gregorius asend hider on eard þisne eadigan biscop Paulinum, þe we todæg weorðiað,
And he swa dide unforhtlice swa lange, oð he gehwyrfde Eadwine, Norðhembra cyng, to cristendome,
And þærr wearð ærcebiscop on Euerwic.
And þær wunede þa hwile þe se cyng Eadwine leofode, and Norðhembram þæs cristendomes gyman
Þa gelamp hit þæt se cyng Eadwine wærð ofslegen, and se cristendom mid Norðhembrum acolade
And he geseah ða se halga ærcebiscop þæt he nare nota þær nytt beon ne mihte.
Gewende þa hider to Cent he and seo cwen, Eadwines laf cynges, and he wearð þa mid mycelre
Þa wæs þes stede biscopleas, and he ða Sanctus Paulinus be þæs cynges bene undorfeng þisne
