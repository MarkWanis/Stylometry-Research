Þis sy to miclangrafe on tenet on eastan middel.
Þonne þonan suþ andlang dices up on fileþa þonan on stræte on eala wihing pyt þonan andlang weges
Þonne þonan &lang dices on haman fleot west andlang ea on middel gewæd & þonan west on seolcing
Þis is þara VI sulunga boc æt Miclangrafe þe Eadmund cing gebocode Ælfstane his þegne on ece yrfe.
