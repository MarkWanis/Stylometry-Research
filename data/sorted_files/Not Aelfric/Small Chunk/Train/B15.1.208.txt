H thurh Gode gefu Aenglelandes kyning griete aelle biscopes and ealle mine eorles and mine scirgerevan
And ic kithe eow that ic habbe heom geunnon thet hi beon æle ther land wurde the hi hæfdon on
And ic nelle thet ænig man ænig thing her on tyo buton hi and hera wicneres the hi hit betaecan willath,
Forthan thingan the ic habbe Crist thas gerithae forgyvon mine swale to ecere alysadnesse ealswa
And ic nelle gethavian thet aenig man this to brece be minen fullan freondscipe.
God eow gehealde.
