Edward king gret wel Willem biscop & Leofwyne eorl & alle mine þeyngnes on Middelsexan frendlice.
& icc kyþe eou þat hit is min fulle unne þat Aylric hauet so gyfen þat land at Greneforde Crist ant Seinte
& icc ann heom þar to saca & socna, toll & theam, infangeneþef & alle þas rithe þe to me belunged.
God eow so helde.
