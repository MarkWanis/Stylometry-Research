Ðis sind þa land ge mæro to Gæinge.
Ærest of lillan hlæwe crundele middeweardan to loddera beorge þonne to grenan hlince westewardan of
Þonne to holan dic eastwearde andlang þære dic twa furlang norð weard ðonne east be heafdan twa
Of þære dene to þan readan stane.
Of þan stane eft on lillan hlæwe crundele.
