Eadward cyncg gret Ælfward biscop & Wulfgar minne portgerefa & ealle þa burhware on Lundene
& ic cyþe eow þæt ic wille þæt mine men on Ænglisc cnihte gilde beon heore sace & heore socne wurðe
& ic wille þæt heo beon swa godre lage wurðe swa heo wæron on Eadgares dæge cynges & on mines
& ic wille eac hit mid gode geeacnian.
& ic nelle geþafian þæt heom ænig man misbeode ac beon heo ealle gesunde.
& God eow ealle gehealde.
