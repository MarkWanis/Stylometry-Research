HER IS GYT RIHTLIC WARNUNG & SOÐLIC MYNGUNG ÐEODE TO ÐEARFE.
Leofan men, utan understandan ealswa us þearf is, þæt we heonanforð fadian symle ure wisan for Gode
Ac soð is þæt ic secge, gyme se þe wille: þæt mæg on þeode swyþast to steore þæt man þa onhisce
Ac þy hit is þe wyrse wide on earde þe man oft herede þæt man scolde hyrwan & to forð hyrwde þæt
Ac hwilum þa hit god wæs, eal he wearð to woroldscame se þe stod on mane & on misdæde ænige
And þa hyt wæs on þeode for Gode & for worolde wislic & weorðlic, þa man riht lufode & unriht
Ac nu þincð þe wærra & mycele þe snotera se ðe can mid leasungan wæwerdlice werian & mid unsoðe
Ac wa him þæs wærscipes & ealles þæs weorðscipes, butan he geswice.
La, riht is þæt we lufian þa þe God lufian & hetelice ascunian þa þe God græmian & nænne gemanan
& se ðe þæt nelle, ehte we his ealle mid woroldlicre steore, þe læs þe we habban ænigne gemanan nu
Ac utan don swa us þearf is, utan God gladian & Godes lage healdan & anræde weorðan to
