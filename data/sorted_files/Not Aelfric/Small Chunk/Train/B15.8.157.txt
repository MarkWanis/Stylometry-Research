Ðis is þara V hyda boc æt Polhamatunæ þæ edmund cining gæbocodæ æðeldriðæ on ece yrfæ.
Þis synt þa land ge mero to Polhæmatune.
Ærest of þrim beorgum to ruwan beorge þonon to deopan wege.
Þonon on yddeles hammas þonon on wiles yge ðonon be westan fecces wudu þær ða weges togeðre
Þonon andlang stret to bosan hangran þonon on fecces wudu easte weardne þonon on entan hlew þonon
Of terstan andlang weges on þistel leage of þistel leage to bean leage þonon on suð leage easte wearde
