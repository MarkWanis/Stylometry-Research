His territoriis. Hoc rus complectitur.
Aerest of blacan broce on taan.
Þonne of dune on taan of beaddig broc þonne upp on beadding broc on halgan welle.
Þanon of halgan wylle on curig ie mære upp on æt guldæs geate þonne to readan flodan.
Þonne &lang dic to alor broce.
Þonne upp on igen stream to orcerd cumbe.
Fram orcerdcumbe on mædwegas sylfe.
Fram mæd wegum to fearn leage on þa stigele.
An ge mær hagan to hrimwoldes beorge to þæm ealdan wege.
Of dune on wice hrycg.
Of wice rycge ofdune on þone eastenan stream andlang streames to buchanforda.
Ðonon west be wyrrttrume to egwulfes ge mære.
To ge menan hylle midde weardne.
Ðonne to wealacan dic to ludanalrum.
Þonne andlang streames to wisforda ufan werdu.
Þonne east to offan dic.
Þonne suð on þa for yrþe to þæm beorge of dune to þæm wylle.
Ðonne forð rihte to broce ofer wiðan mæd.
