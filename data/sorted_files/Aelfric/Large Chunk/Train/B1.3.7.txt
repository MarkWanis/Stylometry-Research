XUIII KALENDAS FEBRUARII NATALE SANCTI MAURI ABBATIS.
MAURUS WÆS GEHATEN SUM SWYÞE HALIG abbod, se wæs to lare befæst sona fram iugoðe þam
He þeah wel on lare and wæs swiðe gehyrsum þam halgan Benedicte on eallum his hæsum, and on
Hwilon ær we sædon on sumere oðre stowe hu se ylca Maurus, þurh godes mihte eode uppon yrnendum
On sumne sæl eode se halga Maurus ham to mynstre weard mid his gebroðrum.
And Benedictus wæs þa mid anum æwfæstum were, forþan þe his wif wæs mid wodnysse gedreht.
And Maurus ða gemette ær he to mynstre come ænne dumbne cnapan and se wæs creopere eac, ac se
Hi feollon ða butu mid flowendum tearum to Maures fotum and befengon his cneowa, halsigende þurh
Þa nolde Maurus ðam mannum þæs tiðian, cwæð þæt seo dæd nære him gedafenlic oðþæt þa gebroðra
Hwæt þa Maurus sona beseah to heofonum and cwæþ, þu ure hælend Crist, þe behete þinum
Æfter þisum wordum he cwæð to ðam wanhalan, On þære halgan ðrynnysse naman, beo þu hal, cnapa,
Þa aras se cnapa and up rihte eode, and mid bliðre stemne bletsode his drihten, þe hine gehælde þurh
Eft ða þa se halga Benedictus ham to mynstre com, and þæt wif wearð gewittig þe he fram wodnysse
Maurus wæs eac oftost mid þam eadigan Benedicte, and his wundra wiste and mid him worhte wundra.
Sume þæra we secgað her, sume we forsuwiað.
And he wæs fyrmest muneca to ðam mæran Benedicte, and þæs mynstres geweold swa swa him
On ðam timan asende sum gesælig biscop of Francena rice to ðam halgan fæder mænigfealde lac and
Hwæt ða Benedictus be his gebroðra ræde swa swa him God geswutolode asende þa Maurum, þeah ðe
He funde him eac geferan feower oðre munecas and sume læwede menn to ðam lande mid him, and
Eft on ærnemergen sende se arwurða Benedictus twægen his muneca to Maure mid lacum, þæt is mid
Ic sæcge ðe to soðan þæt þu scealt gewitan on ðam sixteoðan geare þæs ðe þu munuc wurde of þisseræ
Ic secge þe eac nu þæt eower sið ne bið na swa, swa swa we wendon, ac wyrð elles gefadod and on
Farað nu gesunde and gesælige becumað.
Maurus ða ferde mid mycelre blisse, and his geferan samod, to Francena rice, ac twegen his geferan
Eac hi gemetton on sumum mynstre be wege ænne blindne man se bæd his hæle georne æt ðam halgan
Eft he gehælde on oðre stowe anre wydewan sunu þe unwene ða læg, and gefrefrode þa modor, and
And se cnapa arn sona to Maure hrymende and cweðende, þu ahreddest mine sawle fram fyrenum
Maurus geseah eac swa swa him God geswutelode on þam ylcan færelde Benedictes forðsið, and hu
Hi ferdon þa forð to ðam foresædan bisceope, þe hi gelangian het, ac he ne leofode na þa, ac wæs for
Hi wurdon þa unrote, and eoden swa þeah to ðam niwan bisceope, bædon his rædes, cunnodon
He cwæð þæt he ne mihte embe munuclif þa smeagen be oðres bisceopes dihte, ac wolde beon embe
Hi feordon þa þanon fram þære scire bisceope, and God him foresceawode on sumere oðre scire on
Sum forðþegn wæs ða welig on þam lande, Florus gehaten, and se hæfde gemynt mynster to arærenne,
Se þegn wæs wunigende butan wifes neawiste, forðan þe his gebedda gefaren wæs of life, and læfde
Hwæt þa Florus ferde fægen him togeanes, and mid micelre arwurðnysse þa æðelan godes menn
Se Florus wæs ða fyrmest þæra Francena þegna, and ðam cyninge leofest, þe on þæra leode rixode,
Florus ða cydde þam cyninge his willan, and be his leafan arærde on his agenum lande mynster and
He offrode eacc þa on ðam ylcan dege his ancennedan sunu þam ælmihtigan Gode to munuclicere
And cwæð þæt he sylf wolde, gif hit God swa foresceawode eall woruldðing forlætan, and wunian on
He gegaderode þa swiðe gode wyrhtan gehwanon, and arærde þæt mynster eall be Maures ræde wið
Hwæt ða on sumum dæge sæton him ætgædere Florus and Maurus to middan þam wyrhtum, and
Þa feol sum preost færlice of þam weorce, swa þæt he samcucu læg, sweltendum gelic, and fleow eall
Ac Maurus hine het beran to Martines cyrcan, and Maurus inn eode, and bæd ðone ælmihtigan for his
He aras þa eftsona and eode to ðam preoste, and his wunda bletsode and cwæð him baldlice to, On
He aras þa gesund, swylce of slæpe awreht and began to wundrigenne hu he wurde ðider gebroht.
Þa cwæð se halga wer, ne wurde ðu hider geferod on þinum agenum fotum, ac ðe feredon oþre; ac
Ða eode se preost eft to his weorce, and Florus hine astrehte to Maures fotum and cwæð, þu eart
Æfter þysum ongunnon of ðam gegaderwyrhtum tælan ðone halgan þe gehælde þone preost, cwædon
Ac ða þa hi swiþost tældon þone soðfæstan Maurum, þa wurdon afyllede mid ðam fulan gaste þry
Maurus þa sona mycclum wearð astyred, and wepende eode into þæra cyrcan, and mid mycelre
He eode þa eft ut to þam earmum wodum, and on drihtnes naman þa deoflu afligde of ðam wodum
He het eac beran into his gebædhuse þæs forðfarenan lic þe se feond acwealde, and wacode ealle þa
Þa wearð geendod eall þæs mynstres weorc binnan eahte gearum, and eac gehalgod mid healicum
Hwæt ða Florus gemunde hwæt he gemynte æt fruman, and hwæt he Gode behet þa ða he began þæt
Se cyning ða geþafode þam þegne his willan, þeah þe he uneaðe mihte on his mode findan þæt he swa
Æfter ðysum ferde Florus to ðam mynstre.
And his kynehlaford com swa swa hi cwædon, and bæd þæt he moste beon heora broðor for Gode, and
Florus het ða forðbæran his fægeran maðmas on golde and on seolfre and hi Gode geoffrode, and his
And se cyning blyssode and blyðe wearð on mode, for ðæs þegnes gecyrrednysse and soðum geleafan.
Eft æfter gereorde, het se æðela cyning þæt Florus hine gespræce ær þan þe he þanon ferde, and he
Þa weop se cyning, and þancode Gode his godan wyllan, and tihte hine georne þæt he geornful wære
Eft þæs on mærgen rad Maurus to þam lande þe se cyning him geaf, and his cepte sum beddryda, se
Þa bletsode Maurus þone beddrydan mann, and he sona gesund sylf upp aras, swa ðæt hit næs gesene
Þa bugon gehwylce æðelborenne menn to Maures mynstre to munuclicere drohtnunge.
Sume eac befæstan heora suna him to Godes þeowdome, oðþæt þær gadorod wæs hund teontig
Þa gecwæð se abbod and ealle þa gebroðra þæt þer ne mihte na ma muneca wunian, þe læs þe ðam
Hit gelamp ða siððan þæt se gesæliga Florus gewat of worulde wuldorful to Criste, on ðam þrytteoðan
Sum ercediacon com eac hwilum to Maure, þa næfdon hi nan win buton on anum gewealdenan
And Maurus þa bletsode bliðelice þæt win, cwæð þæt God mihte gemycclian þone wætan, se ðe iu on
Hi druncon ða þæt win wel hundseofontig manna, and æfre wæs se buteruc brerdful wines.
Maurus gemette ænne man eft se wæs yfele getawod and hine æt se cancor, and his weleres wæron
Þa bletsode Maurus þone mann feorran, and he sona wearð wundorlice gehæled.
Siððan nolde Maurus of ðam mynstre faran for nanre neode, butan he nyde sceolde, forðan þe he wiste
He wearð þa gebysgod on his gebedum þearle on Martines cyrcan mid his twam munecum, to ðriddan
Þa on sumere nihte geseah he þone deofol, þa þa he wolde gan into Godes cyrcan.
Se feond hæfde him mid fela oðre sceoccan, and forwyrnde him inganges, and mid graman him cwæþ
Ac ðu scealt witan nu þæt ic mid mislicum deaðe þine munecas acwelle, swa þæt earfoðlice heora
Þa cwæð se halga wer to ðam hetolan sceoccan, ðreage ðe se hælend, þe hæfð ealles geweald, þu
Hwæt ða se sceocca sona fordwan of his gesihðe mid swiðlicum reame, swa þæt ða munecas micclum
Maurus ða eode into Martines cyrcan, and mid micelre sarnysse þone soðfæstan God bæd, þæt he him
Maurus ða geseah ænne scinende engel wið hine standende, and þas word him secgende, þu Godes
Witodlice se deofol wat towerde ðing hwilon, na symle, þurh sume gebicnunge be þam þe he oft
Æfter ðisum wordum gewat se engel him fram.
And Maurus þæs on mergen þa munecas gespræc, and ealle his gesihðe him openlice sæde, and
Æfter þisum wordum wurdon þa munecas mycclum abryrde and heora gebeda sungon, and hi sylfe
Him com ða se cwealm swa swa se engel gecwæð, swa þæt an hund muneca and syxtyne munecas
Þær belifon swa þeah lifes on ðam mynstre feower and twentig muneca æfter Maures forðsiðe, and he
Þær beoð wundra geworhte ðurh þone halgan wer ðam ælmihtigan to lofe, seðe lyfað a on ecnysse.
Þæs halgan Maures lif wæs þus gelogod; ða þa he twelf wintra wæs he wæs betæht Benedicte, and he
Twegen þæra muneca ðe mid him þyder comon forðferdon þær on þam foresædan cwealme, and
Sy wuldor and lof ðam welwillendan Gode, seðe wurðað his halgan mid wuldre on ecnysse.
