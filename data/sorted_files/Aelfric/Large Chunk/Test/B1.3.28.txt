X KAL OCTOBRIS, PASSIO SANCTI MAURICII ET SOCIORUM EIUS.
MAXIMIANUS HATTE SUM HÆÐEN CASERE SE ferde to Franclande mid mycelre fyrdinge wolde
Se casere wæs cene and reðe, and deofolgild beeode, dwollice libbende, and acwealde Godes men mid
Þa wæron on þære fyrde fela Cristene menn, and an synderlic eorod of easternum leodum, swiþe
An eorod is gecweden on ðam ealdan getele six ðusend manna and six hund and six and syxtig.
Swa fela manna wæron on þam foresædan eorode, swiþe gelyfede on þone lyfigendan God, þeah þe
On þam flocce wæron þa fyrmestan menn, Mauricius ærest, and Exuperius, Candidus and Uitalis, and
Hwæt þa Maximianus mid micclum þrymme ferde, oð þæt hi comon to muntum, and se manfulla
Þa gewende seo eorod þe we ær embe spræcon, forð on heora weg, and forflugon þæt deofolgild,
Þa ongeat se casere þæt þa Cristenan þær næron, and het ða mid graman his gegadan to faran, and
Hwæt ða ærendracan ða ardlice ridon, and budon þam Cristenum ðæs caseres geban, ac hi ealle
Þa ridon þa ærendracan raðe eft ongean and cyddon þam casere þæt þa Cristenan noldon his hæsum
Maximianus wearð þa mid micclum graman ontend, and het þa hæþenan faran and þa halgan ofslean,
And hi þa caflice ferdon to gefyllenne his beboda.
Hwæt þa Mauricius, se mæra godes ðegn, and Exuperius, mid eadmodnysse afyllede, tihton heora
Betwux þysum tihtingum tengdon þa hæþenan mid andþrecum wæpnum to þam æwfaestum heape, and
Eft ða se casere sende to þam cwellerum, and het þæt hi ne belæfdon of þam geleaffullum werode
Þæra sawla underfengon sona Godes englas manega of heofonum mid micelre blisse, swa swa þa
Þa dældon þa cwelleras þæra Cristes martyra wæpna and gewæda, forþan þe se wælhreowa het þæt
Æfter ðam gedale þa dyrstigan cwelleras gesæton him ætsomne mid swiðlicre blisse, and fengon to
Þa com þærto ridan sum Cristen man sona, harwencge and eald, se wæs gehaten Uictor.
Þa ongunnon þa cwelleras clypian þone ealdan to heora gereorde, ac he hraðe axode for hwilcum
Þa sæde heora sum þæt hi Cristene wæron, and se casere hete hi ealle ofslean oðrum to bysne.
Se ealde Uictor þa incundlice geomerode, and hlude clypode, Eala, come ic ær, þæt ic mihte geendian
Hwæt ða hæþenan þa hine bestodon, and heton hine secgan mid swyðlicum þreate hweþer he Cristen
He beseah þa to heofonum, and sæde mid geomerunge, Eall middaneard is mid miste befangen deopre
Æfter þysum wordum seo wedende meniu ofslogon þone Uictor þæt he feallende sweolt, and he swa
Hwæt wille we furðor secgan hu se unsæliga casere his fyrdinge geendode, þonne he forferde on
Ac uton þencan georne þonne we þyllic gehyrað, þæt we þe beteran beon, þurh þa boclican lare.
We sceolon swincan, and oferswyðan unþeawas mid godre drohtnunga Godes rice geearnian; þæt we
We sceolon geþencan hu geþyldige hi wæron þa þe for Cristes naman gecwylmede wæron; hi man
Nu synd we swa asolcene þæt we swincan nellað nan þincg, fornean ne urum lustum wiðcweþan wið
Geþyld is micel mægen, and mannum nydbehefe, swa swa ure Hælend cwæð to his halgum
Gif se geþyldiga man mid his þolmodnysse his sawle gehylt, swa swa us sæde Crist, þonne forlyst se
Gif we wislice libbað, swa swa us wissiað bec, þonne mage we becuman to Cristes halgum, æfter ure
Þa halgan þe we heriað, and heora gelican, forsawon þisne middaneard, þeah þe he myrge wære, þa ða
Þa wæs langsum lif, and liþe gewederu, hælo on lichaman, on langsumere sibbe, wæstmbærnys on
Efne nu þæs middaneard is for micclum geswenct, and mid manegum earfoðnyssum yfele geþreatod;
Hwilon þæs middaneard teah menn fram Gode, mid his fægernyssum, and nu he fylst us to Gode,
Uton forþy awendan urne willan to Gode, and to þam ecan life ure smeagunge nu, þæt we eft moton
Þam is wuldor and wurðmynt a to worulde; AMEN.
