Lawedum mannum is to witenne, þæt hi sceolon healdan heora clænnysse on halgum timan, and on ðam
Læsse pleoh bið þam cristenan men þæt he flæsces bruce on Lenctentiman, þonne he wifes bruce.
On Lenctene sind getealde ealles ðæs geares teoðungdagas, on ðam dagum sceolon cristene men heora
Nis ðæs mannes fæsten naht, þe hine sylfne on forhæfednysse dagum fordrencð.
Se Scyppend, þe eow gesceop, sylle eow godne willan, and eow gelæde to ðam ecan life.
