Mæsse creda.
Ic gelyfe on ænne God, Fæder ælmihtigne, Wyrcend heofenan and eorðan, and ealra gesewenlicra ðinga
He ðrowode eac swylce on rode ahangen for us, and he wæs bebyrged.
And he aras on ðam ðriddan dæge, swa swa gewritu seðað, and he astah to heofonum, and he sitt æt
And he eft cymð mid wuldre to demenne þam cucum and ðam deadum, and his rices ne bið nan ende.
And ic gelyfe on ðone Halgan Gast, ðone Liffæstendan God, se gæð of ðam Fæder and of ðam Suna,
Ic andette ða anan halgan and ða geleaffullan and ða apostolican gelaðunge, and an fulluht on
And ic andbidige æristes deadra manna, and þæs ecan lifes þære toweardan worulde.
Sy hit swa.
